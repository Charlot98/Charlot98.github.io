放射术语

Glossary of Radiologic Terms
放射术语对照表

A

abscess An accumulation of purulent material in an abnormal cavity surrounded by a wall and typically associated with inflammation. May be caused by infection or presence of foreign material.
脓肿 是指脓性物质在异常空腔内积聚，该空腔周围有壁，通常伴有炎症。脓肿可能由感染或异物引起。

absorbed x-ray An x-ray that loses all of its energy inside the patient and completely disappears. Where x-rays are absorbed in radiography, the resultant image is more white (less black).
被吸收的 X 射线 是指在患者体内失去所有能量并完全消失的 X 射线。在放射成像中，X 射线被吸收的部位成像更偏白（黑度更低）。

achondroplasia "Without cartilage formation"; a genetic disorder of bone growth that results in disproportionate dwarfism. Appendicular bones are short and thick, metaphyses are cupped and flared, and physes are irregular. Some dog breeds normally are classified as achondrodysplastic (e.g., Bulldog) (a term that often is used interchangeably with chondrodystrophic).
软骨发育不全 字面意为“无软骨形成”；一种骨生长的遗传性疾病，导致不成比例的侏儒症。四肢骨短而粗，干骺端呈杯状并外翻，骺板不规则。一些犬种天生被归类为软骨发育不全型（如斗牛犬），该术语常与软骨营养不良型互换使用。

air bronchogram A radiographic sign that occurs during alveolar filling due to retention of air in a bronchus. It appears as a tubular, sometimes branching, gas opacity structure (bronchus) surrounded by soft tissue opacity lung parenchyma. The degree of contrast between the gas-filled bronchus and fluid-filled alveoli depends on the amount of alveolar filling; more complete filling = greater contrast. Air bronchograms only occur with alveolar filling, but alveolar filling can occur without air bronchograms. In most cases of alveolar filling, the bronchus also fills with fluid. Sometimes gas in the bronchus is resorbed. Air bronchograms rarely are seen in collapsed lungs. When present, there usually is some degree of alveolar filling in the collapsed lung.
空气支气管征 是一种发生于肺泡充填时的放射学征象，由于支气管内仍保留空气所致。影像上表现为管状、可呈分支状的气体密度结构（支气管），其周围为软组织密度的肺实质。充气支气管与充液肺泡之间的对比度取决于肺泡充填程度；充填越完全，对比度越高。空气支气管征仅见于肺泡充填，但肺泡充填并不一定出现空气支气管征。多数肺泡充填病例中，支气管本身也会被液体充满；有时支气管内气体会被吸收。肺萎陷时较少见空气支气管征；若出现，通常提示萎陷肺内仍存在一定程度的肺泡充填。

air gap Distance between patient (subject or object) and image receptor; increasing the air gap allows scattered x-rays to dissipate but results in magnification of the image.
空气间隙 指患者（受检体或物体）与影像接收器之间的距离；增加空气间隙可使散射 X 射线衰减，但会导致影像放大。

ALARA (As Low As Reasonably Achievable) The overriding principle of radiation safety.
ALARA 原则 （As Low As Reasonably Achievable，合理可行的最低剂量）是辐射安全的基本总原则。

aliasing In digital radiographs, an artifact that appears as a series of repeating lines superimposed on the image.
混叠伪影 在数字放射成像中表现为叠加在影像上的一系列重复线条的伪影。

alveolar filling Replacement of the air in the lung air spaces due to an influx of fluid (e.g., blood, edema) or cells (e.g., neoplasia, purulent material). Any solid area of soft tissue opacity in the lung that is not vessel, nodule, or mass is alveolar filling. The air bronchogram sign is characteristic of alveolar filling but is not necessary for diagnosis of alveolar filling. Common causes include pneumonia, hemorrhage, neoplasia, edema.
肺泡充填 指肺泡气腔内的空气被液体（如血液、水肿液）或细胞（如肿瘤细胞、脓性物质）所取代。凡肺内出现非血管、非结节、非肿块的实性软组织密度区域，均可视为肺泡充填。空气支气管征是肺泡充填的典型表现，但并非诊断所必需。常见原因包括肺炎、出血、肿瘤和水肿。

alveolar pulmonary pattern A radiographic appearance that occurs due to alveolar filling or collapse. Appears as soft tissue opacity in the lung that obscures the margins of adjacent soft tissue structures (e.g., blood vessels, pulmonary nodules, diaphragm, cardiac silhouette).
肺泡型 由肺泡充填或肺萎陷引起的一种放射学表现，影像上表现为肺内软组织密度影，遮盖邻近软组织结构（如血管、肺结节、膈肌、心影）的边界。

amyloidosis A rare disorder of protein metabolism that results in deposition of insoluble protein (amyloid) in glomeruli and interstitium of kidneys (and also in other organs). May be heritable (e.g., Shar-Pei, Abyssinian) or acquired (sequela to chronic inflammation/infection). Associated syndromes include Shar-Pei fever, Akita fever/polyarthropathy, Gray Collie. Some cat breeds are predisposed (e.g., Abyssinian, Asian shorthair, Siamese). Clinical signs vary with severity of disease and organs affected; many animals die shortly after onset of signs.
淀粉样变性 一种罕见的蛋白质代谢障碍，导致不溶性蛋白（淀粉样蛋白）沉积于肾小球及肾间质（亦可累及其他器官）。可为遗传性（如沙皮犬、阿比西尼亚猫）或获得性（多继发于慢性炎症或感染）。相关综合征包括沙皮犬热、秋田犬热/多关节病、灰色柯利犬综合征。部分猫品种（如阿比西尼亚猫、亚洲短毛猫、暹罗猫）具有易感性。临床表现随病情严重程度及受累器官而异，许多动物在症状出现后不久即死亡。

angiocardiography A radiographic contrast study of the heart and great vessels.
心血管造影 对心脏及大血管进行的放射学造影检查。

angiography A radiographic contrast study of blood vessels.
血管造影 对血管系统进行的放射学造影检查。

Anode Positive terminal in an x-ray tube that attracts electrons from the cathode (negative terminal). Anode contains a target made of metal (tungsten) with a high melting point to withstand the intense heat generated by the electron beam bombardment.
阳极 X 射线管中的正极，吸引来自阴极（负极）的电子。阳极内含高熔点金属（通常为钨）制成的靶材，以承受电子束轰击所产生的高热。

anode heel effect See heel effect.
阳极效应 见 heel effect

apophysis Essentially, a non-weight-bearing epiphysis; a normal bony projection that serves for the attachment of tendons or ligaments (e.g., greater trochanter, tibial tuberosity); a secondary ossification center that contributes to the shape of a bone but does not contribute to overall bone length.
骨突 本质上为不承重的骺部；一种正常的骨性突起，用于肌腱或韧带附着（如大转子、胫骨粗隆）；属于次级骨化中心，影响骨形态但不参与骨长度的增长。

appendicular Pertaining to the limbs.
四肢骨骼的 指与四肢相关的。

arteriovenous fistula An abnormal communication between an artery and a vein.
动静脉瘘 动脉与静脉之间的异常交通。

arthritis Inflammation of a joint; may be caused by trauma, infection, or immune-mediated disease. Term often is erroneously used for degenerative joint disease.
关节炎 指关节的炎症，可由外伤、感染或免疫介导性疾病引起。该术语常被误用于指代退行性关节病。

arthrography A radiographic contrast study of a joint. Arthrogram.
关节造影 对关节进行的放射学造影检查，所得影像称为关节造影片。

artifact An erroneous finding. Artifacts may be caused by technical problems associated with film, screens, grid, development, exposure, cassettes, x-ray table, etc., or they may be patient-related issues such as debris on skin/ haircoat, superimposed cutaneous or subcutaneous structures. Artifacts can mimic disease, impair image quality, and obscure abnormalities.
伪影 指错误的影像表现。可由胶片、屏片、滤线栅、显影、曝光、暗盒、X 线检查床等技术问题引起，也可由患者因素造成，如皮肤或被毛附着物、皮肤或皮下结构重叠等。伪影可模拟疾病、降低图像质量并掩盖真实异常。

asynchronous growth Refers to the disproportionate rate of growth between paired bones (e.g., radius/ulna, tibia/fibula), typically caused by damage to a physis and commonly resulting in an angular limb deformity and joint subluxation.
生长不同步 指成对骨骼（如桡骨/尺骨、胫骨/腓骨）之间生长速度不一致，通常由骺板损伤引起，常导致肢体成角畸形及关节半脱位。

atelectasis Incomplete expansion of part or all of a lung. Also used to describe partial or complete lung collapse. Atelectasis may result from disease of lung or pleural space. Common with animals positioned in lateral recumbency.
肺不张 指肺的部分或全部扩张不全，也可用于描述部分或完全性肺萎陷。可由肺部或胸膜腔疾病引起，在侧卧位动物中较为常见。

atresia ani A rare malformation of the anus. Radiographs sometimes are used to assess the severity of the condition, which varies from a stenosis to a complete blockage. Typically, the colon is largely distended with feces. A fistula can develop between the rectum and either the vagina, urinary bladder, or urethra, enabling the patient to defecate through the vagina or urethra. Gas must be in the rectum to visualize the abnormality in survey radiographs. Sometimes elevating the caudal end of the patient will allow gas in the descending colon to move into the rectum. Evaluate the distance between the rectum and the anal membrane using positive contrast medium (either oral or per-rectal dosing) or a radiopaque probe inserted into the anus. When possible, add a negative-contrast enema (pneumocolon) to identify the rectum. Infusion of iodinated contrast medium into the urethra, vagina, or rectum may document a fistula or other malformation.
肛门闭锁 一种罕见的肛门畸形。放射检查有时用于评估其严重程度，范围可从狭窄至完全阻塞。通常结肠因粪便潴留而明显扩张。直肠与阴道、膀胱或尿道之间可形成瘘管，使患者经阴道或尿道排便。平片检查需直肠内有气体方可显示异常；有时抬高动物尾侧可使降结肠气体进入直肠。可通过口服或经直肠给予阳性对比剂，或将不透射探针插入肛门，评估直肠与肛膜之间的距离；条件允许时可联合负性对比灌肠（气结肠造影）以显示直肠。向尿道、阴道或直肠注入含碘对比剂可证实瘘管或其他畸形。

avascular necrosis of the femoral head A loss of blood supply to the developing femoral head that results in bone necrosis and eventual collapse. Condition may be idiopathic, genetic, or traumatic. The eponym is "Legg-Calvé-Perthes disease."
股骨头缺血性坏死 指发育中的股骨头血供丧失，导致骨坏死并最终塌陷。病因可为特发性、遗传性或外伤性，其别名为雷卡佩氏病（Legg-Calvé-Perthes病）。

axial Pertains to the median line of the body, a limb, or other structure. Axial skeletal structures include vertebral column, skull, hyoid bones, ribs, and sternum.
轴向的 指与身体、肢体或其他结构的中线相关。轴向骨骼结构包括脊柱、颅骨、舌骨、肋骨及胸骨。

B

barium sulfate A positive contrast medium with a high degree of opacity (atomic number of barium is 56). Barium is a white crystalline powder that is micro-pulverized and placed in suspension. It adheres well to suspending agents, enabling it to efficiently coat mucosal surfaces. Barium is not metabolized or absorbed. The word "barium" comes from the Greek word meaning "heavy."
硫酸钡 一种高密度的阳性对比剂（钡的原子序数为 56）。钡为白色结晶性粉末，经微粉化后制成悬浮液，能良好附着于悬浮剂，从而有效包覆黏膜表面。硫酸钡不被机体代谢或吸收。“barium”一词源自希腊语，意为“沉重”。

barium enema A radiographic contrast study of the colon using barium sulfate.
钡灌肠 使用硫酸钡对结肠进行的放射学造影检查。

beak sign Occurs when contrast medium fills only the entrance of the pyloric sphincter; commonly associated with pyloric stenosis.
喙征 指对比剂仅充填幽门括约肌入口处的影像表现，常与幽门狭窄相关。

beam direction Path of the x-rays as they enter and exit a structure (e.g., dorsoventral, dorsopalmar).
射线方向 指 X 射线进入并穿出结构时的路径（如背腹向、背掌向）。

beam hardening Refers to increasing the average energy of the x-ray beam by using filters to remove lower energy x-rays. Filters commonly are made of aluminum and positioned in the path of the primary x-ray beam near the x-ray tube window.
射线硬化 指通过使用滤过器去除低能 X 射线，从而提高 X 射线束的平均能量。滤过器通常由铝制成，放置于靠近 X 射线管窗口的主射线束路径中。

bezoar A concretion of fibrous material commonly found in the stomach or intestine.
胃石（毛球） 常见于胃或肠道内的纤维性物质凝结块。

binary number A Base 2 numbering system used by computers. The position of each digit (bit) in a binary number represents an exponent of 2. In a base 10 numbering system (decimal system), the position of each digit represents an exponent of 10. This means that moving right to left in a decimal number, the value of each digit is 10⁰, 10¹, 10², 10³, 10⁴, etc., whereas in a binary number the value of each digit (bit) is 2⁰, 2¹, 2², 2³, 2⁴, etc. Each decimal digit can be any numeral 0–9, but each binary digit can only be 0 or 1. In both numbering systems, adding more digits to the number makes the total value of the number grow exponentially, either by factors of 2 or 10.
二进制数 计算机使用的以 2 为基数的计数系统。二进制数中每一位（bit）的位置代表 2 的幂。在以 10 为基数的十进制系统中，每一位的位置代表 10 的幂。这意味着在十进制中从右向左，各位的数值分别为 10⁰、10¹、10²、10³、10⁴ 等，而在二进制中各位的数值为 2⁰、2¹、2²、2³、2⁴ 等。十进制每一位可取 0–9 的任意数值，而二进制每一位只能为 0 或 1。在两种系统中，位数的增加都会使数值呈指数级增长，分别以 2 或 10 为倍数。

bit The smallest unit of digital data. A bit is a digit in a binary number ("bit" is short for Binary Digit).
比特 数字数据的最小单位。比特是二进制数中的一位数字（“bit”是 Binary Digit 的缩写）。

bit depth The number of unique shades of gray available to display a radiograph. Not every gray shade will necessarily be used in the radiograph, but the shades can be displayed with that level of precision.
位深 指用于显示放射影像的不同灰阶数量。并非所有灰阶都会在影像中使用，但系统具备以该精度显示灰阶的能力。

block vertebra A congenital defect caused by failure of one or more vertebral bodies to segment normally during development. Vertebrae appear fused, but each vertebral body maintains normal length.
椎体融合 一种先天缺陷，由于一个或多个椎体在发育过程中分节失败所致。影像上椎体呈融合状态，但各椎体长度仍保持正常。

bone cyst A cavity within bone, often filled with fluid. Usually, well-defined margins and may be septated.
骨囊肿 骨内的空腔，常充满液体，通常边界清楚，可伴有分隔。

bone infarcts Foci of necrosis caused by decreased blood supply to intramedullary bone; appear as multiple small areas of increased medullary opacity.
骨梗死 由于骨髓内血供减少导致的局灶性坏死，影像上表现为多发的小片状髓腔密度增高区。

bone sclerosis An increase in opacity of bone; eburnation is a type of bone sclerosis.
骨硬化 指骨密度增高；象牙样硬化（eburnation）是骨硬化的一种类型。

bone spur A well-defined bony projection. Term is used to describe osteophytes (occur along non-weight-bearing articular margins) and enthesophytes (occur at sites of attachment to bone, e.g., ligaments, tendons, joint capsules).
骨刺 边界清楚的骨性突起。该术语用于描述骨赘（位于非负重关节缘）及附着点矿化（发生于韧带、肌腱、关节囊等骨附着处）。

border effacement Occurs when two structures of same opacity are in contact and their individual margins are no longer distinguishable where they touch. The margin of a structure is only visible when it is adjacent to a different opacity (i.e., opacity interface). Other names: edge effacement, silhouette sign/effect. Erroneously called "loss of detail."
边界消除效应 当两个具有相同密度的结构相互接触时，其接触部位的边缘无法区分。结构边界只有在邻近不同密度时才可见（即密度界面）。又称边缘消失、剪影征/效应，常被误称为“细节丢失”。

brachycephalic Refers to the shape of a skull in which the nasal cavity is considerably shorter than the cranium. Also, the cranium commonly is more dome shaped, frontal sinuses tend to be small or absent, and the occipital protuberance often is small. Examples: English Bulldog, Pug.
短头型 指鼻腔明显短于颅腔的颅骨形态，颅骨多呈圆顶状，额窦常较小或缺如，枕外隆突通常较小。例：英国斗牛犬、巴哥犬。

Bremsstrahlung x-ray An x-ray that is produced from the loss of kinetic energy that occurs when a speeding electron from the x-ray tube cathode nears the nucleus of a target atom in the anode, causing the electron to slow down and change direction; bremsstrahlung = "braking radiation."
轫致辐射 X 射线 当来自 X 射线管阴极的高速电子接近阳极靶原子核时，因减速和改变方向而损失动能所产生的 X 射线；bremsstrahlung 意为“制动辐射（braking radiation）”。

bronchial blood vessels Part of the systemic circulation that provides blood for lung nutrition. Bronchial arteries and veins are too small to be seen in radiographs. They contribute only about 1% of the total blood volume in the lungs (the pulmonary blood vessels supply the remaining 99%).
支气管血管 属于全身循环的一部分，为肺组织提供营养。支气管动脉和静脉过小，无法在放射影像中显示，仅占肺总血容量约 1%，其余 99%由肺血管系统提供。

bronchial cuffing Thickened soft tissue opacity around a bronchus due to accumulation of fluid, cells, or fibrosis; a sign of bronchial disease or peribronchial accumulation; also peribronchial cuffing, peribronchial infiltrates.
支气管套征 指支气管周围因液体、细胞或纤维组织积聚而形成的软组织密度增厚影，是支气管疾病或支气管周围积聚的征象；亦称支气管周围套征或支气管周围浸润。

bronchial pulmonary pattern A radiographic appearance in which bronchial walls appear thickened and/or increased in opacity (latter includes mineralization of bronchial walls). Commonly caused by proliferation or infiltration of collagenous and cartilaginous tissues and associated mucous glands. May also result from accumulation of peribronchial interstitial fluid or cellular material. The size and shape of the bronchial lumen may be altered.
支气管型 一种放射学表现，表现为支气管壁增厚和/或密度增高（包括支气管壁矿化）。常由胶原及软骨组织增生或浸润及相关黏液腺改变引起，也可因支气管周围间质液体或细胞物质积聚所致。支气管腔的大小和形态可发生改变。

bronchiectasis Abnormal and permanent dilation of bronchi or bronchioles with loss of normal branching and tapering.
支气管扩张 支气管或细支气管异常且永久性扩张，并伴有正常分支及渐细结构的消失。

bronchitis Inflammation of the bronchi.
支气管炎 指支气管的炎症。

bronchography A radiographic contrast study of the major airways.
支气管造影 对主要气道进行的放射学造影检查。

bronchopneumonia Inflammation of the lungs that usually begins in the terminal bronchioles.
支气管肺炎 通常起始于终末细支气管的肺部炎症。

brown fat Present in juvenile animals to help with thermoregulation. Brown fat is composed of smaller lipids and receives a larger blood supply than mature fat. It is higher in water content and therefore more opaque than adult fat.
棕色脂肪 存在于幼年动物体内，有助于调节体温。棕色脂肪由较小的脂滴组成，血供较成熟脂肪丰富，含水量更高，因此在影像中密度高于成年脂肪。

brush border The edge or margin resembles the bristles of a brush.
刷状缘 指边缘或轮廓呈刷毛样外观。

buccal On the side that faces the cheek; used to describe lesions associated with the mouth.
颊侧的 指面向面颊的一侧，用于描述口腔相关病变。

Bucky A device that holds a grid within the x-ray table. The Bucky moves the grid rapidly during exposure to blur the grid lines. Modern grid lines are very thin and only faintly visible; therefore, a Bucky may not be necessary with modern equipment. First introduced by Dr. Hollis Potter and often called a Potter-Bucky diaphragm.
Bucky装置 安装于 X 射线检查床内用于固定滤线栅的装置，在曝光过程中快速移动滤线栅以模糊栅线。现代滤线栅栅线极细，仅轻微可见，因此现代设备中未必需要巴基装置。该装置最早由 Hollis Potter 医生引入，亦称 Potter-Bucky 滤线栅。

Bucky factor See grid factor
Bucky因子 见 滤线栅因子

C

calcification The normal or abnormal deposition of calcium salts in soft tissues.
钙化 钙盐在软组织中的正常或异常沉积。

calcinosis Abnormal deposition of calcium salts in various tissues.

Detail has nothing to do with the patient and everything to do with how the radiograph was created. Detail should not be confused with definition (described above) or the opacity interfaces in the subject (patient). Detail is enhanced by using a small focal spot, tight collimation, larger distance between the focal spot and image receptor, and a shorter distance between subject and image receptor. Other factors that increase detail include using a grid to reduce scatter radiation and avoiding patient motion.

fracture A break or discontinuity in bone caused by a physical force that exceeds the structural capacity of the bone or by a disease that weakens the bone.

  • articular fracture: A break in a bone that extends to a joint surface.
    关节内骨折： 骨折线延伸至关节面。
  • avulsion fracture: A break in a bone that occurs at an attachment site (e.g., ligament, tendon) and results in a piece of bone being pulled away from the main bone.
    撕脱性骨折： 发生于韧带或肌腱等附着处，骨片被牵拉脱离主体骨。
  • chip fracture: A corner or edge of a bone is broken. 骨片骨折： 骨的角或边缘发生断裂。
  • closed fracture: The overlying skin and soft tissues are intact.
    闭合性骨折： 覆盖其上的皮肤及软组织完整。
  • comminuted fracture: Multiple breaks in a bone that communicate at a single point; results in three or more fragments.
    粉碎性骨折： 多条骨折线在一点相交，形成三块或以上骨片。
  • complete fracture: A break in a bone that extends through the entire bone.
    完全性骨折： 骨折线贯穿整个骨骼。
  • compression fracture: The ends of the bone fragments are impacted into each other, resulting in a shortened or collapsed bone.
    压缩性骨折： 骨折端相互嵌入，导致骨缩短或塌陷。
  • depression fracture: A fragment of a broken bone is located below the normal bone surface.
    凹陷性骨折： 骨折片位于正常骨表面以下。
  • fissure fracture: An incomplete break in a bone that extends from another fracture.
    裂隙骨折： 自其他骨折延伸出的不完全性骨折。
  • folding fracture (torus or buckling fracture): The cortex is broken on the concave side.
    折叠性骨折（屈曲骨折）： 皮质骨在凹侧发生断裂。
  • greenstick fracture (hairline fracture): An incomplete linear break on the convex side of a bone.
    青枝样骨折（毛发状骨折）： 骨凸侧发生的不完全线性骨折。
  • incomplete fracture: A partial break in a bone; the bone is not in complete discontinuity; appears in radiographs as a break in only one side of the cortex.

    开放性骨折： 覆盖皮肤及软组织被穿破。

    应力性骨折（疲劳性骨折）： 反复应力作用导致的骨折。
  • pathologic fracture: A break in a bone that has been weakened by underlying disease or a developmental defect.
    病理性骨折： 由于潜在疾病或发育缺陷削弱骨强度而发生的骨折。

  • slab fracture: The edge of a bone is broken and the fracture line extends from one articular surface to the other.
    板状骨折： 骨缘断裂，骨折线从一个关节面延伸至另一个关节面。

gastric dilatation and volvulus (GDV) A potentially life threatening, emergency situation that occurs when the stomach rotates around its axis (predominately its longitudinal or mesenteric axis). Rotation displaces the pylorus dorsally and to the left, which results in the duodenum wrapping around the esophagus. Gastric volvulus most often occurs in the clockwise direction (viewing the patient from caudal to cranial). Rotation greater than 180° twists the gastroesophageal junction to prevent eructation and obstructs the pyloric outflow. Gastric dilation can precede or follow gastric volvulus. Transient, intermittent volvulus can occur without dilation. Clinical signs of GDV include a distended and painful abdomen, non-productive retching, hypersalivation, dyspnea, and shock.

gastric ulcer Open sores or holes in the lining of stomach. Gastric ulcers vary from small, superficial erosions to deep, indurated depressions in the gastric mucosa. They often are difficult to see in radiographs. To be reliably diagnosed, the radiographic signs of an ulcer must persist in multiple images. When viewed from the side (x-ray beam is tangential to the ulcer), an ulcer typically appears as a barium-filled outpouching that extends away from the stomach lumen. Larger, more chronic ulcers often are surrounded by thickened mucosa, which may project into the stomach lumen. The edges of an ulcer may taper smoothly, appear irregular, or they may be shaped like a square, the latter similar in appearance to pseudoulcers in the duodenum. When viewed en face (the x-ray beam is perpendicular to the ulcer), an ulcer may resemble a bullseye target (⊙). This is because barium fills the central crater in the ulcer and the surrounding thickened wall creates a circular filling defect. A double-contrast gastrogram tends to be more sensitive than barium alone for detecting gastric ulcers. A barium-filled stomach can mask small mucosal lesions.
胃溃疡 指胃黏膜表面的开放性溃疡或穿孔，范围可从浅表糜烂到深部硬化性凹陷。胃溃疡在放射影像中常较难观察，为可靠诊断，溃疡征象需在多张影像中持续存在。侧位观察（射线与溃疡切线方向）时，溃疡常表现为充钡的囊样突出，向胃腔外延伸。较大或慢性溃疡常被增厚的黏膜包绕，并可向胃腔内突起。溃疡边缘可呈平滑渐细、不规则，或呈方形，后者与十二指肠假性溃疡相似。正面观察（射线垂直于溃疡）时，溃疡可呈靶征（⊙），因钡剂充填中央溃疡凹陷，而周围增厚的壁形成环形充盈缺损。双重对比胃造影较单纯钡剂更敏感；充满钡剂的胃腔可掩盖小的黏膜病变。

grid A device designed to block scatter x-rays from reaching the image receptor while allowing transmitted x-rays to pass through. The grid is positioned between the subject (patient) and the image receptor.

A grid factor may be 2, 3, or 4 meaning that the exposure must be increased 2, 3, or 4 times compared to the exposure used with no grid. This calls for more output from the x-ray tube and delivers more exposure to the patient. If the grid factor is 2, then the grid would block 1/2 of the primary rays. Also called "bucky factor" or "dose penalty."
滤线栅因子 指滤线栅吸收透射 X 射线的比例。因子可为 2、3 或 4，表示相较于不使用滤线栅时，曝光量需增加 2、3 或 4 倍，从而要求 X 射线管输出更高并使患者接受更多辐射。若滤线栅因子为 2，则约阻挡一半主射线；亦称“bucky因子”或“剂量惩罚”。

heel effect Refers to the variation in intensity of the x-ray beam between cathode and anode sides of the x-ray tube. Because the target is angled, the bottom or “heel” of the anode focal spot absorbs some of the x-rays. Absorption on the anode side results in more x-rays being emitted from the cathode side of the x-ray tube. Therefore, the thicker part of the subject should be positioned on the cathode side to take advantage of the heel effect. The heel effects occurs because x-rays originate not only from the surface of the anode target but also from deep inside. X-rays produced inside the target have to travel back out to join the x-ray beam, and some of these will be attenuated. Some of the x-rays that have to pass through the thicker heel part of the anode will be absorbed, which means fewer x-rays will be emitted from the anode side of the x-ray tube than from the cathode side. The difference between the two sides can be as much as 40%.
阳极效应 指X射线管阴极侧与阳极侧之间X射线束强度的差异。由于靶板倾斜，X射线束底部或"跟部"区域的曝光强度较弱。阳极焦点会吸收部分X射线。阳极侧的吸收导致X射线管阴极侧释放更多X射线。因此，被检物较厚的部位应置于阴极侧以利用跟部效应。该效应的产生源于X射线不仅来自阳极靶表面，也来自其深层内部。 靶材内部产生的X射线需折返汇入射束，其中部分会发生衰减。穿过阳极较厚跟部区域的X射线会被吸收，这意味着X射线管阳极侧的射线输出量将少于阴极侧，两侧差异可达40%。

hydrocephalus An excessive accumulation of fluid in the ventricular system of the brain. Obstructive or noncommunicating hydrocephalus is the more common form. It is caused by a ventricular obstruction, which can result from a congenital abnormality, trauma, infection, parasite migration, or neoplasia. Nonobstructive or communicating hydrocephalus is less common and is caused by failure of arachnoid villi to absorb cerebral spinal fluid at an adequate rate. Congenital hydrocephalus is more often reported in small, toy, and brachycephalic dogs (e.g., Yorkshire Terrier, Chihuahua, Maltese, Toy Poodle, Boston Terrier, English Bulldog, Pekingese, Lhasa Apso, Pug, Pomeranian, Cairn Terrier). In cats, hydrocephalus is less common than in dogs (may be hereditary in Siamese cats), and it may be associated with feline infectious peritonitis. Acute obstruction often is very painful, and the kidneys may appear normal in radiographs during early/mild obstruction. Chronic partial obstructions may be relatively asymptomatic until the animal is in renal failure. Progressive hydronephrosis leads to unilateral or bilateral renomegaly, usually with smooth margins. intervertebral disc disease (IVDD) Protrusion or extrusion of disc material into the spinal canal which compress the spinal cord or nerve roots and leads to neurological symptoms. IVDD can occur in any disc and most often results from degeneration of the disc. Dehydration of nucleus pulposus leads to replacement of gelatinous material with cartilage or fibrous tissue, resulting in loss of disc pliability. The nucleus may mineralize, resulting in further loss of compressibility. The annulus fibrosis also degenerates and weakens, allowing disc material to protrude or extrude into spinal canal. The dorsal part of the annulus is the thinnest. Disc material may be gelatinous, fibrous, or mineralized and can damage the spinal cord. • Hansen type I disc herniation Complete extrusion of nucleus pulposus into spinal canal; an acute condition that tends to be clinically more severe than type II and is more common in chondrodystrophic breeds. • Hansen type II disc herniation Protrusion (or bulging) of disc into spinal canal without rupture of annulus; often a more gradual and progressive disease process; more common in older non-chondrodystrophic breeds; also associated with cervical spondylopathy and lumbosacral instability.

Mach band An optical illusion in which a false black line or false white line occurs at the boundary between two different opacities. A negative Mach band (black line) occurs along the edge of a convex structure (e.g., outer margin of bone cortex). A positive Mach band (white line) occurs at the edge of a concave structure (e.g., acetabulum and under femoral head on a ventrodorsal pelvic radiograph), which may be mistaken for osteophytes (i.e., on the femoral neck). Mach bands cannot be eliminated by knowledge of their existence; the illusion occurs in the retina, and the brain actually sees it. Mach bands were described in 1865 by physicist Ernst Mach. Other names: Mach line, edge enhancement.
马赫带 一种光学错觉现象，表现为两种不同不透明度交界处出现虚假的黑线或白线。 负马赫带（黑线）出现在凸结构边缘（如骨皮质外缘）；正马赫带（白线）则见于凹结构边缘（如骨盆正位片中的髋臼及股骨头下方），可能被误认为骨赘（如股骨颈处）。 即使知晓其存在也无法消除马赫带；这种视觉错觉发生于视网膜，且大脑确实能感知到它。该现象由物理学家恩斯特·马赫于1865年首次描述。其他名称：马赫线、边缘增强效应。

The magnetic field temporarily alters the positions of protons in the patient. As these protons return to their normal positions, they emit radio waves that are detected by computers to create images with superior anatomic detail. The patient is not exposed to harmful radiation during an MRI procedure.

转移性钙化 指远离病灶部位的组织中钙盐沉积，通常由钙磷代谢异常引起。

微石症 指器官（通常为肺部）内直径小于3毫米的局灶性矿化区域。（别名：异位骨化、肺骨瘤）。

粟粒样结节 小于1-2毫米的微小结节，形似小米粒。别称：微结节。

mineralization The abnormal bioprecipitation of inorganic substances (such as calcium) in organic tissue; includes petrification and fossilization. Calcification is the normal or abnormal deposition of calcium salts. A type of normal calcification is ossification, which is the deposition of calcium to create new bone. Calcification occurs during ossification, but the reverse is not true. Heterotopic ossification produces extra-skeletal bone, which is bone in soft tissues where bone normally does not exist. Petrification and fossilization are additional types of mineralization.
矿化作用：有机组织中无机物质（如钙）的异常生物沉淀；包括石化和化石化。 钙化是指钙盐的正常或异常沉积。正常钙化的一种类型是骨化，即通过钙沉积形成新骨。钙化发生于骨化过程中，但反之则不然。异位骨化会产生非骨骼系统骨组织，即在正常无骨的软组织中形成骨骼。石化与化石化是矿化作用的其他类型。

mixed pulmonary pattern Two or more lung patterns are visible concurrently (i.e., bronchointerstitial, interstitial, and alveolar).

单关节性 仅累及单一关节（即所有病变位于同一关节）。

单骨性（monostotic） 指病变发生于同一块骨骼。

moth-eaten osteolysis Multiple areas of bone loss, smaller and less distinct than geographic osteolysis.
虫蚀样骨溶解 多发性骨缺损区域，较地理性骨溶解更小且界限模糊。

多糖贮积症 一组遗传性疾病，特征为黏多糖在体内异常堆积，可导致肌肉骨骼系统、眼部、神经系统及循环系统遭受永久性损伤。

myelocele Neural defect in which the spinal cord protrudes through a dorsal spinal defect.
脊髓膨出 脊髓通过背侧脊柱缺损突出体外的神经缺陷。

myelography A radiographic contrast study of the spinal cord involving injection of positive contrast medium into the spinal subarachnoid space. Myelogram.
脊髓造影术 通过向脊髓蛛网膜下腔注射阳性造影剂，对脊髓进行放射学造影检查。脊髓造影图。

myelosclerosis Increased opacity in the medullary cavity of bone, fibrosis of the bone marrow
骨髓硬化症 骨髓腔内密度增高，伴随骨髓纤维化。

myositis ossificans The deposition of inorganic materials in muscle, usually secondary to trauma.
骨化性肌炎 肌肉中沉积无机物质，通常继发于创伤。

mural Pertaining to or occurring in the wall of a body cavity.
壁性 指发生于体腔壁的。

negative contrast agent A contrast medium that absorbs very few x-rays and is less opaque than soft tissue or fat (i.e., a gas such as room air, carbon dioxide, nitrous oxide, or oxygen).
阴性造影剂 吸收极少X射线的造影剂，其不透明度低于软组织或脂肪（如室温空气、二氧化碳、一氧化二氮或氧气等气体）。

肾显影期 排泄性尿路造影中肾脏被阳性造影剂显影的阶段。

niche A recess in the wall of a hollow organ that retains contrast. Ulcers and diverticuli are examples.

结节性病变 直径不超过30毫米的球形病变。

nodular pulmonary pattern One or more small, rounded lesions in the lung (i.e., a structured interstitial pulmonary pattern).
结节性肺部病变模式 肺部出现一个或多个小而圆的病变（即具有结构化的间质性肺部病变模式）。

nonionic iodinated contrast media Positive contrast agents that are water-soluble but not converted into ions, similar in osmolality to plasma, and cause fewer adverse reactions compared with ionic contrast media.
非离子型碘造影剂 水溶性但不离子化的阳性造影剂，渗透压与血浆相似，与离子型造影剂相比不良反应较少。

nonunion fracture Fracture healing ceases before a complete union has occurred.
不愈合性骨折 骨折愈合过程在完全愈合前停止。

Norberg angle A numerical assessment of coxofemoral joint subluxation made on an extended ventrodorsal pelvic radiograph. Measurement is made by drawing a line connecting the centers of the two femoral heads and another line extending from the center of each femoral head to the cranial edge of the corresponding acetabular rim. Norberg angle greater than 105 is normal, between 90 and 105 is borderline, and less than 90 is abnormal.
Norberg角 通过伸展的腹背盆骨X光片对髋臼股骨关节半脱位进行数值评估。 测量方法：连接两股骨头中心点绘制一条线，再从每侧股骨头中心点延伸至对应髋臼盂边缘的头侧端绘制另一条线。诺伯格角大于105°为正常，90°至105°为临界值，小于90°为异常。

对象 接受X线检查的物体或部位（即患者或身体部位）。其他名称：受检者、患者。

object-film distance (OFD) The distance between the patient and the film or image receptor. In digital radiography, object-receptor distance.
物体-胶片距离（OFD） 患者与胶片或图像接收器之间的距离。在数字放射成像中，指物体-接收器距离。

物体-接收器距离（ORD） 患者与图像接收器之间的距离。

oblique radiograph Animal or body part is slightly rotated from true lateral or true ventrodorsal/dorsoventral positioning to displace overlying structures from area of interest or to view a structure in profile. Oblique radiography may also be accomplished by changing the angle of the x-ray beam.
斜位X线片 动物或身体部位相对于真实侧位或真实腹背/背腹定位略微旋转，以使覆盖结构移离感兴趣区域或观察侧面结构。斜位X线摄影也可通过改变X射线束角度实现。

OFA Orthopedic Foundation for Animals, established in 1966.

oligocephalic Shape of skull in which the nasal cavity is approximately equal in length to the cranium. Other names: mesaticephalic.
小头畸形 指颅骨形态中鼻腔长度近似等于颅骨长度的状态。别称：中头畸形。

opacity (radiopacity) Characteristic of a material to block (attenuate or absorb) x-rays. The more opaque a material, the more x-rays are blocked and the whiter the material appears in a radiograph. One of five basic opacities is assigned to a material; gas, fat, soft tissue, bone, or metal. The inherent opacity of a material is related to its density (atomic number and degree of compaction). Overall opacity of a material relates to its thickness.
不透明度（放射密度） 物质阻挡（衰减或吸收）X射线的特性。物质越不透明，阻挡的X射线越多，在X射线照片中呈现的颜色越白。 物质被归入五种基本不透射性之一：气体、脂肪、软组织、骨骼或金属。物质的固有不透射性与其密度（原子序数及致密程度）相关。物质的整体不透射性与其厚度相关。

opacity interface The visible difference in shades of gray between two adjacent structures which allows them to be differentiated in a radiograph. Without opacity interfaces, a radiography would be homogenous, with no visible structures (see relative opacity). Opacity interfaces are affected by body condition (e.g., emaciation, immature age) and disease processes (e.g., effusion) and should not be confused with detail (affected by technical factors) or definition (refers to margination of a lesion).
不透明度界面相邻结构间可见的灰度差异，使它们能在X射线片上被区分开来。 若无不透明度界面（例如腹部浆膜细节下降），放射影像将呈现均匀状态，无法辨识结构（参见相对不透明度）。不透明度界面受身体状况（如消瘦、未成熟年龄）及病理过程（如积液）影响，不应与细节（受技术因素影响）或界限清晰度（指病灶边缘形态）混淆。

opaque Not clear or transparent; impenetrable, not allowing light, radiation, sound, heat, etc. to pass through; not transmitting any of these.
不透明度 不清晰或不透明；不可穿透，不允许光线、辐射、声音、热量等通过；不传递任何上述物质。

organic iodinated compounds Positive contrast agents commonly used to study intravascular, intrathecal, intraarticular, and soft tissue structures. High atomic number and mass of iodine result in high opacity in these agents, which are available as ionic (hypertonic) and nonionic (isotonic) preparations.
有机碘化物常用于研究血管内、鞘内、关节内及软组织结构的阳性造影剂。碘的高原子序数与质量赋予这些制剂高显影度，可制备为离子型（高渗）与非离子型（等渗）制剂。

orthogonal radiographs Radiographs made at right angles (perpendicular or 90 degrees) to each other. Radiographs are two-dimensional images of three-dimensional subjects and orthogonal projections are required for meaningful evaluation.
正交射线照片相互垂直（90度）拍摄的射线照片。射线照片是三维物体的二维图像，需通过正交投影才能进行有效评估。

ossification The process of creating new bone by osteoblasts, usually in a cartilaginous or fibrous tissue matrix. Calcification occurs during ossification, but the reverse is not true.
骨化 成骨细胞在软骨或纤维组织基质中生成新骨的过程。骨化过程中会发生钙化，但反之不成立。

ossification center A site of bone formation; primary centers of ossification develop in the diaphyses, secondary centers develop in the epiphyses and apophyses, and accessory centers form sesamoid bones.
骨化中心 骨形成部位；原发性骨化中心位于骨干，继发性骨化中心位于骨骺与骨突，副骨化中心则形成籽骨。

osteoblasts Cells that deposit bony matrix and are responsible for ossification.
成骨细胞：沉积骨基质并负责骨化的细胞。

osteoclasts Cells that dissolve bone mineral and matrix; an osteoclast can resorb eight times amount of bone produced by an osteoblast.
破骨细胞 溶解骨矿物质与基质的细胞；单个破骨细胞可吸收相当于成骨细胞生成量八倍的骨组织。

osteocytes Cells in bone that help maintain calcium homeostasis and mechanical properties of bone.
骨细胞 存在于骨组织中，协助维持钙稳态及骨骼力学特性的细胞。

osteochondritis dissecans (OCD) A developmental condition in which abnormal blood supply to epiphyseal bone disturbs endochondral ossification, resulting in thickening, softening, necrosis, and eventual collapse of cartilage.
分离性骨软骨病（OCD） 一种发育性疾病，表现为骨骺骨异常血供干扰软骨内成骨过程，导致软骨增厚、软化、坏死，并最终塌陷。

osteochondroma A benign bony mass caused by abnormal location of physeal cartilage. Mass usually stops growing at skeletal maturity, but malignant transformation to chondrosarcoma or osteosarcoma can occur. Appears as an expansile, inhomogeneous, osseous mass extending away from bone, typically with irregular borders and welldefined margins. Multiple osteochondroma is called osteochondromatosis or multiple cartilaginous exostoses.
骨软骨瘤 由生长板软骨位置异常形成的良性骨性肿块。通常在骨骼成熟后停止生长，但可能恶变为软骨肉瘤或骨肉瘤。表现为向外扩张的不均匀骨性肿块，典型特征为边缘不规则且界限清晰。多发性骨软骨瘤称为骨软骨瘤病或多发性软骨外生骨疣。

osteogenesis imperfecta Rare in dogs and cats, it is a generalized condition caused by a defect in collagen production and resulting in fragile bones. May be confused with hyperparathyroidism.
成骨不全症 犬猫中罕见，由胶原蛋白合成缺陷引发的全身性疾病，导致骨骼脆性增加。可能与甲状旁腺功能亢进症混淆。

osteolysis An abnormal area of active bone resorption caused by disease. The pattern of osteolysis reflects the aggressiveness of the disease process. The more aggressive the disease process, the less distinct the bony margins, the longer the zone of transition between normal and abnormal bone, and the more rapid the rate of change in serial radiographs. Patterns of osteolysis include geographic, motheaten, and permeative.
骨溶解 由疾病引发的异常活跃骨吸收区域。骨溶解形态反映疾病进程的侵袭性：病程越凶险，骨骼边缘越模糊，正常骨与病变骨的过渡区越长，连续X光片中的变化速率越快。骨溶解形态包括地图样、虫蚀样及浸润样。

osteoma A rare, benign, slow-growing, osseous neoplasm that protrudes from the surface of a bone, usually on the skull.
骨瘤 一种罕见的良性、生长缓慢的骨质新生物，从骨表面突出，通常发生于颅骨。

osteomalacia Softening of bones due to faulty mineralization of available osteoid; may result from nutritional deficiency, chronic renal or intestinal disease. The ratio of mineral to osteoid is low (bone is "bad quality, good quantity").
骨软化症 因成骨组织矿化缺陷导致的骨质软化；可能由营养缺乏、慢性肾脏或肠道疾病引起。矿化与成骨组织比例偏低（骨质呈现"质劣量多"状态）。

osteomyelitis Inflammation of bone; usually caused by an acute or chronic infection (e.g., bacterial, fungal, protozoal organisms). Technically, bone and medullary cavity.
骨髓炎 骨组织炎症，通常由急性或慢性感染（如细菌、真菌、原生动物）引发。严格定义涵盖骨体及骨髓腔。

osteopenia "Too little bone"; there is a decrease in bone mass due to osteolysis, osteoporosis, or osteomalacia.
骨质减少症（osteopenia）"骨量不足"；因骨溶解、骨质疏松或骨软化症导致骨量减少。

osteopetrosis Uncommon congenital disease leading to a generalized increase in bone thickness and opacity. Other names: marble bone.
骨硬化症 罕见先天性疾病，导致全身骨骼增厚与密度增高。别称：大理石骨病。

osteophyte A well-defined bony growth or bone spur projecting outward from a periarticular margin. Results from degeneration and mineralization of proliferating articular cartilage in non-weight-bearing areas. The common true sign of degenerative joint disease.
骨赘 指从关节周围边缘向外突出的界限清晰的骨质增生或骨刺。由非负重区域增生性关节软骨的退化与矿化形成，是退行性关节病最常见的真实征象。

osteoporosis "Porous bones"; loss of bone mass leading to weak and brittle bones with decreased opacity; may result from disuse or metabolic disease. The ratio of mineral to osteoid is normal, but both are diminished (bone is "good quality, bad quantity"). Other names: bone atrophy.
骨质疏松症 "多孔性骨骼"；骨质流失导致骨骼脆弱易碎且密度降低；可能由长期不活动或代谢性疾病引起。矿物质与骨基质比例正常，但两者含量均减少（骨骼呈现"质量良好，数量不足"状态）。别名：骨萎缩。

osteosclerosis (sclerosis) Increased bone opacity; an area of bone with elevated density (more mineral is present in the bone). Radiographically more opaque.
骨硬化症（硬化） 骨密度增高；骨组织密度升高区域（骨内矿物质含量增加）。X线影像中呈更强显影度。

These calculi would not be visible in a bladder filled with urine, yet they are labeled "radiolucent." The same calculi would be labeled "radiopaque" in a negative contrast cystogram. They would again be labeled "radiolu- cent" in a double contrast cystogram. It is much more useful to simply describe the calculi as "soft tissue opacity."
透射性/不透射性 传统上，这些术语用于标记阻挡少量或大量X射线的物质，或指X光片中比预期更亮或更暗的区域。这些主观术语传递的信息不如直接描述相对不透明度有用。例如，软组织不透明的囊性结石通常会被标记为"透射性"。 在充满尿液的膀胱中，此类结石不可见，却仍被标记为"透射性"。而在负性造影膀胱片中，相同结石会被标记为"不透射性"。若采用双重造影膀胱片检查，它们又将被标记为"透射性"。相比之下，直接描述为"软组织密度"显然更为实用。

relative opacity Refers to the differences in x-ray absorption by various materials. Every material absorbs x-rays in a characteristic manner, depending on the physical composition of that material. It is the variation in x-ray absorption that allows various materials to be differentiated on radiographs. Relative opacity of a material is described as it compares to (i) one of the five basic opacities (gas, fat, fluid, mineral, or metal), (ii) the opacity of adjacent structures (i.e., relatively increased or decreased in opacity), or (iii) the opacity of a known normal structure. Overall opacity of a material is also affected by its thickness.
相对不透明度指不同材料对X射线的吸收差异。每种材料吸收X射线的方式各具特征，取决于其物理组成。正是X射线吸收量的差异使得不同材料能在X射线照片上被区分开来。 材料的相对致密度可通过以下三种方式描述：(i) 与五种基本致密度（气体、脂肪、液体、矿物质或金属）的比较；(ii) 与邻近结构致密度的对比（即相对增高或降低）；(iii) 与已知正常结构致密度的对照。材料的整体致密度亦受其厚度影响。

Radiography Rules of Two

  • Two views orthogonal radiographs are needed for complete interpretation.
  • Two opacities a structure is visible only if it is adjacent to a structure with a different opacity.
  • Two occurrences radiographic signs usually are visible in more than one view; if not, rule out artifact.
  • Two lesions after finding an abnormality do not stop searching, always look for a second.
  • Two joints include joints above and below a long bone.
  • Two tissues if bone appears abnormal, examine the adjacent soft tissues; if soft tissue looks abnormal, examine the nearby bones.
  • Two sides compare the opposite side or limb with sus- pected abnormality to opposite side or limb.
  • Two examples compare radiographic findings with known normal radiographs.
  • Two occasions compare current radiographs with previous radiographs (sometimes the old radiograph is the most important one).
  • Two visits make follow-up radiographs to assess progress and response to therapy.
  • Two reads evaluate radiographs again in light of patient history and results of laboratory testing.

X射线摄影双重规则

  • 需拍摄两个正交视角的X光片才能进行完整解读。
  • 双密度法则：结构物仅在与密度不同的邻近结构共存时方可显影。
  • 放射学征象通常在多个视角可见；若未见，则需排除伪影。
  • 发现异常后，两个病灶的检索不会停止，务必寻找第二个病灶。
  • 两个关节指长骨上下的相邻关节。
  • 若骨骼异常，需检查相邻软组织；若软组织异常，需检查邻近骨骼。
  • 两侧对比：将疑似异常侧的肢体与对侧肢体进行对照。
  • 双例对照：将放射影像发现与已知正常影像进行比对。
  • 两次检查：将当前X光片与既往影像对比（旧片有时最具诊断价值）。
  • 两次就诊：拍摄随访影像以评估治疗进展及疗效。
  • 复查两次：结合病史及实验室检查结果重新评估影像。

target Anode (positive) part of x-ray tube and site of x-ray production; composed of metal alloy with high melting point which is able to withstand the high temperatures generated by interaction with electron beam.
靶材X射线管的阳极（正极）部件，即X射线产生部位；由高熔点金属合金构成，能承受电子束作用产生的高温。

teat sign Describes the appearance of the pyloric antrum during a barium contrast study in which a relatively sharp, pointed, out-pouching of the pyloric antrum develops along the lesser curvature as a peristaltic wave pushes the contrast medium up against a mass-like or stenotic lesion around the pylorus.
乳头征：指钡剂造影检查中幽门窦的形态表现。当蠕动波将造影剂推向幽门周围的肿块样或狭窄病变时，幽门窦沿小弯侧形成相对锐利、尖端突出的囊状膨出。

technique chart A table of reliable x-ray exposures based on body part and thickness. Chart may be kVp variable or mAs variable. If a technique chart is not available, here are some quick tips to estimate mAs and kVp:
技术图表：根据身体部位和厚度列出的可靠X射线曝光参数表。该图表可为kVp可变型或mAs可变型。若无技术图表可用，可参考以下快速估算mAs和kVp的方法：

  • Estimate mAs: multiply the patient's body weight in pounds by 0.1 and add 1 (mAs = (body wt.× 0.1)+ 1). For example: to make an abdominal radiograph of a 22 pound dog, the mAs should be 3.2; (22 × 0.1)+ 1 = 3.2. For thorax radiographs use 1/2 the mAs and for orthopedic images double it.
  • Estimate mAs: another method is to estimate mAs based on patient body weight. For patients less than 10 kg, use 2.5-5 mAs; patients that weigh 10-25 kg, use 5-10 mAs, and for patients over 25 kg, sue 10-15 mAs.
  • Estimate kVp: multiply the body part thickness in centimeters by 2 and add the SID in inches (kVp = (thickness × 2)+ SID). For example: to make an abdominal radiograph of a body part that measures 14 cm thick and using a 35 inch SID, the kVp should be 63; (14 × 2)+ 35 = 63.
  • 估算mAs值：将患者体重（磅）乘以0.1后加1（mAs = (体重 × 0.1) + 1）。 例如：为体重22磅的犬拍摄腹部X光片时，mAs值应为3.2；(22 × 0.1) + 1 = 3.2。胸部X光片使用1/2的mAs值，骨科影像则需加倍。
  • 估算mAs值：另一种方法是根据患者体重估算mAs值。体重低于10千克的患者使用2.5-5 mAs；体重10-25公斤的患者使用5-10 mAs；超过25公斤的患者使用10-15 mAs。
  • 估计kVp值：将身体部位厚度（单位：厘米）乘以2，再加入射距离（单位：英寸）（kVp = (厚度 × 2) + 入射距离）。 例如：对厚度为14厘米的身体部位进行腹部X光检查，使用 35英寸 SID， kVp 应 63; (14 × 2) + 35 = 63。

telangiectasia A congenital vascular malformation that leads to dilation of ducts; can affect kidneys and other organs and may lead to thromboembolic disease, hydronephrosis; has been reported in Welsh Terriers and other dog breeds.
毛细血管扩张症 指先天性血管畸形导致导管扩张；可累及肾脏及其他器官，可能引发血栓栓塞性疾病、水肾；已见于威尔士梗犬及其他犬种。

tenting Describes the appearance of an extremely caudally displaced diaphragm in which the diaphragmatic attachments become visible as cranially directed, pointed projections on a ventrodorsal or dorsoventral radiograph. May result from tension pneumothorax or severe overinflation of lungs.
帐篷征 描述极度向尾侧移位的膈肌外观，在腹背或背腹X光片上可见膈肌附着点形成头侧向尖突。可能由张力性气胸或肺过度充气引起。

thoracic inlet The cranial opening into the thoracic cavity, bounded bilaterally by the first pair of ribs, dor- sally by the first thoracic vertebra, and ventrally by the manubrium.
胸腔入口 胸腔的颅侧开口，两侧由第一对肋骨界定，背侧由第一胸椎界定，腹侧由胸骨柄界定。

tissue density Refers to the physical property of a body structure to attenuate x-rays based on the mass or specific gravity of that structure. Also called subject density and in modern radiography opacity. Bone and teeth are most dense; gas is least dense. Increased tissue density is increased opacity and leads to increased x-ray attenuation and whiter areas in a radiograph. Radiographic density is the blacker areas in a radiograph.
组织密度 指人体结构基于其质量或比重衰减X射线的物理特性。亦称被检者密度和

tracheal ratio Technique used to objectively measure the size of the tracheal lumen by comparing the width of the trachea to the width of the thoracic inlet. Measurements are made on a lateral view at the level of a line connecting the first sternebra and first thoracic vertebra.
气管比值 通过比较气管宽度与胸廓入口宽度，客观测量气管腔大小的技术。测量在侧位片上进行，位置为连接第一胸骨节与第一胸椎的连线水平。

tracheal stripe sign Occurs when the esophagus contains gas and the ventral esophageal wall blends with the dorsal tracheal wall to mimic false thickening of the tracheal wall (sign indicates presence of gas in the esophagus). Other name; tracheoesophageal stripe sign.
气管条纹征 当食管内含气体时，食管腹侧壁与气管背侧壁融合，形成类似气管壁假性增厚的影像（该征象提示食管内存在气体）。别名：气管食管条纹征。

tracheography A radiographic contrast study of the lumen of the trachea.
气管造影术 对气管管腔进行放射性造影检查。

tram lines Parallel soft tissue or mineral opacity lines representing abnormal bronchial walls (walls are thickened and/or increased in opacity and do not taper normally). Characteristic of a bronchial pulmonary pattern, but not as reliable for identifying thickened bronchi as donut signs (paired pulmonary vessels are often mistaken for thick- ened bronchial walls and confused with tram lines). Other names: railroad tracks.
双轨征 平行软组织或矿化密度线，代表异常支气管壁（壁增厚和/或密度增高且未正常变细）。为支气管肺野征的特征表现，但识别增厚支气管的可靠性不及甜甜圈征（成对肺血管常被误认为增厚支气管壁并与轨道征混淆）。别名：铁轨征。

transitional vertebra A developmental abnormality in which a vertebra has anatomic characteristics of two adjacent vertebral regions. (e.g., sacralization of L7, lumbariza- tion of S1).
移行椎：一种发育异常，指椎骨同时具有相邻两个椎体区域的解剖特征（如L7椎骨骶化、S1椎骨腰化）。

transmitted x-rays X-rays that pass through a patient unchanged. In an average abdominal study, only 1% of the primary x-rays (those emitted by the x-ray tube) that reach the patient are transmitted through the body. The rest are either scattered or absorbed, mostly scattered. Of the 1% transmitted x-rays, only 0.5% actually interact with the image receptor to form an image.
透射X射线 指未发生改变即穿透患者身体的X射线。在常规腹部检查中，抵达患者的初级X射线（由X射线管发射）中仅有1%能穿透身体。其余射线或发生散射或被吸收，其中散射现象占绝大多数。在这1%的透射X射线中，仅有0.5%能与成像接收器发生实际交互作用形成图像。

Triadan system A numbering system that provides a con- sistent method of identifying teeth across all animal species.
三联体系统 一种为所有动物物种牙齿提供统一识别方法的编号系统。

trichobezoar An accumulation or concretion of hair in the alimentary tract (usually stomach or intestine).
毛发结石 指毛发在消化道（通常为胃或肠道）内积聚形成的结石。

trichophytobezoar A concretion of hair and plant mate- rials in the alimentary tract (usually stomach or intestine).
毛草石 指毛发与植物材料在消化道（通常为胃或肠道）内形成的结石。

tungsten An element that is advantageous in the production of x-rays. Its high atomic number (Z = 74) gives more efficient Bremsstrahlung production compared to lower atomic number materials. Tungsten has a high melting point (3422°) and a low rate of evaporation, which makes it capable of high thermionic emission. Tungsten is highly malleable, so it can be formed into thin wire for the cathode filament. 钨一种在X射线生产中具有优势的元素。其较高的原子序数（Z=74）使其相比原子序数较低的材料能更高效地产生刹车辐射。钨具有高熔点（3422°）和低蒸发速率，使其能产生强烈的热电子发射。钨具有极佳的延展性，可制成细丝作为阴极灯丝。

typhlitis Inflammation of the cecum.
盲肠炎：盲肠的炎症。

U

ultrasonography An imaging procedure that uses highfrequency sound waves to noninvasively examine soft tissue architecture and function with real-time dynamic imaging. No ionizing radiation or other known safety hazard is encountered with diagnostic ultrasonography. Value of ultrasonography is highly user dependent, relying on the sonographer's patience, skill, and experience.
超声检查 通过高频声波实现软组织结构与功能的实时动态成像检查，属于无创性影像技术。诊断性超声检查不涉及电离辐射或其他已知安全隐患。其诊断价值高度依赖操作者--超声医师的耐心、技术水平及临床经验。

ununited anconeal process Failure of the ossification center to fuse with the proximal ulna before 22 weeks of age (24 weeks in German shepherd dog).
肘突不闭合 指骨化中心在22周龄前（德国牧羊犬为24周龄）未能与尺骨近端融合。

upper gastrointestinal study A radiographic contrast study of the stomach and small intestine.
上消化道造影检查 胃和小肠的放射性造影检查。

urachus A fetal embryologic canal or tube allowing communication between the fetal urinary bladder and the umbilical cord placenta.
脐尿管 胎儿胚胎发育通道，连接胎儿膀胱与脐带胎盘。

ureterocele A congenital abnormality resulting in an abnormal, focal, cyst-like dilation of a terminal ureter.
输尿管囊肿 导致输尿管末端异常局灶性囊样扩张的先天性畸形。

urethrography A radiographic contrast study of the urethra. Urethrogram.
尿道造影术 对尿道进行的放射性造影检查。尿道造影。

urography A radiographic contrast study of the kidneys and ureters. Other names: excretory urography, intravenous urography (old term: intravenous pyelogram or IVP).
泌尿系统造影 肾脏与输尿管的放射性造影检查。别称：排泄性泌尿造影、静脉泌尿造影（旧称：静脉肾盂造影或IVP）。

V

vacuum phenomenon In a joint, a collection of gas that results when traction is applied creating negative intraarticular pressure, which allows nitrogen in the synovial fluid to escape and leak into the joint.
真空现象 关节内牵引产生负的关节腔内压，使滑液中的氮气逸出并渗入关节腔，形成气体积聚。

vaginography A radiographic contrast study of the vagina, cervix, and urethra.
阴道造影术 对阴道、宫颈及尿道的放射性造影检查。

vascular pulmonary pattern Pulmonary arteries or/ and veins are larger or smaller than normal.
血管性肺模式 肺动脉和/或肺静脉较正常尺寸增粗或缩小。

ventral Toward the belly or sternum. Contrast dorsal.
腹侧 指朝向腹部或胸骨的方向。反义词背侧。

ventral recumbency Animal is positioned lying on its sternum.
腹位（ventral recumbency） 动物呈俯卧于胸骨的姿势。

ventrodorsal (VD) radiograph The x-ray beam enters the ventral surface of a body part and exits the dorsal surface.
腹背位（VD）X光片 X射线束从身体部位的腹侧面进入，从背侧面射出。

vertebral heart size (VHS) An objective measurement of the size of the cardiac silhouette made on a lateral view thoracic radiograph using the lengths of thoracic vertebral bodies as units of measure. Technique is based on a study performed by Buchanan and Bucheler in 1995. Also called vertebral heart score, vertebral heart sum.
椎体心脏比值（VHS） 基于侧位胸片心脏轮廓的客观测量值，以胸椎体长度为计量单位。该技术源自1995年 Buchanan 和 Bucheler的一项研究。该术语亦称椎体心脏评分或椎体心脏总和。

vertical beam The x-ray beam is directed vertically.
垂直束 X射线束垂直投射。

visual acuity The ability to resolve a spatial pattern in an image.
视敏度 区分图像中空间图案的能力。

vomit Forceful expulsion of gastric or intestinal content through the mouth due to reflex contractions of stomach and abdominal muscles and preceded by nausea; usually caused by a problem caudal to the diaphragm (differentiate from regurgitation).
呕吐 因胃部及腹部肌肉反射性收缩，伴随恶心感，导致胃肠内容物经口腔强力排出；通常由膈肌下方病变引起（需与反流区分）。

W

well-defined A distinct margin. A simple test to determine whether a margin is well defined or not is to attempt to accurately draw a line around the lesion or border. If you can easily trace the edges, it is well-defined. "Well-defined" often is incorrectly described as "smooth"; but smooth is a shape, whereas well-defined is a description of the margin.
边界清晰 边缘分明。判断病灶边缘是否清晰的简易方法是尝试精确描绘病灶轮廓线。若能轻松勾勒边缘则属界限清晰。"界限清晰"常被误解为"光滑"，但光滑指形态特征，而界限清晰描述的是边缘形态。

Wolff's Law "Bone is deposited and removed according to the stresses placed on the frame."
沃尔夫定律："骨骼的沉积与吸收取决于骨骼框架所承受的应力。"

X

x-rays A type of electromagnetic radiation (a form of energy with wave-like behavior that travels at the speed of light). The short wavelength and high frequency of x-rays allows them to penetrate many objects and to be useful for medical imaging. X-rays are a form of ionizing radiation and are carcinogenic; exposure to x-rays should always be "as low as reasonably achievable" (ALARA).
X射线 一种电磁辐射（以波的形式传播、速度等同于光速的能量形式）。X射线因其短波长和高频特性，能穿透多种物质，在医学影像领域具有重要应用价值。X射线属于电离辐射，具有致癌性；接触X射线时应始终遵循"尽可能低"原则（ALARA）。

x-ray film Consists of a transparent sheet (base) coated with an emulsion containing silver halide crystals, which are sensitive to x-rays, visible light, heat, and pressure.
X射线胶片 由透明基底涂覆感光乳剂构成，其中含对X射线、可见光、热量及压力敏感的卤化银晶体。
