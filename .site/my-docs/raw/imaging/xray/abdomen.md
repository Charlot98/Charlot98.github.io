#### 胃扩张扭转（gastric dilatation and volvulus，GDV）

GDV可在胃围绕其自身轴线旋转时发生。该病更常见于大型、深胸犬，但任何犬种、任何年龄均可能发生。猫中较为罕见，但曾有合并膈疝的猫发生胃扭转的报道。胃扭转患者通常以急诊形式就诊，病情危及生命。处于严重危重状态的患者在进行影像检查前必须先行稳定处理。
GDV can occur when the stomach rotates around its axis. It is more common in large, deep-chested dogs, but can occur in any breed of dog and at any age. It is rare in cats but has been reported in a cat with a diaphragmatic hernia. Patients with GDV frequently present as an emergency, in a life-threatening situation. Patients in severe distress must be stabilized prior to imaging.

在胃扭转过程中，幽门会从其正常位于腹腔右侧腹侧的位置旋转至左侧背侧（图 4.59、4.60）。同时，食管在胃贲门处发生扭转。十二指肠随幽门一起向背侧及左侧移位，常绕行于食管周围。胃扭转会导致无法嗳气，并造成幽门流出道阻塞。
During gastric volvulus, the pylorus rotates from its normal position in the ventral, right side of the abdomen to the dorsal, left side (Figures 4.59 and 4.60). Volvulus also twists the esophagus at the cardia of the stomach. The duodenum follows the pylorus and moves dorsally and to the left, often wrapping around the esophagus. GDV results in an inability to eructate and an obstructed pyloric outflow.

The radiographic appearance of GDV is summarized in Table 4.2. Note: radiographic findings depend on the stomach contents, the degree of gastric distention, and the extent of rotation. The key to diagnosis of GDV is to locate the pylorus (Figures 4.60 - 4.63). Locating the pylorus is essential to differentiate GDV from gastric dilatation only. A displaced pylorus is easiest to identify when it contains gas. Because the pylorus typically rotates dorsally and to the left, the right lateral and DV views tend to be the most diagnostic. Remember, the dependent (down) parts of the stomach fill with fluid (and ingesta) while the nondependent (up) parts fill with gas. In right lateral and ventral recumbencies, a pylorus that is displaced to the left and dorsally will be "up" and more likely to fill with gas.

右侧卧位下 GDV 的经典影像表现通常称为胃分隔征（图 4.60、4.61）。胃分隔征表现为位于颅背侧、充满气体的幽门，与一个同时含气体和液体的胃底之间由一条软组织密度带分隔。许多人认为该征象对 GDV 具有特异性，但与大多数放射学征象一样，它并非 100% 具有诊断确定性。
The classic appearance of GDV in the right lateral view is commonly called compartmentalization of the stomach (Figures 4.60 and 4.61). Compartmentalization appears as a gas-filled pylorus positioned craniodorsal to a gas-and-fluid-filled fundus, and with a band of soft tissue opacity between them. Compartmentalization is considered by many to be pathognomonic for GDV but, like most radiographic signs, it is not 100% diagnostic.

如果在右侧卧位和 DV 位片上无法识别幽门，应获取胃的四个体位片（右侧卧、左侧卧、DV 和 VD）。若仍无法明确判断，且患者病情允许进一步检查而非立即手术，则胃造影可能有所帮助。
If the pylorus cannot be identified from right lateral and DV views, make all four views of the stomach (right lateral, left lateral, DV, and VD). If identification remains equivocal and the condition of the patient warrants further diagnostics instead of surgery, a gastrogram may be helpful.

胃扭转通常因胃脾韧带的牵连而导致脾脏移位。随着胃的旋转，脾脏通常向右侧、尾侧及腹侧移位，其在左侧颅背腹部的正常“脾三角”影像将消失。移位的脾脏可因静脉淤血而肿大，部分病例可并发脾扭转。若移位的脾脏未发生肿大，则可能较难识别。
Gastric volvulus usually leads to splenic displacement due to the gastrosplenic attachment. The spleen typically is displaced to the right, caudally and ventrally as the stomach rotates. The splenic triangle will be absent from its normal position in the left craniodorsal abdomen. The displaced spleen may enlarge due to venous congestion. A splenic torsion sometimes is concurrent with GDV. If the displaced spleen does not enlarge, it may be difficult to identify.

GDV 还可能伴发反射性肠麻痹。在此情况下，由于肠蠕动减弱，小肠内广泛充满气体。肠管本身并无明显扩张，但在连续拍摄的放射片中，气体分布模式可能呈静止、不变状态。反射性肠麻痹通常在 GDV 成功治疗后自行缓解。
GDV may be accompanied by a reflex ileus. In these cases, the small intestine is extensively gas-filled due to weak peristalsis. The bowel is not abnormally distended, but the gas pattern may appear static and unchanged in serial radiographs. Reflex ileus usually resolves following successful treatment of GDV.

胃扩张常导致后腔静脉受压，回心静脉血流受限，因此后腔静脉及心影可能显得偏小。GDV 亦可引起食管扩张，通常伴随气体潴留，这是由于胃食管交界处发生阻塞所致。
Gastric dilatation often leads to compression of the caudal vena cava and restriction of the venous return to the heart. The caudal vena cava and the cardiac shadow may be small. GDV also leads to esophageal dilation, usually with gas, due to gastroesophageal obstruction.

GDV 患者出现腹腔游离气体，可能源于胃破裂或近期对胃进行过穿刺减压。由于胃坏死及气体经门静脉系统迁移，有时还可在肝脏或胃壁内见到气体影。
Pneumoperitoneum in patients with GDV can occur due to stomach rupture or recent trocharization of the stomach. Gas occasionally is seen in the liver or gastric wall due to stomach necrosis and migration of gas through the portal system.

需要特别强调的是，缺乏明显的胃严重扩张并不能排除 GDV，这只会使诊断更加困难。有时在镇静后或摆位进行放射检查的过程中，胃可能发生自行复位。应牢记，胃的四体位放射片往往有助于定位幽门，而幽门定位仍然是 GDV 诊断的关键。一种较难诊断的 GDV 类型发生于胃旋转达 360° 的情况下，此时幽门和胃底可能重新回到其解剖学上的正常位置。
Importantly, lack of severe gastric distention does not rule out GDV. It just makes diagnosis more difficult. Sometimes the stomach will de-rotate after the patient is sedated or during positioning for radiography. Remember, four-view radiographs of the stomach often are useful to locate the pylorus, which again, is key to diagnosis of GDV. A difficult-to-diagnose form of GDV occurs when the stomach rotates a full 360°. In these cases, the pylorus and fundus reoccupy their normal positions.
