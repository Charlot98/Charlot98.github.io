# 放射术语

> 

## Glossary of Radiologic Terms <br /> 放射术语对照表

## `A`

**abscess** An accumulation of purulent material in an abnormal cavity surrounded by a wall and typically associated with inflammation. May be caused by infection or presence of foreign material.
<br />

**脓肿** 是指脓性物质在异常空腔内积聚，该空腔周围有壁，通常伴有炎症。脓肿可能由感染或异物引起。

**absorbed x-ray** An x-ray that loses all of its energy inside the patient and completely disappears. Where x-rays are absorbed in radiography, the resultant image is more white (less black).
<br />

**被吸收的 X 射线** 是指在患者体内失去所有能量并完全消失的 X 射线。在放射成像中，X 射线被吸收的部位成像更偏白（黑度更低）。

**achondroplasia** "Without cartilage formation"; a genetic disorder of bone growth that results in disproportionate dwarfism. Appendicular bones are short and thick, metaphyses are cupped and flared, and physes are irregular. Some dog breeds normally are classified as achondrodysplastic (e.g., Bulldog) (a term that often is used interchangeably with chondrodystrophic).
<br />

**软骨发育不全** 字面意为“无软骨形成”；一种骨生长的遗传性疾病，导致不成比例的侏儒症。四肢骨短而粗，干骺端呈杯状并外翻，骺板不规则。一些犬种天生被归类为软骨发育不全型（如斗牛犬），该术语常与软骨营养不良型互换使用。

**air bronchogram** A radiographic sign that occurs during alveolar filling due to retention of air in a bronchus. It appears as a tubular, sometimes branching, gas opacity structure (bronchus) surrounded by soft tissue opacity lung parenchyma. The degree of contrast between the gas-filled bronchus and fluid-filled alveoli depends on the amount of alveolar filling; more complete filling = greater contrast. Air bronchograms only occur with alveolar filling, but alveolar filling can occur without air bronchograms. In most cases of alveolar filling, the bronchus also fills with fluid. Sometimes gas in the bronchus is resorbed. Air bronchograms rarely are seen in collapsed lungs. When present, there usually is some degree of alveolar filling in the collapsed lung.
<br />

**空气支气管征** 是一种发生于肺泡充填时的放射学征象，由于支气管内仍保留空气所致。影像上表现为管状、可呈分支状的气体密度结构（支气管），其周围为软组织密度的肺实质。充气支气管与充液肺泡之间的对比度取决于肺泡充填程度；充填越完全，对比度越高。空气支气管征仅见于肺泡充填，但肺泡充填并不一定出现空气支气管征。多数肺泡充填病例中，支气管本身也会被液体充满；有时支气管内气体会被吸收。肺萎陷时较少见空气支气管征；若出现，通常提示萎陷肺内仍存在一定程度的肺泡充填。

**air gap** Distance between patient (subject or object) and image receptor; increasing the air gap allows scattered x-rays to dissipate but results in magnification of the image.
<br />

**空气间隙** 指患者（受检体或物体）与影像接收器之间的距离；增加空气间隙可使散射 X 射线衰减，但会导致影像放大。

**ALARA** (**A**s **L**ow **A**s **R**easonably **A**chievable) The overriding principle of radiation safety.
<br />

**ALARA 原则** （As Low As Reasonably Achievable，合理可行的最低剂量）是辐射安全的基本总原则。

**aliasing** In digital radiographs, an artifact that appears as a series of repeating lines superimposed on the image.
<br />

**混叠伪影** 在数字放射成像中表现为叠加在影像上的一系列重复线条的伪影。

**alveolar filling** Replacement of the air in the lung air spaces due to an influx of fluid (e.g., blood, edema) or cells (e.g., neoplasia, purulent material). Any solid area of soft tissue opacity in the lung that is not vessel, nodule, or mass is alveolar filling. The air bronchogram sign is characteristic of alveolar filling but is not necessary for diagnosis of alveolar filling. Common causes include pneumonia, hemorrhage, neoplasia, edema.
<br />

**肺泡充填** 指肺泡气腔内的空气被液体（如血液、水肿液）或细胞（如肿瘤细胞、脓性物质）所取代。凡肺内出现非血管、非结节、非肿块的实性软组织密度区域，均可视为肺泡充填。空气支气管征是肺泡充填的典型表现，但并非诊断所必需。常见原因包括肺炎、出血、肿瘤和水肿。

**alveolar pulmonary pattern** A radiographic appearance that occurs due to alveolar filling or collapse. Appears as soft tissue opacity in the lung that obscures the margins of adjacent soft tissue structures (e.g., blood vessels, pulmonary nodules, diaphragm, cardiac silhouette).
<br />

**肺泡型** 由肺泡充填或肺萎陷引起的一种放射学表现，影像上表现为肺内软组织密度影，遮盖邻近软组织结构（如血管、肺结节、膈肌、心影）的边界。

**amyloidosis** A rare disorder of protein metabolism that results in deposition of insoluble protein (*amyloid*) in glomeruli and interstitium of kidneys (and also in other organs). May be heritable (e.g., Shar-Pei, Abyssinian) or acquired (sequela to chronic inflammation/infection). Associated syndromes include Shar-Pei fever, Akita fever/polyarthropathy, Gray Collie. Some cat breeds are predisposed (e.g., Abyssinian, Asian shorthair, Siamese). Clinical signs vary with severity of disease and organs affected; many animals die shortly after onset of signs.
<br />

**淀粉样变性** 一种罕见的蛋白质代谢障碍，导致不溶性蛋白（*淀粉样蛋白*）沉积于肾小球及肾间质（亦可累及其他器官）。可为遗传性（如沙皮犬、阿比西尼亚猫）或获得性（多继发于慢性炎症或感染）。相关综合征包括沙皮犬热、秋田犬热/多关节病、灰色柯利犬综合征。部分猫品种（如阿比西尼亚猫、亚洲短毛猫、暹罗猫）具有易感性。临床表现随病情严重程度及受累器官而异，许多动物在症状出现后不久即死亡。

**angiocardiography** A radiographic contrast study of the heart and great vessels.
<br />

**心血管造影** 对心脏及大血管进行的放射学造影检查。

**angiography** A radiographic contrast study of blood vessels.
<br />

**血管造影** 对血管系统进行的放射学造影检查。

**Anode** Positive terminal in an x-ray tube that attracts electrons from the cathode (negative terminal). Anode contains a target made of metal (tungsten) with a high melting point to withstand the intense heat generated by the electron beam bombardment.
<br />

**阳极** X 射线管中的正极，吸引来自阴极（负极）的电子。阳极内含高熔点金属（通常为钨）制成的靶材，以承受电子束轰击所产生的高热。

**anode heel effect** See **heel effect**.
<br />

**阳极效应** 见 **heel effect**

**apophysis** Essentially, a non-weight-bearing epiphysis; a normal bony projection that serves for the attachment of tendons or ligaments (e.g., greater trochanter, tibial tuberosity); a secondary ossification center that contributes to the shape of a bone but does not contribute to overall bone length.
<br />

**骨突** 本质上为不承重的骺部；一种正常的骨性突起，用于肌腱或韧带附着（如大转子、胫骨粗隆）；属于次级骨化中心，影响骨形态但不参与骨长度的增长。

**appendicular** Pertaining to the limbs.
<br />

**四肢骨骼的** 指与四肢相关的。

**arteriovenous fistula** An abnormal communication between an artery and a vein.
<br />

**动静脉瘘** 动脉与静脉之间的异常交通。

**arthritis** Inflammation of a joint; may be caused by trauma, infection, or immune-mediated disease. Term often is erroneously used for degenerative joint disease.
<br />

**关节炎** 指关节的炎症，可由外伤、感染或免疫介导性疾病引起。该术语常被误用于指代退行性关节病。

**arthrography** A radiographic contrast study of a joint. Arthrogram.
<br />

**关节造影** 对关节进行的放射学造影检查，所得影像称为关节造影片。

**artifact** An erroneous finding. Artifacts may be caused by technical problems associated with film, screens, grid, development, exposure, cassettes, x-ray table, etc., or they may be patient-related issues such as debris on skin/ haircoat, superimposed cutaneous or subcutaneous structures. Artifacts can mimic disease, impair image quality, and obscure abnormalities.
<br />

**伪影** 指错误的影像表现。可由胶片、屏片、滤线栅、显影、曝光、暗盒、X 线检查床等技术问题引起，也可由患者因素造成，如皮肤或被毛附着物、皮肤或皮下结构重叠等。伪影可模拟疾病、降低图像质量并掩盖真实异常。

**asynchronous growth** Refers to the disproportionate rate of growth between paired bones (e.g., radius/ulna, tibia/fibula), typically caused by damage to a physis and commonly resulting in an angular limb deformity and joint subluxation.
<br />

**生长不同步** 指成对骨骼（如桡骨/尺骨、胫骨/腓骨）之间生长速度不一致，通常由骺板损伤引起，常导致肢体成角畸形及关节半脱位。

**atelectasis** Incomplete expansion of part or all of a lung. Also used to describe partial or complete lung collapse. Atelectasis may result from disease of lung or pleural space. Common with animals positioned in lateral recumbency.
<br />

**肺不张** 指肺的部分或全部扩张不全，也可用于描述部分或完全性肺萎陷。可由肺部或胸膜腔疾病引起，在侧卧位动物中较为常见。

**atresia ani** A rare malformation of the anus. Radiographs sometimes are used to assess the severity of the condition, which varies from a stenosis to a complete blockage. Typically, the colon is largely distended with feces. A fistula can develop between the rectum and either the vagina, urinary bladder, or urethra, enabling the patient to defecate through the vagina or urethra. Gas must be in the rectum to visualize the abnormality in survey radiographs. Sometimes elevating the caudal end of the patient will allow gas in the descending colon to move into the rectum. Evaluate the distance between the rectum and the anal membrane using positive contrast medium (either oral or per-rectal dosing) or a radiopaque probe inserted into the anus. When possible, add a negative-contrast enema (pneumocolon) to identify the rectum. Infusion of iodinated contrast medium into the urethra, vagina, or rectum may document a fistula or other malformation.
<br />

**肛门闭锁** 一种罕见的肛门畸形。放射检查有时用于评估其严重程度，范围可从狭窄至完全阻塞。通常结肠因粪便潴留而明显扩张。直肠与阴道、膀胱或尿道之间可形成瘘管，使患者经阴道或尿道排便。平片检查需直肠内有气体方可显示异常；有时抬高动物尾侧可使降结肠气体进入直肠。可通过口服或经直肠给予阳性对比剂，或将不透射探针插入肛门，评估直肠与肛膜之间的距离；条件允许时可联合负性对比灌肠（气结肠造影）以显示直肠。向尿道、阴道或直肠注入含碘对比剂可证实瘘管或其他畸形。

**avascular necrosis of the femoral head** A loss of blood supply to the developing femoral head that results in bone necrosis and eventual collapse. Condition may be idiopathic, genetic, or traumatic. The eponym is "Legg-Calvé-Perthes disease."
<br />

**股骨头缺血性坏死** 指发育中的股骨头血供丧失，导致骨坏死并最终塌陷。病因可为特发性、遗传性或外伤性，其别名为雷卡佩氏病（Legg-Calvé-Perthes病）。

**axial** Pertains to the median line of the body, a limb, or other structure. Axial skeletal structures include vertebral column, skull, hyoid bones, ribs, and sternum.
<br />

**轴向的** 指与身体、肢体或其他结构的中线相关。轴向骨骼结构包括脊柱、颅骨、舌骨、肋骨及胸骨。

## `B`

**barium sulfate** A positive contrast medium with a high degree of opacity (atomic number of barium is 56). Barium is a white crystalline powder that is micro-pulverized and placed in suspension. It adheres well to suspending agents, enabling it to efficiently coat mucosal surfaces. Barium is not metabolized or absorbed. The word "barium" comes from the Greek word meaning "heavy."
<br />

**硫酸钡** 一种高密度的阳性对比剂（钡的原子序数为 56）。钡为白色结晶性粉末，经微粉化后制成悬浮液，能良好附着于悬浮剂，从而有效包覆黏膜表面。硫酸钡不被机体代谢或吸收。“barium”一词源自希腊语，意为“沉重”。

**barium enema** A radiographic contrast study of the colon using barium sulfate.
<br />

**钡灌肠** 使用硫酸钡对结肠进行的放射学造影检查。

**beak sign** Occurs when contrast medium fills only the entrance of the pyloric sphincter; commonly associated with pyloric stenosis.
<br />

**喙征** 指对比剂仅充填幽门括约肌入口处的影像表现，常与幽门狭窄相关。

**beam direction** Path of the x-rays as they enter and exit a structure (e.g., dorsoventral, dorsopalmar).
<br />

**射线方向** 指 X 射线进入并穿出结构时的路径（如背腹向、背掌向）。

**beam hardening** Refers to increasing the average energy of the x-ray beam by using filters to remove lower energy x-rays. Filters commonly are made of aluminum and positioned in the path of the primary x-ray beam near the x-ray tube window.
<br />

**射线硬化** 指通过使用滤过器去除低能 X 射线，从而提高 X 射线束的平均能量。滤过器通常由铝制成，放置于靠近 X 射线管窗口的主射线束路径中。

**bezoar** A concretion of fibrous material commonly found in the stomach or intestine.
<br />

**胃石（毛球）** 常见于胃或肠道内的纤维性物质凝结块。

**binary number** A Base 2 numbering system used by computers. The position of each digit (bit) in a binary number represents an exponent of 2. In a base 10 numbering system (decimal system), the position of each digit represents an exponent of 10. This means that moving right to left in a decimal number, the value of each digit is 10⁰, 10¹, 10², 10³, 10⁴, etc., whereas in a binary number the value of each digit (bit) is 2⁰, 2¹, 2², 2³, 2⁴, etc. Each decimal digit can be any numeral 0–9, but each binary digit can only be 0 or 1. In both numbering systems, adding more digits to the number makes the total value of the number grow exponentially, either by factors of 2 or 10.
<br />

**二进制数** 计算机使用的以 2 为基数的计数系统。二进制数中每一位（bit）的位置代表 2 的幂。在以 10 为基数的十进制系统中，每一位的位置代表 10 的幂。这意味着在十进制中从右向左，各位的数值分别为 10⁰、10¹、10²、10³、10⁴ 等，而在二进制中各位的数值为 2⁰、2¹、2²、2³、2⁴ 等。十进制每一位可取 0–9 的任意数值，而二进制每一位只能为 0 或 1。在两种系统中，位数的增加都会使数值呈指数级增长，分别以 2 或 10 为倍数。

**bit** The smallest unit of digital data. A bit is a digit in a binary number ("bit" is short for **Bi**nary Digi**t**).
<br />

**比特** 数字数据的最小单位。比特是二进制数中的一位数字（“bit”是 **Bi**nary Digi**t** 的缩写）。

**bit depth** The number of unique shades of gray available to display a radiograph. Not every gray shade will necessarily be used in the radiograph, but the shades can be displayed with that level of precision.
<br />

**位深** 指用于显示放射影像的不同灰阶数量。并非所有灰阶都会在影像中使用，但系统具备以该精度显示灰阶的能力。

**block vertebra** A congenital defect caused by failure of one or more vertebral bodies to segment normally during development. Vertebrae appear fused, but each vertebral body maintains normal length.
<br />

**椎体融合** 一种先天缺陷，由于一个或多个椎体在发育过程中分节失败所致。影像上椎体呈融合状态，但各椎体长度仍保持正常。

**bone cyst** A cavity within bone, often filled with fluid. Usually, well-defined margins and may be septated.
<br />

**骨囊肿** 骨内的空腔，常充满液体，通常边界清楚，可伴有分隔。

**bone infarcts** Foci of necrosis caused by decreased blood supply to intramedullary bone; appear as multiple small areas of increased medullary opacity.
<br />

**骨梗死** 由于骨髓内血供减少导致的局灶性坏死，影像上表现为多发的小片状髓腔密度增高区。

**bone sclerosis** An increase in opacity of bone; **eburnation** is a type of bone sclerosis.
<br />

**骨硬化** 指骨密度增高；**象牙样硬化**（**eburnation**）是骨硬化的一种类型。

**bone spur** A well-defined bony projection. Term is used to describe osteophytes (occur along non-weight-bearing articular margins) and enthesophytes (occur at sites of attachment to bone, e.g., ligaments, tendons, joint capsules).
<br />

**骨刺** 边界清楚的骨性突起。该术语用于描述骨赘（位于非负重关节缘）及附着点矿化（发生于韧带、肌腱、关节囊等骨附着处）。

**border effacement** Occurs when two structures of same opacity are in contact and their individual margins are no longer distinguishable where they touch. The margin of a structure is only visible when it is adjacent to a different opacity (i.e., opacity interface). Other names: edge effacement, silhouette sign/effect. Erroneously called "loss of detail."
<br />

**边界消除效应** 当两个具有相同密度的结构相互接触时，其接触部位的边缘无法区分。结构边界只有在邻近不同密度时才可见（即密度界面）。又称边缘消失、剪影征/效应，常被误称为“细节丢失”。

**brachycephalic** Refers to the shape of a skull in which the nasal cavity is considerably shorter than the cranium. Also, the cranium commonly is more dome shaped, frontal sinuses tend to be small or absent, and the occipital protuberance often is small. Examples: English Bulldog, Pug.
<br />

**短头型** 指鼻腔明显短于颅腔的颅骨形态，颅骨多呈圆顶状，额窦常较小或缺如，枕外隆突通常较小。例：英国斗牛犬、巴哥犬。

**Bremsstrahlung x-ray** An x-ray that is produced from the loss of kinetic energy that occurs when a speeding electron from the x-ray tube cathode nears the nucleus of a target atom in the anode, causing the electron to slow down and change direction; bremsstrahlung = "braking radiation."
<br />

**轫致辐射 X 射线** 当来自 X 射线管阴极的高速电子接近阳极靶原子核时，因减速和改变方向而损失动能所产生的 X 射线；bremsstrahlung 意为“制动辐射（braking radiation）”。

**bronchial blood vessels** Part of the systemic circulation that provides blood for lung nutrition. Bronchial arteries and veins are too small to be seen in radiographs. They contribute only about 1% of the total blood volume in the lungs (the pulmonary blood vessels supply the remaining 99%).
<br />

**支气管血管** 属于全身循环的一部分，为肺组织提供营养。支气管动脉和静脉过小，无法在放射影像中显示，仅占肺总血容量约 1%，其余 99%由肺血管系统提供。

**bronchial cuffing** Thickened soft tissue opacity around a bronchus due to accumulation of fluid, cells, or fibrosis; a sign of bronchial disease or peribronchial accumulation; also **peribronchial cuffing**, **peribronchial infiltrates**.
<br />

**支气管套征** 指支气管周围因液体、细胞或纤维组织积聚而形成的软组织密度增厚影，是支气管疾病或支气管周围积聚的征象；亦称**支气管周围套征**或**支气管周围浸润**。

**bronchial pulmonary pattern** A radiographic appearance in which bronchial walls appear thickened and/or increased in opacity (latter includes mineralization of bronchial walls). Commonly caused by proliferation or infiltration of collagenous and cartilaginous tissues and associated mucous glands. May also result from accumulation of peribronchial interstitial fluid or cellular material. The size and shape of the bronchial lumen may be altered.
<br />

**支气管型** 一种放射学表现，表现为支气管壁增厚和/或密度增高（包括支气管壁矿化）。常由胶原及软骨组织增生或浸润及相关黏液腺改变引起，也可因支气管周围间质液体或细胞物质积聚所致。支气管腔的大小和形态可发生改变。

**bronchiectasis** Abnormal and permanent dilation of bronchi or bronchioles with loss of normal branching and tapering.
<br />

**支气管扩张** 支气管或细支气管异常且永久性扩张，并伴有正常分支及渐细结构的消失。

**bronchitis** Inflammation of the bronchi.
<br />

**支气管炎** 指支气管的炎症。

**bronchography** A radiographic contrast study of the major airways.
<br />

**支气管造影** 对主要气道进行的放射学造影检查。

**bronchopneumonia** Inflammation of the lungs that usually begins in the terminal bronchioles.
<br />

**支气管肺炎** 通常起始于终末细支气管的肺部炎症。

**brown fat** Present in juvenile animals to help with thermoregulation. Brown fat is composed of smaller lipids and receives a larger blood supply than mature fat. It is higher in water content and therefore more opaque than adult fat.
<br />

**棕色脂肪** 存在于幼年动物体内，有助于调节体温。棕色脂肪由较小的脂滴组成，血供较成熟脂肪丰富，含水量更高，因此在影像中密度高于成年脂肪。

**brush border** The edge or margin resembles the bristles of a brush.
<br />

**刷状缘** 指边缘或轮廓呈刷毛样外观。

**buccal** On the side that faces the cheek; used to describe lesions associated with the mouth.
<br />

**颊侧的** 指面向面颊的一侧，用于描述口腔相关病变。

**Bucky** A device that holds a grid within the x-ray table. The Bucky moves the grid rapidly during exposure to blur the grid lines. Modern grid lines are very thin and only faintly visible; therefore, a Bucky may not be necessary with modern equipment. First introduced by Dr. Hollis Potter and often called a Potter-Bucky diaphragm.
<br />

**结石** 指在器官或管道内形成的结晶性物质，通常为矿物盐（复数：*calculi*）。肾结石（renal calculi）亦称为 *nephroliths*、*uroliths* 或 *kidney stones*。

**松质骨** 位于长骨两端、椎骨及扁骨内的骨小梁状、海绵样或多孔骨组织。

**犬白细胞黏附缺陷综合征（CLAD）** 一种罕见的遗传性疾病，由粒细胞功能异常引起，可导致下颌骨骨膜反应及干骺端膨大。

**carcinomatosis** Widespread distribution of neoplasia throughout a body cavity; commonly used to describe diffuse implantation of tumor nodules within the peritoneal space (e.g., pancreatic carcinoma).
<br />

**癌扩散** 指肿瘤在体腔内的广泛分布，常用于描述腹腔内肿瘤结节的弥漫性种植（如胰腺癌）。

**cardiac silhouette** Outline of the pericardial sac and its contents as seen in a thoracic radiograph.
<br />

**心影** 胸部放射影像中心包及其内容物的轮廓。

**carnassial teeth** Relatively large teeth that are adapted for shearing; includes the maxillary fourth premolar teeth and the mandibular first molar teeth.
<br />

**裂齿** 适于剪切食物的较大牙齿，包括上颌第四前磨牙和下颌第一磨牙。

**cartilage joint** Two or more bones united by hyaline cartilage or fibrocartilage (e.g., costochondral junctions, pelvic symphysis).
<br />

**软骨关节** 由透明软骨或纤维软骨连接的两个或多个骨骼（如肋软骨结合部、耻骨联合）。

**cassette** A light-tight, semi-rigid container used to house and protect either x-ray film and screens or a phosphor-coated imaging plate during exposure and transport to processing.
<br />

**暗盒** 一种避光的半刚性容器，用于在曝光及转运过程中容纳并保护 X 线胶片及增感屏或涂覆磷光体的成像板。

**cathode** The negative terminal in the x-ray tube. Cathode filament emits electrons, which are attracted to the anode (positive terminal). Current (electrons) flows from cathode to anode. In x-ray tubes, the cathode commonly consists of a helical tungsten filament, behind which is a molybdenum reflector cup used to focus the electron emission toward the anode target.
<br />

**阴极** X 射线管中的负极。阴极灯丝释放电子，这些电子被吸引至阳极（正极），电子流由阴极流向阳极。X 射线管中的阴极通常由螺旋状钨丝组成，其后方设有钼制反射杯，用于将电子聚焦指向阳极靶面。

**caudal** Toward the tail or hind part of the body. Also used to designate the flexor side of a limb proximal to the carpus and tarsus.
<br />

**尾侧的** 指朝向身体尾部或后部的方向；亦用于描述腕关节和跗关节近端肢体的屈侧。

**caudocranial** Refers to the path of the x-ray beam (x-rays enter the caudal aspect of the body part and exit the cranial aspect).
<br />

**尾头向** 指 X 射线束自身体尾侧进入、由头侧射出的路径。

**cecal inversion** Also called cecocolic intussusception. Prolapse of the cecum into the ascending colon is uncommon in dogs and cats. It typically affects young animals (less than 4 years) but can occur at any age. Cecal inversion usually is associated with hypermotility due to inflammation or parasitism. It may be predisposed in animals with a weak ileocecocolic ligament. Clinical signs vary with severity and duration of the obstruction (hematochezia, tenesmus, vomiting).
<br />

**盲肠翻转** 又称盲结肠套叠。盲肠脱入升结肠在犬猫中较少见，多发生于 4 岁以下的年轻动物，但任何年龄均可发生。通常与炎症或寄生虫引起的肠道高蠕动相关，在回盲结肠韧带薄弱的动物中更易发生。临床症状随阻塞的严重程度和持续时间而异，包括血便、里急后重及呕吐。

**celiography** A radiographic contrast study of the peritoneal space.
<br />

**腹腔造影** 对腹膜腔进行的放射学造影检查。

**central beam** Refers to the x-ray photons in the middle of the x-ray beam.
<br />

**中心射线** 指位于 X 射线束中央的射线光子。

**cervical spondylopathy** Syndrome that may include deformity of vertebral bodies, narrowing of the vertebral canal, vertebral instability, and/or malarticulation with varying degrees of spinal cord compression in the neck.
<br />

**颈椎脊髓病** 一种综合征，可包括椎体变形、椎管狭窄、椎体不稳定和/或关节错位，并在颈部产生不同程度的脊髓压迫。

**characteristic x-ray** An x-ray that is produced when an outer shell electron gives up energy as it drops to fill a void created by a lost inner shell electron; x-ray energy corresponds to the difference in binding energy between the outer shell and inner shell electrons, which is characteristic of the type of atom.
<br />

**特征 X 射线** 当外层电子跃迁以填补内层电子空位并释放能量时产生的 X 射线，其能量等于外层与内层电子结合能之差，具有元素特异性。

**characteristic curve** A graphic representation of the dynamic range of an image receptor. Characteristic curves are also known as *H & D curves*, named for the two chemists who developed them in 1890, Hurter and Driffield.
<br />

**特性曲线** 图形化表示影像接收器动态范围的曲线，又称 *H & D 曲线*，得名于 1890 年提出该曲线的 Hurter 和 Driffield 两位化学家。

**cholecystocholangiography** A radiographic contrast study of the gall bladder and bile ducts.

**cholecystography** A radiographic contrast study of the gall bladder.

**胆囊造影** 对胆囊进行的放射学造影检查。

**chondrodysplasia** Inherited deformities of the bony skeleton caused by abnormal cartilage development. Affected animals are characterized by a normal-sized trunk with abnormally shortened limbs.
<br />

**软骨发育不良** 由软骨发育异常引起的遗传性骨骼畸形，患病动物通常躯干大小正常，但四肢明显缩短。

**chondrodystrophic** Refers to an animal exhibiting chondrodysplasia (e.g., Dachshund, Bassett Hound). Term often is used interchangeably with achondrodysplastic.
<br />

**软骨营养不良型** 指表现出软骨发育不良的动物（如腊肠犬、巴吉度犬），该术语常与软骨发育不全型互换使用。

**chondroma** A benign, slow-growing tumor of cartilage, more often occurring in flat bones (e.g., scapula, pelvis, ribs) and appearing as a well-defined, expansile mass that is less opaque than surrounding bone. A chondroma may contain mineralized components.
<br />

**软骨瘤** 一种良性、缓慢生长的软骨肿瘤，多发生于扁骨（如肩胛骨、骨盆、肋骨），影像上表现为边界清楚、膨胀性的肿块，其密度低于周围骨组织，内部可含矿化成分。

**chylothorax** Accumulation of chyle in the pleural space, often secondary to rupture of the thoracic duct.
<br />

**乳糜胸** 乳糜液在胸膜腔内的积聚，常继发于胸导管破裂。

**chylous effusion** Accumulation of chylous fluid in the pleural space, may be associated with heart failure, mediastinal neoplasia, or mediastinitis.
<br />

**乳糜性积液** 乳糜样液体在胸膜腔内的积聚，可与心力衰竭、纵隔肿瘤或纵隔炎相关。

**cineradiography (cineflurography)** The making of a video record directly from a fluorescent screen (i.e., recording of real-time radiographic imaging.)
<br />

**动态放射成像（荧光电影成像）** 直接从荧光屏记录视频的成像方式，即实时放射影像的记录。

**cirrhosis of the liver** Hepatic cirrhosis is scarring and fibrosis of liver that is caused by chronic inflammation that eventually leads to liver failure. Many animals with hepatic cirrhosis are asymptomatic until liver failure; regenerative nodules may develop in damaged liver. Clinical signs vary with severity of disease, but may include polyuria, polydipsia, GI signs, neurologic signs (hepatoencephalopathy). Causes of hepatic cirrhosis include infection (e.g., viral, leptospirosis), toxins, copper storage disease, immune-mediated diseases, and idiopathic.
<br />

**肝硬化** 肝脏因慢性炎症导致瘢痕形成和纤维化，最终可发展为肝功能衰竭。许多动物在出现肝衰竭前无明显症状，受损肝脏内可形成再生结节。临床表现随病情严重程度而异，可包括多尿、多饮、胃肠道症状及神经系统症状（肝性脑病）。病因包括感染（如病毒、钩端螺旋体）、毒素、铜储积病、免疫介导性疾病及特发性原因。

**clock face analogy** Technique used to approximate the locations of major components of the cardiac shadow by mentally superimposing the numbers of a clock face over the cardiac silhouette on a lateral or ventrodorsal/dorsoventral thoracic radiograph.
<br />

**表盘类比法** 一种通过在侧位或腹背/背腹位胸片中心影上假想叠加钟表刻度，以估测心影主要组成部分位置的技术。

**Codman's triangle** Occurs when aggressive soft tissue opacity material wedges between a mineralized periosteal response and the underlying cortex. The periosteal response may appear solid, well-defined, and homogeneous in this area, giving the erroneous appearance of a benign, inactive lesion (which is not the true nature of the disease process). Codman's triangle develops at the periphery of a rapidly growing disease process, usually on the diaphyseal side.
<br />

**科德曼三角** 当侵袭性软组织密度物质嵌入矿化的骨膜反应与下方皮质骨之间时形成。该区域的骨膜反应可表现为致密、边界清楚且均匀，从而造成良性、非活动性病变的假象（并非疾病真实性质）。Codman 三角多形成于快速生长病变的边缘，通常位于骨干侧。

**coin lesion** A descriptive term referring to a pulmonary nodule approximately 1 cm in diameter.
<br />

**硬币状病灶** 用于描述直径约 1 cm 的肺结节。

**collimation** Process of restricting the size and shape of the x-ray beam to the area of interest. Collimation helps reduce scatter radiation, which improves image quality and reduces radiation exposure to nearby personnel.
<br />

**准直** 指将 X 射线束的大小和形状限制在感兴趣区域的过程，可减少散射线，提高影像质量，并降低周围人员的辐射暴露。

**collimator** An adjustable device used to restrict the size and shape of the x-ray beam and which is attached near the x-ray tube.
<br />

**准直器** 安装于 X 射线管附近、用于调节射线束大小和形状的装置。

**comparison radiographs** Images made of the side opposite to the one being investigated or images of a similar subject (e.g., littermate) or known normal subject, used to aid in distinguishing normal from abnormal.
<br />

**对照X线片** 拍摄受检部位对侧、相似个体（如同窝动物）或已知正常个体的影像，用于辅助区分正常与异常。

**compartmentalization (of the stomach)** A classic radiographic finding in patients with gastric dilatation and volvulus (GDV). It appears as two large, gas-filled "bubbles" in the cranial abdomen. The "compartments" or "bubbles" represent the gastric pylorus and body of the stomach, separated by a fold or band of soft tissue (sometimes called a "shelf" of soft tissue). Compartmentalization usually is easiest to see in a right lateral view, but it is not present in all cases of GDV. Colloquial nicknames include "reverse C," "boxing glove," "double bubble," "Popeye's arm," and "Smurf hat."
<br />

**胃室分隔现象** 胃扩张扭转（gastric dilatation and volvulus，GDV）患者的经典放射学表现，在颅腹部可见两个充满气体的“大泡”。这些“隔室”或“气泡”分别代表幽门和胃体，中间由一条软组织褶皱或带状结构分隔（有时称为软组织“架”）。该征象通常在右侧卧位最易观察，但并非所有 GDV 病例均出现。俗称包括“倒C样”、“拳击手套样”、“双气泡样”、“大力水手手臂样”和“蓝精灵帽样”。

**compression radiography** Technique used to improve visualization of a structure(s) by displacing the adjacent structures to eliminate superimposition (e.g., displace overlying intestine to better visualize kidney, urinary bladder, uterus, etc. or to separate the digits during radiography of a foot). A low-density plastic paddle or wooden spoon is used to gently compress the abdomen or foot. Abdominal compression is contraindicated in animals with pain, tense abdomen, or peritoneal effusion. X-ray exposure usually is decreased to accommodate thinner body thickness. During the performance of an excretory urogram, caudal abdominal compression is used to deliberately obstruct the flow of urine for a short period of time to dilate the renal collecting system and enhance filling and opacification.
<br />

**加压放射成像** 通过推移邻近结构以消除重叠、改善目标结构显示的技术（如推开肠道以更好显示肾脏、膀胱、子宫，或在足部摄影时分开趾骨）。通常使用低密度塑料压板或木勺对腹部或足部进行轻柔加压。疼痛、腹部紧张或存在腹腔积液的动物禁忌腹部加压。因受检厚度减小，X 射线曝光量通常需相应降低。在进行排泄性尿路造影时，可短暂施加尾腹部加压以人为阻断尿液流动，从而扩张肾集合系统并增强充盈和显影。

**computed tomography (CT)** A diagnostic imaging modality that creates cross-sectional views of a body part using x-rays and computers. The source of x-rays rotates around the patient, transmitting a thin x-ray beam through the body part to detectors on the opposite side. CT images have inherent high contrast and eliminate superimposition of overlying structures.
<br />

**计算机断层扫描（CT）** 一种利用 X 射线和计算机生成身体部位横断面图像的诊断成像方式。X 射线源围绕患者旋转，发射细束射线穿过身体并由对侧探测器接收。CT 图像具有固有的高对比度，并可消除结构重叠。

**consolidation** Refers to pathology of the lung in which alveolar air has been replaced by organized cellular exudate or by invasion of neoplastic cells.
<br />

**肺实变** 指肺泡内空气被有组织的细胞性渗出物或肿瘤细胞侵入所取代的肺部病理状态。

**constipation** Infrequent or difficult passage of feces.
<br />

**便秘** 排便次数减少或排便困难。

**contralateral** Pertaining to the opposite side.
<br />

**对侧的** 指位于身体相对一侧的。

**contrast media** Compounds or materials used to increase or decrease opacity to create an opacity interface between an examined structure and the adjacent tissues.
<br />

**造影剂** 用于增加或降低密度，以在被检查结构与邻近组织之间形成密度界面的物质。

**contrast study** A radiographic procedure using a positive or negative contrast agent to enhance visualization of an organ or structure that is inadequately seen on survey radiographs. A contrast study is indicated only if it will provide information not available on survey radiographs and will assist the clinician in managing the case or in developing a diagnosis without harming the patient.
<br />

**造影检查** 采用阳性或阴性造影剂增强器官或结构显影的放射学检查程序，用于改善常规X线片显示不足的病灶。仅当造影检查能提供常规X线片无法获取的信息，且在不损害患者前提下有助于临床决策或确立诊断时方可实施。

**contusion** A local area of hemorrhage or bruising.
<br />

**挫伤** 局部出血或瘀伤。

**cortical bone** The dense, homogeneous bone that forms the shaft of long bones and the outer layer of small and cuboidal bones.
<br />

**皮质骨** 构成长骨骨干及小骨、短骨外层的致密均质骨组织。

**costophrenic angle** Junction of the thoracic wall (rib cage) and diaphragm at the caudolateral aspect of the thoracic cavity on a ventrodorsal or dorsoventral radiograph. Angle normally is sharp and well-defined, but it becomes rounded when pleural fluid is present.
<br />

**肋膈角** 在腹背位或背腹位胸片上，胸壁（肋笼）与膈肌在胸腔尾外侧交汇形成的角度。正常情况下尖锐清晰，存在胸腔积液时则变钝、变圆。

**cranial** Toward the head; opposite is **caudal**.
<br />

**头侧（cranial）** 指朝向头部的方向；相对方向为 **尾侧**（caudal）。

**craniocaudal** Refers to the path of the x-ray beam (x-rays enter the cranial aspect of the body part and exit the caudal aspect).
<br />

**头尾向** 指 X 射线束自身体头侧进入、由尾侧射出的路径。

**cretinism (congenital hypothyroidism)** Disproportionate dwarfism caused by abnormal development of the thyroid gland, defective thyroid hormone synthesis, or iodine deficiency. Most often is reported in Boxers and sometimes in other breeds (e.g., Affenpinscher, Giant Schnauzer, Great Dane, Scottish Deerhound).
<br />

**克汀病（先天性甲状腺功能减退）** 由甲状腺发育异常、甲状腺激素合成缺陷或碘缺乏导致的体型比例失调性侏儒症。最常见于拳师犬，也可见于其他犬种（如猴面犬、巨型雪纳瑞、大丹犬、苏格兰猎鹿犬）。

**crus** The part of pelvic limb between femur and tarsus, the shank; also a part of the diaphragm that anchors the cupula to the spine, so named because its shape was thought to resemble a leg.
<br />

**小腿 / 膈脚** 指后肢股骨与跗骨之间的部分；亦指膈肌的一部分，用于将膈顶固定于脊柱，因形似腿而得名。

**cut back zone** Refers to the area in a long bone where the wider metaphysis is actively remodeling to become the more-narrow diaphysis. The bony margins in the cut back zone normally appear indistinct and may be mistaken for pathology (e.g., hypertrophic osteodystrophy, osteomyelitis, trauma).
<br />

**回缩区** 指长骨中较宽的干骺端向较窄骨干转变、正在重塑的区域。该区骨缘通常不清晰，可能被误认为病变（如肥大性骨营养不良、骨髓炎或外伤）。

**cystography** A radiographic contrast study of the urinary bladder. Cystogram. Pneumocystogram.
<br />

**膀胱造影术** 对膀胱进行的放射性造影检查，又称膀胱造影片或气膀胱造影。

## `D`

**dacryocystorhinography** A radiographic contrast study of the nasolacrimal duct.
<br />

**泪囊鼻管造影** 对鼻泪管进行的放射学造影检查。

**decubitus** Position assumed when lying down. Opposed to erect or standing.
<br />

**卧位** 指躺卧时所处的体位，与直立或站立位相对。

**degenerative joint disease (DJD)** The most common joint disorder in dogs and cats, characterized by degradation of articular cartilage and subchondral bone (also called osteoarthritis and osteoarthrosis). **ectopic ureter** A congenital defect in which one or both ureters empties abnormally into the urethra or vagina. It is more common in dogs than in cats. Females more often are affected than males. <br />

**骨骺** “位于生长板之上”，指长骨支持关节软骨的圆形末端结构，来源于长骨近端或远端的第二骨化中心。在未成熟动物中，骺与干骺端之间由生长板分隔。

**epithelial** Relating to the thin tissue forming the outer layer of a body's surface and lining the alimentary canal and other hollow structures.
<br />

**上皮的** 指构成体表外层及消化道和其他空腔结构内衬的薄层组织。

**erect** Position in which the animal is vertical (frequently held upright and usually standing on its hind legs); x-ray beam commonly is directed horizontally.
<br />

**直立位** 动物处于垂直姿势（常被扶持直立，通常以后肢站立），X 射线束多为水平入射。

**esophagography** A radiographic contrast study of the esophagus. Esophagram.
<br />

**食管造影** 对食管进行的放射学造影检查，亦称食管造影片。

**excretory urography (EU)** A radiographic contrast study of the kidney and ureters. Other names: intravenous urography (old term: intravenous pyelogram or IVP).
<br />

**排泄性尿路造影（EU）** 对肾脏及输尿管进行的放射学造影检查，亦称静脉尿路造影（旧称静脉肾盂造影 或 IVP）。

**exostosis** A bony growth that projects outward from the surface of a bone. The term is nondescript, and any exostosis should be investigated and further described as to origin (e.g., periosteal response, osteophyte, enthesophyte).
<br />

**外生骨疣** 从骨表面向外突出的骨性生长物。该术语本身不具特异性，任何骨外生物均应进一步明确其来源（如骨膜反应、骨赘或附着点矿化）。

**extension** The straightening of a limb or other body part; opposite is **flexion**.
<br />

**伸展** 指肢体或身体部位的伸直动作，其反义词为 **屈曲**。

**extrapleural sign** Refers to lesions arising outside the parietal or mediastinal pleura; commonly originating from the thoracic wall, diaphragm, or mediastinum. Presents as a mass bulging into the thoracic cavity, displacing (indenting) the adjacent lung. Margins of the mass taper cranially and caudally to blend with the thoracic wall, diaphragm, or mediastinum.
<br />

**胸膜外征** 指起源于壁层或纵隔胸膜之外的病变，常来自胸壁、膈肌或纵隔。影像上表现为向胸腔内突出的肿块，压迫邻近肺组织，其边缘在头尾方向逐渐变细并与胸壁、膈肌或纵隔融合。

## `F`

**Fanconi syndrome** Congenital or acquired renal tubular dysfunction; Hereditary in Basenji; idiopathic in Norwegian Elk-hound, Shetland Sheepdog, Miniature Schnauzer. May be acquired secondary to cystic disease, adverse drug reaction, heavy metal toxins, or multiple myeloma. Affected animals are unable to concentrate urine and cannot resorb glucose, electrolytes, protein, or uric acid. Glucosuria with normal blood glucose is highly suggestive of Fanconi syndrome. Clinical signs of renal disease are progressive, typically beginning at 3–8 years of age and lead to renal failure. Survey and contrast radiographs usually are normal.
<br />

**范可尼综合征** 先天性或获得性肾小管功能障碍；巴辛吉犬为遗传性，挪威猎鹿犬、喜乐蒂牧羊犬和迷你雪纳瑞多为特发性。亦可继发于囊性疾病、药物不良反应、重金属中毒或多发性骨髓瘤。患病动物无法浓缩尿液，亦不能重吸收葡萄糖、电解质、蛋白质或尿酸。正常血糖水平下出现葡萄糖尿高度提示范可尼综合征。肾脏疾病的临床表现呈进行性发展，通常在 3–8 岁起病并最终导致肾衰竭。平片及对比造影通常无明显异常。

**feline polyarthritis** A progressive, noninfectious, erosive or nonerosive inflammation of multiple joints with or without bone proliferation.
<br />

**猫多发性关节炎** 一种进行性的、非感染性多关节炎症，可为侵蚀性或非侵蚀性，可伴或不伴骨增生。

**feline capital physeal dysplasia syndrome** A condition of unknown etiology in which osteonecrosis of the femoral neck leads to pathologic fracture at the capital physis. May be unilateral or bilateral (if unilateral, the contralateral limb often is affected within 6–12 months).
<br />

**猫股骨头生长板发育不良综合征** 一种病因不明的疾病，股骨颈骨坏死导致股骨头生长板发生病理性骨折，可为单侧或双侧；若为单侧，常在 6–12 个月内累及对侧肢体。（别称：**猫自发性股骨头生长板骨折**、股骨头骨骺滑脱、股骨头生长板骨折、股骨颈干骺端骨病）

**fibrous joint** A bony union with little mobility and lacking a joint cavity (e.g., sutures of the skull, attachment of hyoid bones to petrous temporal bone, attachment of tooth to alveolar bone). Other names: synarthrodial joint, synarthrosis.
<br />

**纤维性关节** 缺乏关节腔、活动度极小的骨性连接（如颅骨缝、舌骨与岩部颞骨的连接、牙齿与牙槽骨的连接），亦称联合关节或不动关节。

**field of view** Area of the body through which the primary x-ray beam passes. Field of view should be restricted to the area of interest by collimating the x-ray beam. Restriction of the field of view helps maximize image quality, minimize scatter radiation, and decrease distortion.
<br />

**视野** 主 X 射线束穿过的身体区域。应通过限束将视野限制在感兴趣区域，以最大化影像质量、减少散射辐射并降低畸变。

**filament** A thin, threadlike conducting wire with a high melting point that is part of the cathode (the negative side of the x-ray tube). The filament is the source of electrons.
<br />

**灯丝** 属于阴极（X 射线管负极）的高熔点细导线，是电子的来源。

**filling defect** Anything that prevents contrast medium from occupying a hollow organ or space. Appears as a less opaque area(s) on a positive contrast study and a more opaque area(s) on a negative contrast study. May be caused by pathology or artifact.
<br />

**充盈缺损** 指任何阻止对比剂充满空腔器官或空间的因素。在阳性对比造影中表现为低密度区，在阴性对比造影中表现为高密度区，可由病变或伪影引起。

**film:screen system** Refers to a conventional radiography set up with a piece of x-ray film sandwiched between two intensifying screens and housed in a light-proof cassette.
<br />

**胶片-增感屏系统** 指传统放射成像系统，其中 X 线胶片夹置于两张增感屏之间，并置于避光暗盒内。

**fissure line** See **pleural fissure line**<br />

**裂隙线** 见 **胸膜裂隙线**

**fistulography** A radiographic contrast study used to investigate a fistulous tract or draining wound. Fistulogram.
<br />

**瘘管造影** 用于检查瘘道或引流性创口的放射学造影检查。

**flail chest** Refers to instability of the thoracic wall caused by sequential, segmental rib fractures that result in a portion of the chest wall becoming functionally detached. Flail chest is an emergency situation because it results in paradoxical respiratory movements (detached portion is pulled inward during inspiration and pushed outward during expiration).
<br />

**连枷胸** 指连续节段性肋骨骨折导致胸壁不稳定，使部分胸壁在功能上与胸廓分离。连枷胸为急症，可引起反常呼吸运动（吸气时内陷，呼气时外突）。

**flexion** The bending of a limb or body part; opposite is extension.
<br />

**屈曲** 指肢体或身体部位的弯曲动作，其相反动作为伸展。

**fluoroscopy** A radiologic procedure using x-rays and a fluorescent screen to create real-time radiographic movies of anatomic structures. Fluoroscopy commonly is used to evaluate swallowing, GI motility, dynamic respiratory diseases, movements of the diaphragm, and for interventional studies. Patient and nearby personnel are exposed to relatively high levels of radiation. Modern fluoroscopy systems allow images to be viewed via electronic intensification, which enhances visibility and decreases exposure.
<br />

**透视检查** 一种利用 X 射线和荧光屏获取解剖结构实时动态影像的放射学检查方法，常用于评估吞咽、胃肠动力、动态呼吸系统疾病、膈肌运动及介入操作。患者及周围人员会暴露于相对较高的辐射剂量；现代透视系统通过电子增强显示影像，从而提高可见性并降低辐射暴露。

**fluid level** The interface between fluid and gas, demonstrated by using a horizontal x-ray beam (e.g., standing lateral radiograph, erect ventrodorsal radiograph). Gas must be present to document the fluid level (need to see gas-fluid interface).
<br />

**液平面** 指液体与气体之间的界面，通过水平射线束显示（如站立位侧位片或直立腹背位片）。需存在气体方可显示液气界面。

**focal spot** The area on the anode target of an x-ray tube that is struck by electrons and from which x-rays are emitted.
<br />

**焦点** X 射线管阳极靶面上被电子轰击并发射 X 射线的区域。

**focal film distance (FFD)** The distance from the focal spot to the film or image receptor. Other names: source-to-image distance (SID), focal-receptor distance (FRD).
<br />

**焦片距（FFD）** 指焦点至胶片或影像接收器之间的距离，亦称源-像距（SID）或焦-接收器距（FRD）。

**fogging** Refers to the nondiagnostic blackening (or graying) of a film radiograph. May be caused by scatter radiation, visible light, pressure, heat, or chemicals.
<br />

**雾化** 指胶片影像出现无诊断意义的变黑或变灰，可由散射线、可见光、压力、热量或化学因素引起。

**foreign material** Any object or matter that is not normal to the place where it is found. Sometimes the term *foreign body* is used; however, a *body* is the physical structure of an organism.
<br />

**异物** 指在某一部位出现的非正常物质或物体。有时亦称“异物体”，但“体”通常指生物体的物理结构。

**fracture** A break or discontinuity in bone caused by a physical force that exceeds the structural capacity of the bone or by a disease that weakens the bone.
<br />

**骨折** 指因超过骨结构承受能力的物理作用力或因疾病削弱骨强度而导致的骨连续性中断。

- *articular fracture:* A break in a bone that extends to a joint surface.
<br />

*关节内骨折：* 骨折线延伸至关节面。
- *avulsion fracture:* A break in a bone that occurs at an attachment site (e.g., ligament, tendon) and results in a piece of bone being pulled away from the main bone.
<br />

*撕脱性骨折：* 发生于韧带或肌腱等附着处，骨片被牵拉脱离主体骨。
- *chip fracture:* A corner or edge of a bone is broken.
<r>

*骨片骨折：* 骨的角或边缘发生断裂。

</r>
- *closed fracture:* The overlying skin and soft tissues are intact.
<br />

*闭合性骨折：* 覆盖其上的皮肤及软组织完整。
- *comminuted fracture:* Multiple breaks in a bone that communicate at a single point; results in three or more fragments.
<br />

*粉碎性骨折：* 多条骨折线在一点相交，形成三块或以上骨片。
- *complete fracture:* A break in a bone that extends through the entire bone.
<br />

*完全性骨折：* 骨折线贯穿整个骨骼。
- *compression fracture:* The ends of the bone fragments are impacted into each other, resulting in a shortened or collapsed bone.
<br />

*压缩性骨折：* 骨折端相互嵌入，导致骨缩短或塌陷。
- *depression fracture:* A fragment of a broken bone is located below the normal bone surface.
<br />

*凹陷性骨折：* 骨折片位于正常骨表面以下。
- *fissure fracture:* An incomplete break in a bone that extends from another fracture.
<br />

*裂隙骨折：* 自其他骨折延伸出的不完全性骨折。
- *folding fracture (torus or buckling fracture):* The cortex is broken on the concave side.
<br />

*折叠性骨折（屈曲骨折）：* 皮质骨在凹侧发生断裂。
- *greenstick fracture (hairline fracture):* An incomplete linear break on the convex side of a bone.
<br />

*青枝样骨折（毛发状骨折）：* 骨凸侧发生的不完全线性骨折。
- *incomplete fracture:* A partial break in a bone; the bone is not in complete discontinuity; appears in radiographs as a break in only one side of the cortex.
<br />

*不完全性骨折：* 骨未完全断裂，影像上仅见单侧皮质中断。
- *open fracture:* The overlying skin and soft tissues are perforated.
<br />

*开放性骨折：* 覆盖皮肤及软组织被穿破。
- *physeal fracture:* A break in bone that involves a physis (occurs in immature animals).
<br />

*生长板骨折：* 累及骺板的骨折（见于未成熟动物）。
- *segmental fracture:* Multiple breaks in a long bone that do not communicate; results in an isolated segment or fragment.
<br />

*节段性骨折：* 长骨多处骨折且彼此不相通，形成孤立骨段。
- *simple fracture:* A single break in a bone, resulting in two fragments.
<br />

*单纯性骨折：* 单一骨折线，形成两个骨片。
- *stress fracture (fatigue fracture):* A break in a bone caused by damage from repetitive cycling.
<br />

*应力性骨折（疲劳性骨折）：* 反复应力作用导致的骨折。
- *pathologic fracture:* A break in a bone that has been weakened by underlying disease or a developmental defect.
<br />

*病理性骨折：* 由于潜在疾病或发育缺陷削弱骨强度而发生的骨折。
- *shearing fracture (abrasion fracture):* An open break or loss of bone secondary to severe friction or glancing trauma.
<br />

*剪切性骨折（擦伤性骨折）：* 严重摩擦或掠过性外力造成的开放性骨折或骨缺损。
- *slab fracture:* The edge of a bone is broken and the fracture line extends from one articular surface to the other.
<br />

*板状骨折：* 骨缘断裂，骨折线从一个关节面延伸至另一个关节面。

## `G`

**gas-producing bacteria** Organisms include *E. coli, Proteus, Aerobacter*; animals with diabetes mellitus are predisposed infection and emphysematous cystitis due to glucosuria.
<br />

**产气菌** 包括 *大肠杆菌、变形杆菌、产气杆菌* 等；患有糖尿病的动物因尿糖而易发生感染及气肿性膀胱炎。

**gastric dilatation and volvulus (GDV)** A potentially life threatening, emergency situation that occurs when the stomach rotates around its axis (predominately its longitudinal or mesenteric axis). Rotation displaces the pylorus dorsally and to the left, which results in the duodenum wrapping around the esophagus. Gastric volvulus most often occurs in the clockwise direction (viewing the patient from caudal to cranial). Rotation greater than 180° twists the gastroesophageal junction to prevent eructation and obstructs the pyloric outflow. Gastric dilation can precede or follow gastric volvulus. Transient, intermittent volvulus can occur without dilation. Clinical signs of GDV include a distended and painful abdomen, non-productive retching, hypersalivation, dyspnea, and shock.
<br />

**胃扩张-扭转综合征（GDV）** 一种潜在致命的急症，当胃围绕自身轴线（主要为纵轴或肠系膜轴）发生旋转时出现。旋转使幽门向背侧及左侧移位，导致十二指肠绕过食管。胃扭转多为顺时针方向（自尾向头观察）。当旋转超过 180° 时，胃食管交界处被扭曲，无法嗳气并阻塞幽门排出。胃扩张可发生于胃扭转之前或之后；亦可出现无扩张的短暂或间歇性扭转。临床表现包括腹部膨胀且疼痛、无效呕吐、流涎、呼吸困难及休克。

**gastric ulcer** Open sores or holes in the lining of stomach. Gastric ulcers vary from small, superficial erosions to deep, indurated depressions in the gastric mucosa. They often are difficult to see in radiographs. To be reliably diagnosed, the radiographic signs of an ulcer must persist in multiple images. When viewed from the side (x-ray beam is tangential to the ulcer), an ulcer typically appears as a barium-filled outpouching that extends away from the stomach lumen. Larger, more chronic ulcers often are surrounded by thickened mucosa, which may project into the stomach lumen. The edges of an ulcer may taper smoothly, appear irregular, or they may be shaped like a square, the latter similar in appearance to pseudoulcers in the duodenum. When viewed *en face* (the x-ray beam is perpendicular to the ulcer), an ulcer may resemble a bullseye target (⊙). This is because barium fills the central crater in the ulcer and the surrounding thickened wall creates a circular filling defect. A double-contrast gastrogram tends to be more sensitive than barium alone for detecting gastric ulcers. A barium-filled stomach can mask small mucosal lesions.
<br />

**胃溃疡** 指胃黏膜表面的开放性溃疡或穿孔，范围可从浅表糜烂到深部硬化性凹陷。胃溃疡在放射影像中常较难观察，为可靠诊断，溃疡征象需在多张影像中持续存在。侧位观察（射线与溃疡切线方向）时，溃疡常表现为充钡的囊样突出，向胃腔外延伸。较大或慢性溃疡常被增厚的黏膜包绕，并可向胃腔内突起。溃疡边缘可呈平滑渐细、不规则，或呈方形，后者与十二指肠假性溃疡相似。正面观察（射线垂直于溃疡）时，溃疡可呈靶征（⊙），因钡剂充填中央溃疡凹陷，而周围增厚的壁形成环形充盈缺损。双重对比胃造影较单纯钡剂更敏感；充满钡剂的胃腔可掩盖小的黏膜病变。

**gastrography** A radiographic contrast study of the stomach. Gastrogram.
<br />

**胃部造影** 对胃进行的放射学造影检查。

**geographic osteolysis** A relatively large area of bone loss.
<br />

**地图样骨溶解** 指范围相对较大的骨质缺损区。

**gout** Disease caused by defective metabolism of uric acid. Rare in dogs, most often reported in Dalmatians. High levels of uric acid may lead to formation of cystic calculi and mineral deposits in joints.
<br />

**痛风** 由尿酸代谢异常引起的疾病，在犬中较罕见，最常见于斑点犬。高尿酸水平可导致膀胱结石形成及关节内矿物沉积。

**gram** Years ago, radiographs were also called radiograms. Currently, *gram* is used to describe contrast studies (e.g., esophagram, cystogram); for example, cystography is performed to produce a cystogram.
<br />

**造影片** 早期放射影像亦称为 radiogram；目前 *gram* 常用于指对比造影检查所得影像（如食管造影片、膀胱造影片）。

**granuloma** A mass or nodule of chronically inflamed tissue with granulations (small, rounded masses of tissue that form during healing), usually associated with an infectious process.
<br />

**肉芽肿** 由慢性炎症组织及肉芽组织构成的肿块或结节，通常与感染过程相关。

**graph** In imaging terminology, this term refers to an image. Practically the term has come to mean a survey image ("plain" or non-contrast image): a radiograph.
<br />

**平片（graph）** 在成像术语中指图像；实际使用中常指平片（无对比影像），即放射片。

**gravel sign** The sedimentation and accumulation of tiny, sand-like mineral opacity objects immediately proximal to the site of a gastric or intestinal obstruction. Stasis allows heavier particles to precipitate.
<br />

**砂砾征** 指在胃或肠道阻塞部位近端，可见细小砂样矿物密度物沉积聚集，因内容物停滞使较重颗粒沉淀。

**grid** A device designed to block scatter x-rays from reaching the image receptor while allowing transmitted x-rays to pass through. Grids consist of alternate strips of an x-ray absorbing material (usually lead) interspaced with a relatively non-absorbing material such as carbon fiber or aluminum. The grid is positioned between the subject (patient) and the image receptor.
<br />

**滤线栅** 用于阻挡散射 X 射线进入影像接收器，同时允许透射 X 射线通过的装置。滤线栅由交替排列的吸收射线材料（通常为铅）与相对不吸收材料（如碳纤维或铝）组成，置于受检体与影像接收器之间。

**grid cutoff** A radiographic artifact caused by incorrect use of a grid that results in unwanted absorption of transmitted x-rays, most often seen with parallel grids.
<br />

**滤线栅伪影** 因滤线栅使用不当导致透射 X 射线被异常吸收而产生的放射伪影，多见于平行滤线栅。

**grid factor** The amount of transmitted x-rays absorbed by the grid. A grid factor may be 2, 3, or 4 meaning that the exposure must be increased 2, 3, or 4 times compared to the exposure used with no grid. This calls for more output from the x-ray tube and delivers more exposure to the patient. If the grid factor is 2, then the grid would block 1/2 of the primary rays. Also called "bucky factor" or "dose penalty."
<br />

**滤线栅因子** 指滤线栅吸收透射 X 射线的比例。因子可为 2、3 或 4，表示相较于不使用滤线栅时，曝光量需增加 2、3 或 4 倍，从而要求 X 射线管输出更高并使患者接受更多辐射。若滤线栅因子为 2，则约阻挡一半主射线；亦称“bucky因子”或“剂量惩罚”。

**grid lines** A series of parallel, less opaque (white) lines across the entire radiograph caused by the undesirable absorption of transmitted x-rays by the lead strips in a grid; a negative density artifact.
<br />

**滤线栅线** 由滤线栅内铅条对透射 X 射线的不良吸收造成的整幅影像中平行浅色线条，为一种负密度伪影。

**grid ratio** The ratio of the height of the lead strips to the distance between them. In most grids, the strip height is 2–5 mm and the distance between strips is 0.25–0.4 mm. Grid ratios range from 5:1 to 16:1.
<br />

**滤线栅比** 指铅条高度与铅条间距之比。多数滤线栅中铅条高度为 2–5 mm，间距为 0.25–0.4 mm，栅比范围为 5:1 至 16:1。

**ground-glass appearance** A homogenous, hazy, or somewhat mottled appearing structure that resembles looking at it through semi-opaque glass or "bathroom glass."
<br />

**磨玻璃样表现** 指结构呈均匀、模糊或轻度斑驳外观，类似透过半透明磨砂玻璃观察。

**growth arrest lines** Thin, well-defined, horizontal lines of increased opacity in a long bone diaphysis (most common in femur). May be caused by dietary changes or systemic illness during growth.
<br />

**生长停滞线** 长骨骨干内细薄、清晰的水平高密度线（以股骨最常见），可由生长期间的营养改变或全身性疾病引起。

## `H`

**heel effect** Refers to the variation in intensity of the x-ray beam between cathode and anode sides of the x-ray tube. Because the target is angled, the bottom or “heel” of the anode focal spot absorbs some of the x-rays. Absorption on the anode side results in more x-rays being emitted from the cathode side of the x-ray tube. Therefore, the thicker part of the subject should be positioned on the cathode side to take advantage of the heel effect. The heel effects occurs because x-rays originate not only from the surface of the anode target but also from deep inside. X-rays produced inside the target have to travel back out to join the x-ray beam, and some of these will be attenuated. Some of the x-rays that have to pass through the thicker heel part of the anode will be absorbed, which means fewer x-rays will be emitted from the anode side of the x-ray tube than from the cathode side. The difference between the two sides can be as much as 40%.
<br />

**阳极效应** 指X射线管阴极侧与阳极侧之间X射线束强度的差异。由于靶板倾斜，X射线束底部或"跟部"区域的曝光强度较弱。阳极焦点会吸收部分X射线。阳极侧的吸收导致X射线管阴极侧释放更多X射线。因此，被检物较厚的部位应置于阴极侧以利用跟部效应。该效应的产生源于X射线不仅来自阳极靶表面，也来自其深层内部。 靶材内部产生的X射线需折返汇入射束，其中部分会发生衰减。穿过阳极较厚跟部区域的X射线会被吸收，这意味着X射线管阳极侧的射线输出量将少于阴极侧，两侧差异可达40%。

**hematoma** A localized accumulation of blood within tissues outside of the vessels. Other names: bruise, contusion.
<br />

**血肿** 指血管外组织中血液的局部积聚。别称：瘀伤、挫伤。

**hemimelia** Congenital partial or complete absence of a normally paired bone (e.g., radius/ulna, tibia/fibula).
<br />

**半肢畸形** 指指正常成对骨骼（如桡骨/尺骨、胫骨/腓骨）先天性部分或完全缺失。

**hemivertebra** Congenital defect resulting in only partial development of a vertebral body.
<br />

**半椎体** 导致椎体发育不全的先天性缺陷。

**hemothorax** Accumulation of blood within the pleural space.
<br />

**血胸** 胸膜腔内血液积聚。

**hernia** Abnormal protrusion of an organ or portion of an organ through an opening (commonly in a structure that normally contains it).
<br />

**疝** 器官或器官部分通过开口异常突出（通常发生在正常情况下容纳该器官的结构中）。

**herringbone pattern** In an esophagram of a cat, this is the appearance of the distal esophagus created by the transverse or oblique mucosal folds in the distal third of the feline esophagus.
<br />

**鲱鱼骨征** 在猫的食管造影中，这是由猫食管远端三分之一处横向或斜向黏膜褶皱形成的远端食管外观。

**heterotopic bone formation** Focal ossification in an abnormal location; typically refers to small (less than 3 mm), well-defined and slightly irregular-shaped mineral opacity structures in the lung, which also are called pulmonary *osteomas or pulmonary microlithiasis*.
<br />

**异位骨形成** 指异常部位的局灶性骨化；通常特指肺部出现的小型（小于3毫米）、界限清晰且略呈不规则形状的矿化密度结构，亦称肺骨瘤或肺微结石症。

**hiatus/hiatal** An opening, gap, or cleft; term usually is associated with the diaphragm (e.g., hiatal hernia).
<br />

**裂隙/裂口** 指孔隙、间隙或裂缝；该术语通常与膈肌相关（如膈疝）。

**high-frequency x-ray machine** Type of equipment in which the electric current to the x-ray tube is manipulated to produce nearly constant potential voltage. Constant voltage results in a more uniform production of x-rays with an average energy closer to the selected kVp. With standard x-ray machines (fully rectified, single phase), the voltage rises to peak and falls to zero 120 times per second, which produces an average energy of the x-ray beam equal to only about two-thirds that of the kVp selected on the control panel.
<br />

**高频X射线机**此类设备通过调节流向X射线管的电流，产生近乎恒定的电位电压。 恒定电压可使X射线输出更均匀，其平均能量更接近选定的kVp值。标准X射线机（全整流单相）的电压每秒升降120次，导致X射线束的平均能量仅为控制面板设定kVp值的三分之二左右。

**HIS (hospital information system)** Term to include all aspects of hospital information (e.g., administration, financial, clinical).
<br />

**HIS（医院信息系统）** 涵盖医院信息所有层面的术语（如行政、财务、临床）。

**horizontal beam radiography** Technique in which the x-ray beam is directed horizontally (i.e., across the x-ray table) to make a radiograph. Animal typically is standing or in lateral recumbency. Technique may be used to document presence of free fluid or gas in a body space, to reposition fluid to better visualize obscured structures, or when minimal movement of the patient is required.
<br />

**水平束放射摄影术** 指X射线束水平投射（即横跨X射线检查台）进行影像拍摄的技术。动物通常采取站立或侧卧位。该技术适用于记录体腔内游离液体或气体存在、重新定位液体以清晰显示被遮挡结构，或需患者最小活动量的情形。

**hydrocephalus** An excessive accumulation of fluid in the ventricular system of the brain. Obstructive or noncommunicating hydrocephalus is the more common form. It is caused by a ventricular obstruction, which can result from a congenital abnormality, trauma, infection, parasite migration, or neoplasia. Nonobstructive or communicating hydrocephalus is less common and is caused by failure of arachnoid villi to absorb cerebral spinal fluid at an adequate rate. Congenital hydrocephalus is more often reported in small, toy, and brachycephalic dogs (e.g., Yorkshire Terrier, Chihuahua, Maltese, Toy Poodle, Boston Terrier, English Bulldog, Pekingese, Lhasa Apso, Pug, Pomeranian, Cairn Terrier). In cats, hydrocephalus is less common than in dogs (may be hereditary in Siamese cats), and it may be associated with feline infectious peritonitis. Clinical signs of hydrocephalus include an enlarged, dome-shaped cranium with open fontanelles; visual and auditory deficits; vocalization; and an abnormal gait.
<br />

**脑积水** 脑室系统内液体过度积聚。 梗阻性或非交通性脑积水更为常见，由脑室阻塞引起，可能源于先天性异常、创伤、感染、寄生虫迁移或肿瘤。非梗阻性或交通性脑积水较为罕见，由蛛网膜绒毛无法以足够速度吸收脑脊液所致。 先天性脑积水多见于小型犬、玩具犬及短头犬种（如约克夏梗、吉娃娃、马耳他犬、玩具贵宾犬、波士顿梗、英国斗牛犬、北京犬、拉萨犬、巴哥犬、博美犬、凯恩梗）。 猫的脑积水发病率低于犬（暹罗猫可能具有遗传性），且可能与猫传染性腹膜炎相关。脑积水的临床表现包括：头颅呈穹顶状增大且囟门未闭合；视听功能障碍；异常叫声；步态异常。

**hydrocolpos** Cystic dilation and accumulation of fluid in the vagina with expansion of the vaginal lumen.
<br />

**阴道囊肿** 阴道腔扩张伴囊性扩张及液体积聚。

**hydronephrosis** Dilation of the renal collecting system secondary to a congenital or acquired obstruction to urine outflow. Clinical signs vary with the degree and duration of the obstruction. Acute obstruction often is very painful, and the kidneys may appear normal in radiographs during early/mild obstruction. Chronic partial obstructions may be relatively asymptomatic until the animal is in renal failure. Progressive hydronephrosis leads to unilateral or bilateral renomegaly, usually with smooth margins. Complete obstruction can destroy kidney function within a few days. Telangiectasia can lead to a congenital urinary outflow problem, which may be evident during the vascular phase of an excretory urogram as anomalous vasculature.
<br />

**肾积水**因先天性或后天性尿液流出道梗阻导致的肾集合系统扩张。临床症状随梗阻程度及持续时间而异。急性梗阻常伴剧烈疼痛，早期或轻度梗阻时肾脏在X光片中可能表现正常。 慢性部分梗阻可能在动物出现肾功能衰竭前相对无症状。进行性积水导致单侧或双侧肾脏肿大，边缘通常光滑。完全梗阻可在数日内破坏肾功能。毛细血管扩张可导致先天性尿液流出障碍，在排泄性尿路造影的血管显影期可能表现为异常血管结构。

**hydroureter** Dilation of the ureter, most often due to obstruction. Caudal abdominal compression is used to deliberately dilate the renal collecting system to enhance filling and opacification.
<br />

**输尿管扩张** 输尿管扩张，多由梗阻引起。尾侧腹部压迫法可通过刻意扩张肾集合系统增强造影剂充盈与显影效果。

**hyperostosis** Widening of cortical bone due to appositional growth of osseous tissue at endosteum or periosteum.
<br />

**骨质增生** 因骨内膜或骨膜处骨组织侧向生长导致皮质骨变宽。

**hyperparathyroidism** A systemic condition in which excessive production of parathyroid hormone by overactive parathyroid glands leads to abnormal loss of mineral from bone. May be primary caused by parathyroid gland disease (e.g., adenoma, carcinoma, adenomatous hyperplasia) or secondary due to nutritional deficiency or chronic renal disease.
<br />

**甲状旁腺功能亢进症**一种全身性疾病，因甲状旁腺功能亢进导致甲状旁腺激素分泌过量，进而引发骨矿物质异常流失。可由原发性甲状旁腺疾病（如腺瘤、癌、腺瘤性增生）引起，亦可继发于营养缺乏或慢性肾病。

**hypertrophic osteodystrophy (HOD)** A systemic disease of unknown etiology that results in subperiosteal hemorrhage and transient defects in metaphyseal mineralization. Appears in radiographs as line of decreased opacity parallel to physis and mineralization along the metaphysis. May be associated with canine distemper.
<br />

**肥大性骨营养不良（HOD）** 一种病因不明的全身性疾病，表现为骨膜下出血及骨骺端矿化暂时性缺陷。X光片可见与双生长板征及骨骺端矿化异常。可能与犬瘟热相关。

**hypertrophic osteopathy (HO)** A periosteal response that occurs secondary to a mass-effect in the thoracic or abdominal cavity. HO is also called secondary hypertrophic pulmonary osteopathy or Marie’s disease, the latter named for the French pathologist who in 1890 first associated abnormal bone growth with lung disease. It most often is reported in middle-aged to older dogs, but can occur in any age, breed, or gender. The most common cause of HO is intra-thoracic disease (e.g., large lung tumors, infection, severe cardiomegaly), but it can result from certain intraabdominal infections, tumors, and large masses. The mechanism is unknown, but it likely is associated with periosteal blood flow.
<br />

 **肥大性骨病（HO）** 是一种继发于胸腔或腹腔内肿块效应的骨膜反应。该病亦称继发性肥大性肺骨病或玛丽病，后者得名于1890年首次将异常骨生长与肺部疾病关联起来的法国病理学家。该病多见于中老年犬，但任何年龄、品种或性别的犬只均可能发病。 该病最常见诱因是胸腔内疾病（如大型肺肿瘤、感染、严重心脏肥大），亦可由特定腹腔感染、肿瘤及大型肿块引发。其发病机制尚不明确，但可能与骨膜血流相关。

**hydrothorax** Accumulation of fluid within the pleural space.
<br />

**胸腔积液** 指胸膜腔内液体异常积聚。

## `I`

**iatrogenic** Pathology caused by medical examination or treatment.
<br />

**医源性** 由医疗检查或治疗引发的病理变化。

**idiopathic** Of unknown etiology; occurring without known cause.
<br />

**特发性** 病因不明；无已知诱因的发生。

**ileus** Partial or complete obstruction due to hypomotility or physical blockage. May be associated with intestine, esophagus, ureter, or bile duct.
<br />

**肠梗阻** 因肠道运动功能减弱或物理阻塞导致的部分或完全性阻塞。可发生于肠道、食管、输尿管或胆管。

**ill-defined** The margin of a structure is not well visualized. A simple test is whether or not you can easily trace the edges of the structure with a pencil or stylus. When used to describe bone lesions, ill-defined osteolysis is more aggressive; an ill-defined periosteal response is more active. Ill-defined is not the same as irregular. Well-defined is not the same as smooth.
<br />

**界限不清** 结构边缘未清晰显影。简单测试方法是能否用铅笔或探针轻松描摹结构边缘。用于描述骨病变时，界限不清的骨溶解更具侵袭性；界限不清的骨膜反应更活跃。界限不清不等同于不规则，界限清晰不等同于光滑。

**image detector** See **image receptor**.
<br />

**图像探测器** 见图像接收器。

**image receptor** The part of an imaging system that captures the image. An imaging receptor may be a film (conventional radiography) or a phosphor plate (digital radiography). X-rays that pass through the patient to the image receptor form the latent image. Other names: detector, image detector.
<br />

**影像接收器** 成像系统中捕捉图像的部件。可为胶片（传统放射摄影）或荧光板（数字放射摄影）。X射线穿透患者后在成像接收器上形成潜影。其他名称：探测器、图像探测器。

**indistinct** See ill-defined, definition, distinction.
<br />

**模糊不清** 参见界限不清、清晰度、区分度。

**infiltrate** Accumulation of abnormal cells in a tissue; typically describes diffuse lesions in bone or lung.
<br />

**浸润** 指异常细胞在组织中的聚集；通常描述骨骼或肺部的弥漫性病变。

**intensifying screen** A plastic sheet coated with rare earth phosphorous and used to convert x-rays to visible light. Intensifying screens are mounted inside a light-proof cassette, and a piece of x-ray film is placed between two screens in the cassette. Purpose of intensifying screens is to reduce x-ray exposure by enhancing the effect of x-rays on film.
<br />

**显像屏** 涂覆稀土磷的塑料薄片，用于将X射线转化为可见光。显像屏置于防光盒内，X射线胶片则夹在盒内两片显像屏之间。其作用是增强X射线对胶片的作用效果，从而降低X射线照射剂量。

**intensity of the x-ray beam** The number of x-rays arriving per unit time over a specific area. Beam intensity is controlled by the quality and quantity of x-rays, the size of the field-of-view (amount of collimation), and the distance from the focal spot.
<br />

**X射线束强度** 单位时间内到达特定区域的X射线数量。束强度受X射线质量与数量、视野大小（准直程度）及焦点距离共同调控。

**interlobar fissure line** See **pleural fissure line**.
<br />

**叶间裂隙线** 见胸膜裂隙线

**International Elbow Working Group** Formed in 1989; the IEWG established a grading scale to help classify elbow dysplasia. The severity of the disease is based on the size (thickness) of new bone on the proximal, non-articular border of the anconeal process:

1. Grade 0 (normal): no evidence of new bone production; smooth, well-defined anconeal margin.
2. Grade 1 (mild): anconeal new bone is less than 3 mm thick.
3. Grade 2 (moderate): anconeal new bone is 3–5 mm thick, subchondral osteosclerosis may be present along with the ulnar trochlear notch.
4. Grade 3 (severe): anconeal new bone is greater than 5 mm thick, osteophytes and enthesophytes often are present on the distal humerus and proximal radius.
<br />

 **国际肘关节工作组（IEWG）** 成立于1989年，该组织制定了分级标准以辅助肘关节发育不良的分类。疾病严重程度依据肱骨外上髁近端非关节缘新骨形成厚度判定：
5. 0级（正常）：无新骨形成迹象；肱骨外上髁边缘光滑、界限清晰。
6. 1级（轻度）：鹰嘴突新骨厚度小于3毫米。
7. 2级（中度）：鹰嘴突新骨厚度为3-5毫米，可能伴有桡骨滑车切迹处的软骨下骨硬化。
8. 3级（重度）：鹰嘴突新骨厚度大于5毫米；肱骨远端和桡骨近端常可见骨赘和附着点矿化形成。

**interstitial pulmonary pattern** A radiographic appearance caused by increased amount of tissue, fluid, or cellular material in the non-air-containing lung parenchyma. Increased opacity is within the lung but not involving vessels, bronchi, or alveoli. This is the least specific lung pattern and a frequently overused term. Rarely is a true interstitial lung pattern visible in radiographs without some degree of alveolar filling.
<br />

**间质性肺纹理** 由肺实质内组织、液体或细胞物质增多导致的放射影像表现。肺部出现密度增高区域，但未累及血管、支气管或肺泡。此为特异性最低的肺纹理表现，且常被过度使用。放射影像中罕见纯粹间质性肺纹理，通常伴有不同程度的肺泡充盈。

**intervertebral disc disease (IVDD)** Protrusion or extrusion of disc material into the spinal canal which compress the spinal cord or nerve roots and leads to neurological symptoms. IVDD can occur in any disc and most often results from degeneration of the disc. Dehydration of nucleus pulposus leads to replacement of gelatinous material with cartilage or fibrous tissue, resulting in loss of disc pliability. The nucleus may mineralize, resulting in further loss of compressibility. The annulus fibrosis also degenerates and weakens, allowing disc material to protrude or extrude into spinal canal. The dorsal part of the annulus is the thinnest. Disc material may be gelatinous, fibrous, or mineralized and can damage the spinal cord.
• **Hansen type I disc herniation** Complete extrusion of nucleus pulposus into spinal canal; an acute condition that tends to be clinically more severe than type II and is more common in chondrodystrophic breeds.
• **Hansen type II disc herniation** Protrusion (or bulging) of disc into spinal canal without rupture of annulus; often a more gradual and progressive disease process; more common in older non-chondrodystrophic breeds; also associated with cervical spondylopathy and lumbosacral instability.
<br />

**椎间盘疾病（IVDD）** 指椎间盘物质向椎管内突出或脱出，压迫脊髓或神经根导致神经症状。任何椎间盘均可能发生IVDD，最常见于椎间盘退变。髓核脱水导致凝胶状物质被软骨或纤维组织替代，致使椎间盘失去柔韧性。髓核可能发生矿化，导致压缩性进一步丧失。纤维环同样退化变弱，使椎间盘物质得以向椎管内突出或脱出。纤维环背侧部分最为薄弱。椎间盘物质可呈胶质状、纤维状或矿化状，均可能损伤脊髓。

- **Hansen I型椎间盘突出** 髓核完全脱出至椎管内；属于急性病变，临床表现通常较II型更严重，在软骨发育不良犬种中更为常见。
- **Hansen II型椎间盘突出** 椎间盘向椎管内突出（或膨出）但纤维环未破裂；通常为渐进性病程；常见于老年非软骨发育不良犬种；亦与颈椎病及腰骶椎不稳定相关。

**intramembranous ossification** Refers to the formation of bone in sheets of connective tissue (e.g., bones of the skull).
<br />

**膜内骨化** 指在结缔组织片层中形成骨组织（如颅骨）。

**intravenous urography (IVU)** See excretory urography.
<br />

**静脉尿路造影（IVU）** 参见排泄性尿路造影。

**intussusception** A segment of intestine slides or “telescopes” into the lumen of an adjacent segment leading to a partial or complete bowel obstruction. The invaginated segment is called the intussusceptum and it is displaced into the receiving segment which is called the intussuscipiens. Intussusceptions occur most often either in the jejunum or at the ileocolic junction. An ileocolic intussusception may prevent gas from entering the cecum and ascending colon.
<br />

**肠套叠** 指肠管某段滑入相邻肠段腔内形成部分或完全性肠梗阻。套入段称为套叠部，被套入的肠段称为受套部。此病变多发于空肠或回结肠连接处。 回盲部肠套叠可能阻碍气体进入盲肠及升结肠。

**iodinated contrast media** Positive contrast agents that contain iodine (atomic number 53). Non-ionic agents are preferred. Ionic agents are hyperosmolar (hypertonic), rapidly absorbed, quickly diluted, and can be irritating to tissues.
<br />

**碘造影剂** 含碘（原子序数53）的阳性造影剂。优先选用非离子型制剂。离子型制剂具有高渗性（高浓度），吸收迅速，易被稀释，且可能刺激组织。

**ionization** Process by which an atom acquires a negative or positive charge by losing or gaining electrons; x-rays contain sufficient energy to cause ionization and can alter molecules, break chemical bonds, and damage or destroy living cells.
<br />

**电离** 指原子通过失去或获得电子而获得负电荷或正电荷的过程；X射线具有足够能量引发电离反应，可改变分子结构、破坏化学键，并损伤或摧毁活体细胞。

**ipsilateral** On or pertaining to the same side.
<br />

**同侧的** 指位于或涉及同侧的。

**irregular** A descriptive term that refers only to the shape of a structure or lesion. The term *irregular* should not be confused with *ill-defined*. *Irregular* does not indicate cause, activity, or aggressiveness of a lesion.
<br />

**不规则性** 仅描述结构或病变形态的术语。该术语不应与"界限不清"混淆，不规则性不涉及病变的成因、活动性或侵袭性。

## `K`

**kVp (kilovolt peak)** The maximum voltage applied across the x-ray tube. Determines the kinetic energy of the electrons and therefore, the highest energy in the x-ray beam. Determines the quality and penetrating ability of the x-ray beam. Influences the radiographic contrast (degree of difference between black and white in a radiograph). 1 kilovolt = 1000 volts.
<br />

**kVp（千伏峰值）** X射线管上施加的最大电压。它决定了电子的动能，从而决定了X射线束中的最高能量。决定了X射线束的质量和穿透能力。影响放射对比度（X光片上黑白差异程度）。1千伏 = 1000伏特。

**kyphosis** Dorsal curvature of the spine.
<br />

**脊柱背凸/驼背** 脊柱向背侧异常弯曲的状态。

## `L`

**labial** On the side that faces the lips; used to describe lesions associated with the mouth.
<br />

**唇侧** 指朝向嘴唇的一侧；用于描述与口腔相关的病变。

**lateral** Toward the side; away from the midline plane. Opposite is **medial**.
<br />

**外侧** 远离中线平面。相反概念为内侧。

**lateral recumbency** Animal is positioned lying on its side.
<br />

**侧卧位** 动物呈侧卧姿势。

**lateral radiograph** The x-ray beam enters the right or left side of a body part and exits the opposite side.
<br />

**侧位X光片** X射线束从身体部位的右侧或左侧进入，从对侧射出。

**lesion** A detected radiographic abnormality. **levocardia** The normal left side position of the heart. **lingual** On the side that faces the tongue.
<br />

**病变** 指X光片上检测到的异常病灶。左位心 心脏位于正常左侧位置。舌侧 指朝向舌头的侧面。

**lordosis** Ventral curvature of the spine.
<br />

**脊柱腹凸** 脊柱向腹侧弯曲。

**lumbophrenic angle** Junction of the diaphragm and lumbar spine at the dorsocaudal aspect of the thoracic cavity on a lateral radiograph.
<br />

**腰膈角**在侧位X光片上，位于胸腔背侧尾端处，膈肌与腰椎的交界处。

**lumbosacral instability** Abnormal movement between last lumbar and first sacral vertebrae due to a congenital or acquired disorder. Radiographically characterized by narrowing of the vertebral canal or intervertebral foramina (which causes compression of the nerve roots that form the cauda equina in the lumbosacral region). Other names: cauda equina syndrome, lumbosacral stenosis, lumbosacral malarticulation.
<br />

**腰骶不稳** 因先天或后天病变导致末节腰椎与首节骶椎间异常活动。 X线特征表现为椎管或椎间孔狭窄（导致腰骶部马尾神经根受压）。别名：**马尾综合征**、**腰骶管狭窄**、**腰骶关节错位**。

**lung collapse** Partial or complete loss of lung volume due to reduced size of the alveoli and loss of alveolar air. Lung collapse (atelectasis) may appear as alveolar filling and can only be distinguished due to decreased lung size. A mediatinal shift often is present, toward the smaller lung. Common causes include bronchial obstruction, prolonged recumbency.
<br />

**肺塌陷** 因肺泡体积缩小及肺泡气体丧失导致的肺容量部分或完全缺失。肺萎陷（肺不张）影像表现为肺泡充盈，仅能通过肺体积缩小进行鉴别。常伴有纵隔移位，向萎陷侧偏移。常见病因包括支气管阻塞、长期卧位。

**lung consolidation** Increased opacity in lung parenchyma without loss of lung volume. Consolidation is due to alveolar filling. Common causes include pneumonia, hemorrhage, neoplasia, and edema.
<br />

**肺实变** 肺实质密度增高但肺容积未减少。实变由肺泡充盈引起。常见病因包括肺炎、出血、肿瘤及水肿。

**lung markings** Refers to the radiographically visible bronchial walls and pulmonary vessels.
<br />

**肺纹理** 指X线影像中可见的支气管壁及肺血管纹理。

**luxation** Complete loss of contact between articular surfaces.
<br />

**脱位** 关节面之间完全失去接触。

**lymphadenopathy** Abnormal lymph node; term generally is used to describe lymph node enlargement (more correct term is *lymphadenomegaly*).
<br />

**淋巴结病变** 异常淋巴结；该术语通常用于描述淋巴结肿大（更准确的术语应为淋巴结增大）。

**lymphography (lymphangiography)** A radiographic contrast study of the lymphatic vessels.
<br />

**淋巴造影（淋巴管造影）** 淋巴管的放射性造影检查。

## `M`

**Mach band** An optical illusion in which a false black line or false white line occurs at the boundary between two different opacities. A negative Mach band (black line) occurs along the edge of a convex structure (e.g., outer margin of bone cortex). A positive Mach band (white line) occurs at the edge of a concave structure (e.g., acetabulum and under femoral head on a ventrodorsal pelvic radiograph), which may be mistaken for osteophytes (i.e., on the femoral neck). Mach bands cannot be eliminated by knowledge of their existence; the illusion occurs in the retina, and the brain actually sees it. Mach bands were described in 1865 by physicist Ernst Mach. Other names: Mach line, edge enhancement.
<br />

**马赫带** 一种光学错觉现象，表现为两种不同不透明度交界处出现虚假的黑线或白线。 负马赫带（黑线）出现在凸结构边缘（如骨皮质外缘）；正马赫带（白线）则见于凹结构边缘（如骨盆正位片中的髋臼及股骨头下方），可能被误认为骨赘（如股骨颈处）。 即使知晓其存在也无法消除马赫带；这种视觉错觉发生于视网膜，且大脑确实能感知到它。该现象由物理学家恩斯特·马赫于1865年首次描述。其他名称：马赫线、边缘增强效应。

**magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)** A diagnostic imaging procedure that creates cross-sectional images of a body part using powerful magnetic fields. The magnetic field temporarily alters the positions of protons in the patient. As these protons return to their normal positions, they emit radio waves that are detected by computers to create images with superior anatomic detail. The patient is not exposed to harmful radiation during an MRI procedure.
<br />

**磁共振成像（MRI）** 一种诊断成像技术，通过强磁场生成身体部位的横断面图像。磁场会暂时改变患者体内质子的位置。当这些质子恢复正常位置时，会释放出无线电波，经计算机检测后可生成具有卓越解剖细节的图像。MRI检查过程中患者不会接触有害辐射。

**magnification** Process of enlarging the radiographic appearance of a structure(s) by increasing the object to image receptor distance (OID). Using a small focal spot will aid in maintaining definition that is lost by the change in beam geometry.
<br />

**放大** 通过增加物体到成像接收器距离（OID）来增强放射影像中结构显示的放大过程。使用小焦斑有助于维持因射束几何变化而丢失的图像清晰度。

**malunion fracture** The bone has healed, but there is abnormal bone geometry (i.e., bone is abnormally shortened, malaligned, angled, or rotated).
<br />

**畸形愈合** 指骨折愈合后出现骨骼几何形态异常（如骨体异常缩短、错位、弯曲或旋转）。

**manus** Distal part of a thoracic limb; includes carpus, metacarpus, and digits.
<br />

**掌部** 胸肢远端部分；包含腕骨、掌骨及指骨。

**mAs (milliampere seconds)** Product of the strength of the current through the x-ray tube (milliamperes) and the length of time the current is on (seconds). Determines the number of electrons emitted and, therefore, the number of x-rays produced (quantity of x-ray beam). Influences radiographic density (degree of blackness in a radiograph).
<br />

**mAs（毫安秒）** X射线管电流强度（毫安）与通电时间（秒）的乘积。决定电子发射数量，进而决定X射线产生量（X射线束强度）。影响放射密度（X射线照片的黑色程度）。

**mass effect** Displacement of viscera or other structures away from their normal positions without distinct visualization of the borders of a mass.
<br />

**团块效应** 指内脏或其他结构偏离正常位置的移位现象，且肿块边界无法清晰显影。

**mass lesion** A solitary lesion larger than 3 cm in diameter.
<br />

**团块** 直径大于3厘米的单发性病变。

**median** Pertaining to the middle of the body.
<br />

**中线** 指身体中线位置。

**medial** Toward the midline of the body or on the inner (axial) surface of an extremity. Along the median. Opposite is **lateral**.
<br />

**内侧** 指朝向身体中线或四肢内侧（轴向）表面的方向。沿中线分布。相对词为外侧。

**mediastinal shift** Displacement of the heart and/or other mediastinal structures toward the right or left side. May be caused by alteration in lung volume, an intra-thoracic mass, unilateral fluid or gas in pleural space, or congenital defect (e.g., pectus excavatum).
<br />

**纵隔移位** 心脏和/或其他纵隔结构向右侧或左侧移位。可能由肺容量改变、胸腔内肿块、单侧胸膜腔积液或积气，或先天性缺陷（如漏斗胸）引起。

**meningocele** Neural defect in which the meninges protrude through a dorsal spinal defect.
<br />

**脊髓膜膨出** 脑膜通过脊柱背侧缺损突出形成的神经缺陷。

**meningomyelocele** Neural defect in which the spinal cord, meninges, or nerve roots protrude through a dorsal spinal defect.
<br />

**脊髓脊膜膨出** 脊髓、脑膜或神经根通过脊柱背侧缺损突出的神经缺陷。

**mesenchymal** Refers to cells that develop into connective tissue, blood vessels, and lymphatic tissue.
<br />

**间充质** 指可分化为结缔组织、血管及淋巴组织的细胞。

**metaphysis** "Next to the physis". The wider portion of a long bone between the diaphysis and the physis. The area in a long bone where growth cartilage is transformed into cortical bone.
<br />

**骨骺端** 位于骨骺旁。长骨骨干与骨骺之间的宽阔区域，此处生长软骨转化为皮质骨。

**metastatic calcification** The deposition of calcium salts in tissues away from a disease site, most often due to abnormal calcium/phosphorus metabolism.
<br />

**转移性钙化** 指远离病灶部位的组织中钙盐沉积，通常由钙磷代谢异常引起。

**microlithiasis** Focal areas of mineralization (less than 3 mm in size) in an organ, usually the lung. (Other names: heterotopic bone formation, pulmonary osteomas).
<br />

**微石症** 指器官（通常为肺部）内直径小于3毫米的局灶性矿化区域。（别名：**异位骨化**、肺骨瘤）。

**miliary nodules** Small in size, like a millet seed (1-2 mm). Other names: micronodules.
<br />

**粟粒样结节** 小于1-2毫米的微小结节，形似小米粒。别称：微结节。

**mineralization** The abnormal bioprecipitation of inorganic substances (such as calcium) in organic tissue; includes petrification and fossilization. *Calcification* is the normal or abnormal deposition of calcium salts. A type of normal calcification is ossification, which is the deposition of calcium to create new bone. Calcification occurs during ossification, but the reverse is not true. *Heterotopic ossification* produces extra-skeletal bone, which is bone in soft tissues where bone normally does not exist. *Petrification* and *fossilization* are additional types of mineralization.
<br />

**矿化作用**：有机组织中无机物质（如钙）的异常生物沉淀；包括石化和化石化。 钙化是指钙盐的正常或异常沉积。正常钙化的一种类型是骨化，即通过钙沉积形成新骨。钙化发生于骨化过程中，但反之则不然。异位骨化会产生非骨骼系统骨组织，即在正常无骨的软组织中形成骨骼。石化与化石化是矿化作用的其他类型。

**mixed pulmonary pattern** Two or more lung patterns are visible concurrently (i.e., bronchointerstitial, interstitial, and alveolar).
<br />

**混合型肺型** 两种或以上肺部病变模式同时可见（如支气管间质型、间质型及肺泡型）。

**monoarticular** One joint (i.e., all lesions in the same joint).
<br />

**单关节性** 仅累及单一关节（即所有病变位于同一关节）。

**monostotic** In the same bone.
<br />

**单骨性**（monostotic） 指病变发生于同一块骨骼。

**moth-eaten osteolysis** Multiple areas of bone loss, smaller and less distinct than geographic osteolysis.
<br />

**虫蚀样骨溶解** 多发性骨缺损区域，较地理性骨溶解更小且界限模糊。

**mucopolysaccharidosis** A group of inherited diseases characterized by accumulations of mucopolysaccharides, which can lead to permanent damage in musculoskeletal, ocular, neurologic, and circulatory systems.
<br />

**多糖贮积症** 一组遗传性疾病，特征为黏多糖在体内异常堆积，可导致肌肉骨骼系统、眼部、神经系统及循环系统遭受永久性损伤。

**myelocele** Neural defect in which the spinal cord protrudes through a dorsal spinal defect.
<br />

**脊髓膨出** 脊髓通过背侧脊柱缺损突出体外的神经缺陷。

**myelography** A radiographic contrast study of the spinal cord involving injection of positive contrast medium into the spinal subarachnoid space. Myelogram.
<br />

**脊髓造影术** 通过向脊髓蛛网膜下腔注射阳性造影剂，对脊髓进行放射学造影检查。脊髓造影图。

**myelosclerosis** Increased opacity in the medullary cavity of bone, fibrosis of the bone marrow
<br />

**骨髓硬化症** 骨髓腔内密度增高，伴随骨髓纤维化。

**myositis ossificans** The deposition of inorganic materials in muscle, usually secondary to trauma.
<br />

**骨化性肌炎** 肌肉中沉积无机物质，通常继发于创伤。

**mural** Pertaining to or occurring in the wall of a body cavity.
<br />

**壁性** 指发生于体腔壁的。

## `N`

**negative contrast agent** A contrast medium that absorbs very few x-rays and is less opaque than soft tissue or fat (i.e., a gas such as room air, carbon dioxide, nitrous oxide, or oxygen).
<br />

**阴性造影剂** 吸收极少X射线的造影剂，其不透明度低于软组织或脂肪（如室温空气、二氧化碳、一氧化二氮或氧气等气体）。

**nephrogram** The phase in excretory urography during which the kidneys are opacified by positive contrast medium.
<br />

**肾显影期** 排泄性尿路造影中肾脏被阳性造影剂显影的阶段。

**niche** A recess in the wall of a hollow organ that retains contrast. Ulcers and diverticuli are examples.
<br />

**凹陷** 空腔器官壁内可滞留造影剂的凹陷处。溃疡和憩室即属此类。

**nodular lesion** A spherical lesion measuring up to 30 mm in diameter.
<br />

**结节性病变** 直径不超过30毫米的球形病变。

**nodular pulmonary pattern** One or more small, rounded lesions in the lung (i.e., a structured interstitial pulmonary pattern).
<br />

**结节性肺部病变模式** 肺部出现一个或多个小而圆的病变（即具有结构化的间质性肺部病变模式）。

**nonionic iodinated contrast media** Positive contrast agents that are water-soluble but not converted into ions, similar in osmolality to plasma, and cause fewer adverse reactions compared with ionic contrast media.
<br />

**非离子型碘造影剂** 水溶性但不离子化的阳性造影剂，渗透压与血浆相似，与离子型造影剂相比不良反应较少。

**nonunion fracture** Fracture healing ceases before a complete union has occurred.
<br />

**不愈合性骨折** 骨折愈合过程在完全愈合前停止。

**Norberg angle** A numerical assessment of coxofemoral joint subluxation made on an extended ventrodorsal pelvic radiograph. Measurement is made by drawing a line connecting the centers of the two femoral heads and another line extending from the center of each femoral head to the cranial edge of the corresponding acetabular rim. Norberg angle greater than 105 is normal, between 90 and 105 is borderline, and less than 90 is abnormal.
<br />

**Norberg角** 通过伸展的腹背盆骨X光片对髋臼股骨关节半脱位进行数值评估。 测量方法：连接两股骨头中心点绘制一条线，再从每侧股骨头中心点延伸至对应髋臼盂边缘的头侧端绘制另一条线。诺伯格角大于105°为正常，90°至105°为临界值，小于90°为异常。

## `O`

**object** The thing or item being radiographed (i.e., patient or body part). Other names: subject, patient.
<br />

**对象** 接受X线检查的物体或部位（即患者或身体部位）。其他名称：受检者、患者。

**object-film distance (OFD)** The distance between the patient and the film or image receptor. In digital radiography, object-receptor distance.
<br />

**物体-胶片距离（OFD）** 患者与胶片或图像接收器之间的距离。在数字放射成像中，指物体-接收器距离。

**object-receptor distance (ORD)** The distance between the patient and the image receptor or receptor.
<br />

**物体-接收器距离（ORD）** 患者与图像接收器之间的距离。

**oblique radiograph** Animal or body part is slightly rotated from true lateral or true ventrodorsal/dorsoventral positioning to displace overlying structures from area of interest or to view a structure in profile. Oblique radiography may also be accomplished by changing the angle of the x-ray beam.
<br />

**斜位X线片** 动物或身体部位相对于真实侧位或真实腹背/背腹定位略微旋转，以使覆盖结构移离感兴趣区域或观察侧面结构。斜位X线摄影也可通过改变X射线束角度实现。

**obstipation** Inability to pass feces.
<br />

**便秘** 无法排出粪便的状态。

**OFA** Orthopedic Foundation for Animals, established in 1966.
<br />

**OFA** 动物骨科基金会，成立于1966年。

**oligocephalic** Shape of skull in which the nasal cavity is approximately equal in length to the cranium. Other names: mesaticephalic.
<br />

**小头畸形** 指颅骨形态中鼻腔长度近似等于颅骨长度的状态。别称：中头畸形。

**opacity (radiopacity)** Characteristic of a material to block (attenuate or absorb) x-rays. The more opaque a material, the more x-rays are blocked and the whiter the material appears in a radiograph. One of five basic opacities is assigned to a material; gas, fat, soft tissue, bone, or metal. The inherent opacity of a material is related to its density (atomic number and degree of compaction). Overall opacity of a material relates to its thickness.
<br />

 **不透明度（放射密度）** 物质阻挡（衰减或吸收）X射线的特性。物质越不透明，阻挡的X射线越多，在X射线照片中呈现的颜色越白。 物质被归入五种基本不透射性之一：气体、脂肪、软组织、骨骼或金属。物质的固有不透射性与其密度（原子序数及致密程度）相关。物质的整体不透射性与其厚度相关。

**opacity interface** The visible difference in shades of gray between two adjacent structures which allows them to be differentiated in a radiograph. Without opacity interfaces, a radiography would be homogenous, with no visible structures (see **relative opacity**). Opacity interfaces are affected by body condition (e.g., emaciation, immature age) and disease processes (e.g., effusion) and should not be confused with detail (affected by technical factors) or definition (refers to margination of a lesion).
<br />

**不透明度界面**相邻结构间可见的灰度差异，使它们能在X射线片上被区分开来。 若无不透明度界面（例如腹部`浆膜细节`下降），放射影像将呈现均匀状态，无法辨识结构（参见**相对不透明度**）。不透明度界面受身体状况（如消瘦、未成熟年龄）及病理过程（如积液）影响，不应与细节（受技术因素影响）或界限清晰度（指病灶边缘形态）混淆。

**opaque** Not clear or transparent; impenetrable, not allowing light, radiation, sound, heat, etc. to pass through; not transmitting any of these.
<br />

**不透明度** 不清晰或不透明；不可穿透，不允许光线、辐射、声音、热量等通过；不传递任何上述物质。

**organic iodinated compounds** Positive contrast agents commonly used to study intravascular, intrathecal, intraarticular, and soft tissue structures. High atomic number and mass of iodine result in high opacity in these agents, which are available as ionic (hypertonic) and nonionic (isotonic) preparations.
<br />

**有机碘化物**常用于研究血管内、鞘内、关节内及软组织结构的阳性造影剂。碘的高原子序数与质量赋予这些制剂高显影度，可制备为离子型（高渗）与非离子型（等渗）制剂。

**orthogonal radiographs** Radiographs made at right angles (perpendicular or 90 degrees) to each other. Radiographs are two-dimensional images of three-dimensional subjects and orthogonal projections are required for meaningful evaluation.
<br />

**正交射线**照片相互垂直（90度）拍摄的射线照片。射线照片是三维物体的二维图像，需通过正交投影才能进行有效评估。

**ossification** The process of creating new bone by osteoblasts, usually in a cartilaginous or fibrous tissue matrix. Calcification occurs during ossification, but the reverse is not true.
<br />

**骨化** 成骨细胞在软骨或纤维组织基质中生成新骨的过程。骨化过程中会发生钙化，但反之不成立。

**ossification center** A site of bone formation; primary centers of ossification develop in the diaphyses, secondary centers develop in the epiphyses and apophyses, and accessory centers form sesamoid bones.
<br />

**骨化中心** 骨形成部位；原发性骨化中心位于骨干，继发性骨化中心位于骨骺与骨突，副骨化中心则形成籽骨。

**osteoblasts** Cells that deposit bony matrix and are responsible for ossification.
<br />

**成骨细胞**：沉积骨基质并负责骨化的细胞。

**osteoclasts** Cells that dissolve bone mineral and matrix; an osteoclast can resorb eight times amount of bone produced by an osteoblast.
<br />

**破骨细胞** 溶解骨矿物质与基质的细胞；单个破骨细胞可吸收相当于成骨细胞生成量八倍的骨组织。

**osteocytes** Cells in bone that help maintain calcium homeostasis and mechanical properties of bone.
<br />

**骨细胞** 存在于骨组织中，协助维持钙稳态及骨骼力学特性的细胞。

**osteochondritis dissecans (OCD)** A developmental condition in which abnormal blood supply to epiphyseal bone disturbs endochondral ossification, resulting in thickening, softening, necrosis, and eventual collapse of cartilage.
<br />

**分离性骨软骨病（OCD）** 一种发育性疾病，表现为骨骺骨异常血供干扰软骨内成骨过程，导致软骨增厚、软化、坏死，并最终塌陷。

**osteochondroma** A benign bony mass caused by abnormal location of physeal cartilage. Mass usually stops growing at skeletal maturity, but malignant transformation to chondrosarcoma or osteosarcoma can occur. Appears as an expansile, inhomogeneous, osseous mass extending away from bone, typically with irregular borders and welldefined margins. Multiple osteochondroma is called osteochondromatosis or multiple cartilaginous exostoses.
<br />

**骨软骨瘤** 由生长板软骨位置异常形成的良性骨性肿块。通常在骨骼成熟后停止生长，但可能恶变为软骨肉瘤或骨肉瘤。表现为向外扩张的不均匀骨性肿块，典型特征为边缘不规则且界限清晰。多发性骨软骨瘤称为骨软骨瘤病或多发性软骨外生骨疣。

**osteogenesis imperfecta** Rare in dogs and cats, it is a generalized condition caused by a defect in collagen production and resulting in fragile bones. May be confused with hyperparathyroidism.
<br />

**成骨不全症** 犬猫中罕见，由胶原蛋白合成缺陷引发的全身性疾病，导致骨骼脆性增加。可能与甲状旁腺功能亢进症混淆。

**osteolysis** An abnormal area of active bone resorption caused by disease. The pattern of osteolysis reflects the aggressiveness of the disease process. The more aggressive the disease process, the less distinct the bony margins, the longer the zone of transition between normal and abnormal bone, and the more rapid the rate of change in serial radiographs. Patterns of osteolysis include geographic, motheaten, and permeative.
<br />

**骨溶解** 由疾病引发的异常活跃骨吸收区域。骨溶解形态反映疾病进程的侵袭性：病程越凶险，骨骼边缘越模糊，正常骨与病变骨的过渡区越长，连续X光片中的变化速率越快。骨溶解形态包括地图样、虫蚀样及浸润样。

**osteoma** A rare, benign, slow-growing, osseous neoplasm that protrudes from the surface of a bone, usually on the skull.
<br />

**骨瘤** 一种罕见的良性、生长缓慢的骨质新生物，从骨表面突出，通常发生于颅骨。

**osteomalacia** Softening of bones due to faulty mineralization of available osteoid; may result from nutritional deficiency, chronic renal or intestinal disease. The ratio of mineral to osteoid is low (bone is "bad quality, good quantity").
<br />

**骨软化症** 因成骨组织矿化缺陷导致的骨质软化；可能由营养缺乏、慢性肾脏或肠道疾病引起。矿化与成骨组织比例偏低（骨质呈现"质劣量多"状态）。

**osteomyelitis** Inflammation of bone; usually caused by an acute or chronic infection (e.g., bacterial, fungal, protozoal organisms). Technically, bone and medullary cavity.
<br />

**骨髓炎** 骨组织炎症，通常由急性或慢性感染（如细菌、真菌、原生动物）引发。严格定义涵盖骨体及骨髓腔。

**osteopenia** "Too little bone"; there is a decrease in bone mass due to osteolysis, osteoporosis, or osteomalacia.
<br />

**骨质减少症（osteopenia）**"骨量不足"；因骨溶解、骨质疏松或骨软化症导致骨量减少。

**osteopetrosis** Uncommon congenital disease leading to a generalized increase in bone thickness and opacity. Other names: marble bone.
<br />

**骨硬化症** 罕见先天性疾病，导致全身骨骼增厚与密度增高。别称：大理石骨病。

**osteophyte** A well-defined bony growth or bone spur projecting outward from a periarticular margin. Results from degeneration and mineralization of proliferating articular cartilage in non-weight-bearing areas. The common true sign of degenerative joint disease.
<br />

**骨赘** 指从关节周围边缘向外突出的界限清晰的骨质增生或骨刺。由非负重区域增生性关节软骨的退化与矿化形成，是退行性关节病最常见的真实征象。

**osteoporosis** "Porous bones"; loss of bone mass leading to weak and brittle bones with decreased opacity; may result from disuse or metabolic disease. The ratio of mineral to osteoid is normal, but both are diminished (bone is "good quality, bad quantity"). Other names: bone atrophy.
<br />

**骨质疏松症** "多孔性骨骼"；骨质流失导致骨骼脆弱易碎且密度降低；可能由长期不活动或代谢性疾病引起。矿物质与骨基质比例正常，但两者含量均减少（骨骼呈现"质量良好，数量不足"状态）。别名：骨萎缩。

**osteosclerosis (sclerosis)** Increased bone opacity; an area of bone with elevated density (more mineral is present in the bone). Radiographically more opaque.
<br />

 **骨硬化症（硬化）** 骨密度增高；骨组织密度升高区域（骨内矿物质含量增加）。X线影像中呈更强显影度。

**otic canalography** A radiographic contrast study of the external auditory canal.
<br />

**耳道造影术** 对外耳道进行的放射造影检查。

## `P`

**PACS (picture archiving and communication system)** A network of computers used to accomplish many of the services required in digital radiography:
<br />

 **PACS（图片存档与通信系统）** 用于实现数字放射成像所需多项服务的计算机网络：

**P:** Pictures made available for viewing, interpretation, reporting, and consultation from multiple modalities (radiology, ultrasound, CT, MRI, etc.).
<br />

 **P：** 提供多模态影像（放射影像、超声、CT、MRI等）的查看、解读、报告及会诊服务。

 **A：** 影像研究的短期与长期归档存储。

**C:** Communication with local and remote computers, professionals, and consultants to manage workflow and share information.
<br />

 **C：** 与本地及远程计算机、专业人员及顾问进行通信，以管理工作流程并共享信息。

**S:** System coordination and integration to works with radiology information systems (RIS), hospital information systems (HIS), and others to enable a paperless environment.
<br />

**S:** 系统协调与集成，可与放射信息系统（RIS）、医院信息系统（HIS）及其他系统协同工作，实现无纸化环境。

**palmar** Associated with the palm of the hand; refers to the bottom surface of a distal thoracic limb or manus. Opposed to dorsal.
<br />

**掌侧（palmar）** 与手掌相关的；指远端胸肢或手部的底面。与背侧相对。

**panosteitis** A self-limiting bone disorder of unknown etiology that causes an acute onset of pain in one or more bones. Multiple bones may be affected either simultaneously or sequentially. Lameness commonly shifts between legs. Lesions usually are asymmetrical in distribution. Appears in radiographs as areas of increased medullary opacity, typically near a nutrient foramen and sometimes with a periosteal response.
<br />

**全骨炎（Panosteitis）** 全骨炎是一种病因不明的自限性骨骼疾病，常导致一处或多处骨骼突然出现急性疼痛。多个骨骼可能同时或先后受影响，跛行常在不同腿之间转移，病变分布通常不对称。在X光片上表现为髓腔密度增高的区域，常位于营养孔附近，有时伴有骨膜反应。

**parenteral** Any means of administering a substance to a patient that does not involve the alimentary tract (e.g., injecting directly into the body, transcutaneous, inhaled).
<br />

**非肠道给药** 指不通过消化道向患者输送药物的任何途径（例如直接注射入体内、经皮给药、吸入给药）。

**parosteal osteosarcoma** A rare, slow-growing bone tumor that arises from the surface of the bone instead of the intramedullary region. Commonly appears as a smooth-bordered, nonaggressive bony mass arising from the surface of the bone.
<br />

**骨膜性骨肉瘤** 一种罕见的、生长缓慢的骨肿瘤，起源于骨表面而非髓腔区域。通常表现为从骨表面生长的、边缘光滑、非侵袭性的骨质肿块。

**patchy** Refers to multiple, indistinct, irregularly shaped lesions; commonly used to describe amorphous areas of increased opacity in the lungs.
<br />

**斑块状** 指多发性、界限不清、形态不规则的病变；常用于描述肺部呈弥漫性密度增高的区域。

**pathologic fracture** A loss of continuity in a bone that has been weakened by an underlying disease process or developmental defect; typically caused by neoplasia or metabolic bone disease.
<br />

**病理性骨折** 因潜在疾病过程或发育缺陷导致骨骼强度减弱而发生的连续性中断；通常由肿瘤或代谢性骨病引起。

**penumbra** The partial outer shadows that blur the edges of structures and decrease radiographic detail. Occurs due to the fact that x-rays do not originate from a single point but are generated over a small area. Penumbra can be minimized by using a small focal spot, a longer distance between the focal spot and image receptor, and a shorter distance between the patient (subject or object) and image receptor. Other names: **geometric unsharpness**.
<br />

**半影** 指使结构边缘模糊并降低放射影像细节的部分外缘阴影。其成因在于X射线并非源自单一点，而是由小范围区域产生。可通过采用小焦点、延长焦点与成像器之间的距离、缩短患者（受检者或被检物）与成像器之间的距离来最小化半影现象。别称：**几何模糊**。

**peribronchial cuffing or infiltrates** Thickened soft tissue opacity around a bronchus due to accumulation of fluid, cells, or fibrosis; a sign of bronchial disease or peri- bronchial accumulation; also bronchial cuffing.
<br />

**支气管周围套环状**阴影**或浸润性改变**因液体、细胞或纤维化物质积聚导致支气管周围软组织密度增高；提示支气管疾病或支气管周围积聚；亦称支气管套环状改变。

**perineal hernia** Weakening of the muscles that support the rectum (i.e., the pelvic diaphragm) allows displace- ment of fat and organs from the pelvis and abdominal cavity (e.g., rectum, prostate gland, urinary bladder). Rectal displacement or diverticulum leads to partial or complete obstruction and colon largely distended with fecal material (fecal impaction). Soft tissue swelling often is visible in the perineal region, located ventral and lateral to the base of the tail. The rectum may be deviated laterally (VD view). The urinary bladder may or may not be recognized (may need retrograde urethrocystogram for identification). Barium enema: may document rectal deviation or rectal divertic- ulum. The inflated bulb of the rectal catheter can obscure lesions in the anal canal and distal rectum. Pneumocolon can help identify colon location and shape.
<br />

**会阴疝** 支撑直肠的肌肉（即盆底肌）发生薄弱，导致脂肪组织及器官（如直肠、前列腺、膀胱）从盆腔和腹腔移位。直肠移位或憩室形成可引发部分或完全性梗阻，结肠因粪便堆积而严重扩张（粪便嵌塞）。 会阴区常可见软组织肿胀，位于尾根腹侧及外侧。直肠可能向外侧偏移（VD视图）。 膀胱可能可见或不可见（需行逆行尿道膀胱造影以明确）。钡剂灌肠：可显示直肠偏移或直肠憩室。直肠导管充盈的球囊可能遮挡肛管及远端直肠病变。气钡造影有助于确定结肠位置及形态。

**periosteal response** Formation of new bone to fill the space created when the periosteum is separated from the cortex. New bone forms perpendicular to the periosteum. The appearance of the margin of the periosteal new bone formation reflects the activity of the disease process (the less distinct the margin, the more active the disease process).
<br />

**骨膜反应**：当骨膜与骨体分离时，新生骨组织会填充形成的间隙。骨皮质。新骨垂直于骨膜形成。骨膜新骨形成的边缘形态反映疾病进程的活跃程度（边缘越模糊，疾病进程越活跃）。

**periosteum** Outer covering of bones; covers exterior of all bones except at the joint surfaces. Periosteum consists of two layers; a tough outer layer of fibrous connective tissue for protection and an inner cambium layer for bone produc- tion. The cambium produces new bone for circumferential growth in immature bones and healing when bone is damaged and periosteum is separated from cortex.
<br />

**骨膜** 骨的外层覆盖物；除关节面外覆盖所有骨的外侧。骨膜由两层构成：具有保护功能的坚韧纤维结缔组织外层，以及负责骨生成的内层形成层。形成层在未成熟骨中产生新骨以实现周向生长，并在骨损伤且骨膜与皮质骨分离时促进愈合。

**permeative osteolysis** Numerous tiny areas of bone loss, sometimes difficult to distinguish from normal bone.
<br />

**弥漫性骨溶解** 骨质缺损呈多灶性微小病灶分布，有时难以与正常骨组织区分。

**pes** Distal part of a pelvic limb; includes tarsus, meta- tarsus, and digits.
<br />

**跗骨** 骨盆肢远端部分；包含跗骨、跖骨及趾骨。

**phosphor** A rare-earth compound that emits an instanta- neous flash of light after interaction with an x-ray. The light is phosphorescence rather than fluorescence (latter glows for a longer period of time).
<br />

**磷光体** 一种稀土化合物，与X射线作用后会瞬间闪光。该光属于磷光而非荧光（后者持续发光时间更长）。

**physis** The cartilaginous growth plate located between the metaphysis and the epiphysis of an immature long bone. Other names: epiphyseal plate, growth plate.
<br />

**骨骺** 位于未成熟长骨干骺端与骨骺之间的软骨生长板。别称：骨骺板、生长板。

**physeal fracture** A break in bone that occurs through a physis (occurs in immature animals). Fracture com- monly is described using Salter-Harris classification. The physis affected and the age at which the damage occurred are important because the percentage of growth at the proximal and distal ends of a long bone varies over time. The greatest difference between proximal and distal physeal growth occurs in the ulna, where the distal physis con- tributes approximately 90% to overall growth. The distal ulnar physis is particularly susceptible to damage due to its unique conical shape.
<br />

**骨骺骨折** 指通过骨骺发生的骨骼断裂（常见于未成熟动物）。此类骨折通常采用Salter-Harris分类法描述。受累骨骺及损伤发生年龄至关重要，因为长骨近端与远端的生长比例随时间变化。 尺骨近远端生长板差异最为显著，其远端生长板贡献了约90%的整体生长量。尺骨远端生长板因独特的锥形结构而特别易受损伤。

**phytobezoar** An accumulation or concretion of plant material (fruit, vegetable, or grass fibers) within the alimentary tract (usually stomach or intestine).
<br />

**植物性胃石** 指植物性物质（果实、蔬菜或草纤维）在消化道（通常为胃或肠道）内积聚形成的结石。

**plantar** Associated with the sole of the foot; refers to the bottom surface of a distal pelvic limb or pes. Opposite of dorsal when referring to a hindlimb.
<br />

**趾部的** 指与足底相关的；特指远端骨盆肢或足的底面。在后肢解剖中与背侧相对。

**pleural fissure line** Linear increased opacity between lung lobes. May represent pleural thickening (line is uniform in thickness and does not taper), pleural effusion (line widens peripherally and tapers toward the hilus), or interlobar fat (line is wider centrally and tapers peripher- ally). Other names: interlobar fissure line, fissure line.
<br />

**胸膜裂隙线** 肺叶间呈线状增密影。可能表现为胸膜增厚（线状影厚度均匀且不呈锥形收窄）、胸腔积液（线状影向周边增宽并向肺门锥形收窄）或肺叶间脂肪（线状影中央较宽并向周边锥形收窄）。其他名称：肺叶间裂隙线、裂隙线。

**pleural thickening** Increased width and opacity of parietal or visceral pleura due to deposits of fibrin or mineral.
<br />

**胸膜增厚** 由于纤维化或矿化

**pleurography** A radiographic contrast study of the parietal and visceral pleural surfaces made by instilling contrast medium into the pleural space.
<br />

**胸膜造影术** 通过向胸膜腔注入造影剂，对胸膜壁层与脏层表面进行放射学造影检查。

**plication** Abnormal bunching of small intestine with tight turns and irregular pockets of gas, commonly caused by linear foreign material. Other names: pleating, accordion pleating.
<br />

 小肠**褶皱**：小肠异常褶皱，伴有紧绷的弯曲和不规则的气囊，通常由线状异物引起。别名：褶皱、手风琴征。

**pneumatocoele** An air-filled cyst in a lung; usually results from trauma.
<br />

**气胸** 肺部充满空气的囊肿；通常由创伤引起。

**pneumomediastinum** Presence of free gas in the medi- astinum; results in visualization of mediastinal structures not normally seen.
<br />

 **气纵隔：** 纵隔内存在游离气体；导致可观察到正常情况下不可见的纵隔结构。

**pneumopericardiography** A radiographic contrast study of the pericardial space; used to evaluate the peri- cardial sac, epicardial surface of the heart, and origin of the aorta and pulmonary arteries. Typically performed by instilling gas in the pericardial sac.
<br />

**气心膜造影术**：心包腔的放射性造影检查；用于评估心包囊、心脏心外膜表面及主动脉与肺动脉的起源部位。通常通过向心包囊注入气体实施。

**pneumoperitoneum** Free gas in the peritoneal space.
<br />

**气腹（pneumoperitoneum）** 腹腔内存在游离气体。

**pneumoperitoneography** A radiographic contrast study of the peritoneal space; gas is instilled into the peritoneal space.
<br />

**气腹造影术** 腹腔空间的放射性造影检查；通过向腹膜腔注入气体实现。

**pneumothorax** Presence of free gas in the pleural space.

- *Simple* pneumothorax: air in pleural space is less than or equal to atmospheric pressure.
- *Closed* pneumothorax: a simple pneumothorax caused by either a ruptured lung, ruptured airway, or ruptured esophagus, the latter as an extension of a pneumomedi- astinum. The thoracic wall remains intact and the pleural gas originates from inside the body. In patients with small lung ruptures that seal rapidly, pleural gas usually is resorbed within 48 hours.
- *Open* pneumothorax: a simple pneumothorax caused by a penetrating wound that creates an opening in the thoracic wall that allows air (gas) to enter the pleural space. Pleural pressure is similar to atmospheric pressure.
- *spontaneous* pneumothorax occurs acutely with no history of trauma. Gas may leak into the pleural space from a ruptured lung bulla or an eroded airway, the latter due to a tumor, abscess, or parasite migration.
- *Tension* pneumothorax: a closed pneumothorax in which pressure within the pleural space is greater than atmo- spheric pressure, resulting in compression atelectasis of the lung(s).
<br />

**气胸** 胸膜腔内存在游离气体。
- *单纯性*气胸：胸膜腔内气体压力小于或等于大气压。
- *闭合性*气胸：由肺破裂、气道破裂或食管破裂（后者为气胸的延伸）引起的单纯性气胸。胸壁保持完整，胸膜气体源于体内。对于肺破裂面积小且能快速闭合的患者，胸膜气体通常在48小时内被吸收。
- *开放性*气胸：由穿透性创伤导致胸壁破损，使空气（气体）进入胸膜腔所致的单纯性气胸。胸膜腔内压力接近大气压。
- *自发性*气胸急性发生且无外伤史。气体可能从破裂的肺大泡或受侵蚀的气道渗入胸膜腔，后者由肿瘤、脓肿或寄生虫迁移引起。
- *张力性*气胸：一种闭合性气胸，其特征为胸膜腔内压力高于大气压，导致肺组织受压性萎陷。

**pollakiuria** Increased frequency of urination.
<br />

**多尿** 排尿次数增多

**polyarticular** Associated with multiple joints.
<br />

 **多关节性：** 涉及多个关节。

**polyarthritis** Inflammation affecting multiple joints.
<br />

 **多关节炎：** 影响多个关节的炎症。

**polydactyly** Congenital abnormality in which one or more extra digits are present.
<br />

**多指症（polydactyly）** 指先天性异常，表现为存在一个或多个额外指趾。

<br />

**多骨性** ，指涉及多块骨骼的情况。

**poorly-defined** Similar meaning to *ill-defined*, but sometimes used as a qualifier when making a decision between *well-defined* and *ill-defined*. **门静脉造影术** 对门静脉系统的放射性造影检查。造影剂可注入肠系膜静脉（静脉门静脉造影）或脾脏（脾门静脉造影）。

**portosystemic vascular shunt (PSS)** An abnormal communication between the portal vascular system and the systemic circulation. PSS may be congenital or acquired, intra-hepatic or extrahepatic. Congenital intra-hepatic shunts are more common in large-breed dogs (e.g., Irish Wolfhound, Golden Retriever) and often include a persis- tent ductus venosus. Congenital extra-hepatic shunts are more common in cats and small-breed dogs (e.g., Miniature Schnauzer, Yorkshire Terrier) and usually involve azygous vein or caudal vena cava. Congenital PSS can result from microvascular dysplasia, an inherited abnormality in small breed dogs (e.g., Cairn Terrier) in which tiny blood vessels in liver are absent or underdeveloped. Acquired PSS more often are reported in older animals with chronic liver disease that leads to portal hypertension (multiple shunts often are present).
<br />

 **门体循环分流（PSS）** 指门静脉系统与体循环之间存在的异常通道。PSS可为先天性或后天性，可发生于肝内或肝外。先天性肝内分流常见于大型犬种（如爱尔兰猎狼犬、金毛寻回犬），常伴有静脉导管未闭。 先天性肝外分流常见于猫及小型犬（如迷你雪纳瑞、约克夏梗），通常涉及奇静脉或下腔静脉尾部。 先天性门静脉分流可能源于微血管发育不良，这是小型犬（如凯恩梗）的遗传性异常，表现为肝脏微血管缺失或发育不全。获得性门静脉分流多见于患有慢性肝病的老年动物，该病症导致门静脉高压（常伴有多重分流）。

**positive contrast agent** A contrast medium that absorbs many x-rays and is more opaque than soft tissue or bone (e.g., barium sulfate, organic iodinated compounds).
<br />

**阳性造影剂**：能吸收大量X射线且比软组织或骨骼更不透射线的造影剂（如硫酸钡、有机碘化物）。

**pronate** To rotate a limb inward so that the palmar or plantar surface faces downward (or lateral) and the dorsal or cranial surface faces upward (or medial).
<br />

**掌屈** 使肢体向内旋转，掌面或足底朝下（或外侧），背侧或头侧朝上（或内侧）。

**proportionate dwarfism** Chondrodysplasia that affects all parts of the skeleton equally, usually due to inadequate growth hormone caused by a pituitary problem. Most often is reported in German Shepherds, Miniature Pinschers, Spitzes, and Covelian Bear Dogs. Concurrent anomalies may be present, including hypothyroidism, patent ductus arteriosus, and megaesophagus.

**比例性侏儒症** 软骨发育不良症，影响骨骼所有部位且程度均等，通常由垂体问题导致生长激素分泌不足引起。最常见于德国牧羊犬、迷你杜宾犬、松狮犬和科威尔熊犬。可能伴随其他异常，包括甲状腺功能减退、动脉导管未闭及巨食管症。

**proximal** Anatomical term that means nearest the trunk or point of origin. Proximal is used to describe the part of a limb nearest its attachment to the body or the part of a body structure that is closer to its origin (trachea, bronchi, intestines, ureters, etc.). Opposite is **distal**.
<br />

**近端** 解剖学术语，指最靠近躯干或起源点的部位。

**pseudoarthrosis** "False joint"; a fibrous capsule filled with serum that may form at the site of a long-term nonunion fracture, chronic dislocation, or developmental abnormality.
<br />

**假关节**"假性关节"；指长期不愈合骨折、慢性脱位或发育异常部位形成的充满浆液的纤维囊。

**pseudo-nodule** A discrete soft tissue or mineral opacity that mimics a pulmonary nodule (e.g., end-on visualization of a pulmonary vessel, superimposed nipple, costochondral junction, heterotopic bone formation).
<br />

**假结节** 指形态酷似肺结节的孤立性软组织或矿化密度区（如肺血管端面显影、重叠乳头、肋软骨连接处、异位骨形成）。

**pseudogout** Also called *chondrocalcinosis* or *calcium pyro- phosphate deposition disease*, is a rare condition of unknown etiology that can cause soft tissue mineralization near or in a joint.
<br />

**假性**痛风又称_软骨钙沉积症_或_焦磷酸钙沉积病_，是一种病因不明的罕见疾病，可导致关节附近或关节内的软组织矿化。

**pulmonary emphysema** Refers to increased size of lung air spaces due to air trapping secondary to severe bronchial disease and inability to expire trapped air.
<br />

**肺气肿** 指因严重支气管疾病导致气体滞留，且无法呼出滞留气体，致使肺泡腔体积增大的病变。

**pyelogram** The phase in excretory urography during which contrast medium opacifies the renal collecting system (e.g., renal pelvis, pelvic recesses).
<br />

**肾盂造影** 指排泄性尿路造影中造影剂使肾集合系统（如肾盂、肾盂凹陷）显影的阶段。

**pyothorax** Accumulation of purulent exudate in the pleural space.
<br />

**脓胸** 指脓性渗出液在胸膜腔内积聚的病症。

**pyruvate kinase deficiency** An inherited deficit in an erythrocyte enzyme that can lead to hemolytic anemia. Radiographs may reveal increased medullary opacity in all parts of the appendicular and axial skeleton. The disease has been reported in certain dog breeds (e.g., Basenji, West Highland White Terriers) and in cats. Affected animals typi- cally exhibit clinical signs of anemia, including lethargy and exercise intolerance.
<br />

**丙酮酸激酶**缺乏症 指红细胞中一种酶的遗传性缺陷，可导致溶血性贫血。X光片可能显示附肢骨骼和轴骨骼各部位骨髓密度增加。该病已在某些犬种（如巴仙吉犬、西高地白梗）和猫中被报道。患病动物通常表现为贫血临床症状，包括精神萎靡和运动耐受性下降。

**放射征象** 指X射线片中出现的异常结构或意外形态，亦称放射学发现。

<br />

**放射科医师** 接受过X射线、放射性核素、辐射物理学及辐射生物学诊断与治疗应用专业培训的专家。别称：伦琴医师。

**radiology** The scientific discipline of medical imaging using ionizing radiation, radionuclides, nuclear magnetic resonance, and ultrasound.
<br />

**放射学**运用电离辐射、放射性核素、核磁共振及超声波进行医学成像的科学学科。

**radiolucent/radiopaque** Classically, these terms are used to label materials that block few or many x-rays or to indi- cated parts of a radiograph that appear lighter or darker than expected. These are subjective terms that convey less useful information than simply describing the relative opacity. For example, soft tissue opacity cystic calculi might commonly be labeled "radiolucent." These calculi would not be visible in a bladder filled with urine, yet they are labeled "radiolucent." The same calculi would be labeled "radiopaque" in a negative contrast cystogram. They would again be labeled "radiolu- cent" in a double contrast cystogram. It is much more useful to simply describe the calculi as "soft tissue opacity."
<br />

**透射性/不透射性** 传统上，这些术语用于标记阻挡少量或大量X射线的物质，或指X光片中比预期更亮或更暗的区域。这些主观术语传递的信息不如直接描述相对不透明度有用。例如，软组织不透明的囊性结石通常会被标记为"透射性"。 在充满尿液的膀胱中，此类结石不可见，却仍被标记为"透射性"。而在负性造影膀胱片中，相同结石会被标记为"不透射性"。若采用双重造影膀胱片检查，它们又将被标记为"透射性"。相比之下，直接描述为"软组织密度"显然更为实用。

**radiopacity** See **opacity** (in a radiography text, *radio*- is a given and the radiopacity can be shortened to *opacity*).
<br />

**放射密度** 参见**不透射性**（在放射学文献中，"radio-"为固定前缀，"radiopacity"可简称为"*opacity*"）。

**recumbent (recumbency)** Lying down; used to describe the position of a patient during radiography; e.g., lateral recumbency = lying on its side, sternal recumbency = lying on its ventrum, dorsal recumbency = lying on its back.
<br />

**卧位（recumbent/recumbency）** 躺卧；用于描述X光摄影时患者的体位，例如：侧卧位（lateral recumbency）=侧卧，胸骨卧位（sternal recumbency）=俯卧，背卧位（dorsal recumbency）=仰卧。

**receptor** See image receptor
<br />

**接收器** 见影像接收器

**reflux** Reversed flow, such as flow of gastric content into the esophagus, flow of esophageal content into the mouth, flow of contrast medium from urinary bladder into ureters.
<br />

**反流**：逆向流动，如胃内容物流入食管、食管内容物流入口腔、造影剂从膀胱逆流至输尿管。

**regurgitation:** Reflux of undigested food from the esoph- agus into the pharynx or nasal cavity (differentiate from vomiting).
<br />

**反流**：未消化食物从食管逆流至咽部或鼻腔（需与呕吐区分）。

**relative opacity** Refers to the differences in x-ray absorption by various materials. Every material absorbs x-rays in a characteristic manner, depending on the physical composition of that material. It is the variation in x-ray absorption that allows various materials to be differentiated on radiographs. Relative opacity of a material is described as it compares to (i) one of the five basic opacities (gas, fat, fluid, mineral, or metal), (ii) the opacity of adjacent structures (i.e., relatively increased or decreased in opacity), or (iii) the opacity of a known normal structure. Overall opacity of a material is also affected by its thickness.
<br />

**相对不透明度**指不同材料对X射线的吸收差异。每种材料吸收X射线的方式各具特征，取决于其物理组成。正是X射线吸收量的差异使得不同材料能在X射线照片上被区分开来。 材料的相对致密度可通过以下三种方式描述：(i) 与五种基本致密度（气体、脂肪、液体、矿物质或金属）的比较；(ii) 与邻近结构致密度的对比（即相对增高或降低）；(iii) 与已知正常结构致密度的对照。材料的整体致密度亦受其厚度影响。

**retained cartilaginous core** A temporary or permanent presence of cartilage in the distal portion of a long bone, most commonly in the distal ulna. Cartilage is soft tissue opacity. Core typically is shaped like a triangle, cone, or "candle flame."
<br />

**软骨核残留**：长骨远端（最常见于尺骨远端）暂时性或永久性存在的软骨组织。软骨属于软组织密度。该核心通常呈三角形、锥形或"烛焰状"。

**reticular pattern** Refers to a fine interlacing pattern of lines; usually used to describe appearance of an interstitial pulmonary pattern.
<br />

**网状纹理(非血管线性纹理增多)** 指由细线交织形成的图案；通常用于描述间质性肺病变的外观特征。

**rheumatoid arthritis** A severe, progressive, and destructive polyarthritis caused by circulating autoanti- bodies against IgG.
<br />

**类风湿性关节炎** 由针对IgG的循环自身抗体引发的严重、进行性、破坏性多关节炎。

**rhinography** A radiographic contrast study of the nasal cavity.
<br />

**鼻腔造影术** 对鼻腔进行放射性造影检查。

**rickets** Vitamin D deficiency in young animals resulting in softening and weakening of bones.
<br />

**佝偻病** 幼年动物因维生素D缺乏导致骨骼软化和脆弱的疾病。

**RIS (radiology information system)** Used to coordi- nate, manipulate, distribute, and store diagnostic imaging data.
<br />

**RIS（放射信息系统）** 用于协调、处理、分发和存储诊断影像数据。

**Roentgen** Wilhelm Conrad Roentgen, a German physicist who discovered x-rays in 1895 and won the first Nobel Prize in physics for his discovery.
<br />

**伦琴** 威廉·康拉德·伦琴，德国物理学家，1895年发现X射线，并因此获得首届诺贝尔物理学奖。

**Roentgenography** Synonym for radiography.
<br />

**X射线摄影** 与放射摄影（radiography）同义。

**rostral** Toward the nose when discussing the head. Opposite is **caudal**.
<br />

**头侧**（头部解剖术语）指朝向鼻子的方向。相反方向为**尾侧**。

**Radiography Rules of Two**

- **Two views** orthogonal radiographs are needed for complete interpretation.
- **Two opacities** a structure is visible only if it is adjacent to a structure with a different opacity.
- **Two occurrences** radiographic signs usually are visible in more than one view; if not, rule out artifact.
- **Two lesions** after finding an abnormality do not stop searching, always look for a second.
- **Two joints** include joints above and below a long bone.
- **Two tissues** if bone appears abnormal, examine the adjacent soft tissues; if soft tissue looks abnormal, examine the nearby bones.
- **Two sides** compare the opposite side or limb with sus- pected abnormality to opposite side or limb.
- **Two examples** compare radiographic findings with known normal radiographs.
- **Two occasions** compare current radiographs with previous radiographs (sometimes the old radiograph is the most important one).
- **Two visits** make follow-up radiographs to assess progress and response to therapy.
- **Two reads** evaluate radiographs again in light of patient history and results of laboratory testing.

**X射线摄影双重规则**

- 需拍摄**两个**正交**视角**的X光片才能进行完整解读。
- **双密度法则**：结构物仅在与密度不同的邻近结构共存时方可显影。
- 放射学征象通常在多个视角可见；若未见，则需排除伪影。
- 发现异常后，**两个病灶**的检索不会停止，务必寻找第二个病灶。
- **两个**关节指长骨上下的相邻关节。
- 若骨骼异常，需检查相邻软组织；若软组织异常，需检查邻近骨骼。
- **两侧对比**：将疑似异常侧的肢体与对侧肢体进行对照。
- **双例对照**：将放射影像发现与已知正常影像进行比对。
- **两次检查**：将当前X光片与既往影像对比（旧片有时最具诊断价值）。
- **两次就诊**：拍摄随访影像以评估治疗进展及疗效。
- **复查两次**：结合病史及实验室检查结果重新评估影像。

**spina bifida** Congenital abnormality in which failure of the lateral vertebral arches to fuse dorsally results in incomplete development of the dorsal spinal process(es); also called "split spine" or "cleft spine." *Spina bifida occulta* is characterized by a small, asymptomatic split in a vertebra with a normal spinal cord. *Spina bifida manifesta* presents with a larger vertebral defect and a saclike protrusion (herniation) that contains spinal cord, meninges, or both: *myelocele*: sac contains spinal cord; *meningocele*: sac contains meninges; *meningomyelocele*: sac contains meninges and spinal cord or nerve roots. Cross-sectional imaging (e.g., CT, MRI) and myelography provide a more detailed evaluation.
<br />

**脊柱裂** 一种先天性畸形，表现为椎弓侧缘未能向背侧融合，导致背侧棘突发育不全；亦称"脊柱裂开"或"脊柱裂隙"。隐性脊柱裂特征为椎体存在微小无症状裂隙，但脊髓发育正常。 *显性脊柱*裂表现为较大的椎体缺损及囊状突起（疝出），内含脊髓、脑膜或两者：*脊髓膨出*：囊内含脊髓；*脑膜膨出*：囊内含脑膜；*脑脊髓膨出*：囊内含脑膜及脊髓或神经根。 横断面影像学检查（如CT、MRI）和脊髓造影可提供更详细的评估。

**spondylitis** Inflammation of a vertebral body, usually caused by infection (e.g., migrating foreign material).
<br />

**脊椎炎** 椎体的炎症，通常由感染（例如迁移的异物）引起。

**spondylosis deformans** A noninflammatory degenerative disorder associated with the vertebral bodies and characterized by complete or incomplete bony bridging of ventral and lateral margins of vertebrae. NOT osteophytes.
<br />

**脊柱变形症** 一种与椎体相关的非炎症性退行性疾病，特征为椎体前缘与侧缘出现完全或不完全的骨性桥接。非骨赘。

**static fog** Radiographic artifact caused by static electricity; appears as blackened areas with irregularly branching patterns (resembles a picture of lightning) or as multiple foci (black dots) in a radiograph. Occurs more often in areas with low humidity (in some hospitals, antistatic strips are installed to discharge personnel prior to opening cassettes or handling film).

**systematic approach** An organized and deliberate method of examining a radiograph to avoid overlooking diagnostic information.

**系统性方法** 指有组织、有条理地检查X光片以避免遗漏诊断信息的系统方法。

**scatter radiation** Secondary x-rays created by interactions of primary x-rays with the materials in their paths. Scatter x-rays travel in unpredictable directions and do not provide any useful information in the radiograph. **系列X线片** 指在单次检查（如胃肠造影系列、排泄性尿路造影）或较长时间间隔（数日或数周）内连续拍摄的影像，用于疾病或病症的随访评估，以监测病情进展及治疗反应。

## `T`

**table top technique** Image receptor (e.g., film, cassette) is placed on top of the table without an interposed grid between x-ray tube and receptor. Animal is positioned on top of the receptor. Because in most configurations, the grid is in a Bucky tray within the table, techniques using a grid may be called Bucky technique and without a grid, tabletop technique.
<br />

**台面技术** 图像接收器（如胶片、胶片盒）置于X射线管与接收器之间无栅格隔离的台面上。动物置于接收器上方。由于多数配置中栅格位于台面内的Bucky托盘中，故使用栅格的技术称为Bucky技术，不使用栅格的则称为台面技术。

**靶材**X射线管的阳极（正极）部件，即X射线产生部位；由高熔点金属合金构成，能承受电子束作用产生的高温。

**teat sign** Describes the appearance of the pyloric antrum during a barium contrast study in which a relatively sharp, pointed, out-pouching of the pyloric antrum develops along the lesser curvature as a peristaltic wave pushes the contrast medium up against a mass-like or stenotic lesion around the pylorus.
<br />

**乳头征**：指钡剂造影检查中幽门窦的形态表现。当蠕动波将造影剂推向幽门周围的肿块样或狭窄病变时，幽门窦沿小弯侧形成相对锐利、尖端突出的囊状膨出。

**technique chart** A table of reliable x-ray exposures based on body part and thickness. Chart may be kVp variable or mAs variable. If a technique chart is not available, here are some quick tips to estimate mAs and kVp:
<br />

**技术图表**：根据身体部位和厚度列出的可靠X射线曝光参数表。该图表可为kVp可变型或mAs可变型。若无技术图表可用，可参考以下快速估算mAs和kVp的方法：

- Estimate mAs: multiply the patient's body weight in pounds by 0.1 and add 1 (mAs = (body wt.× 0.1)+ 1). For example: to make an abdominal radiograph of a 22 pound dog, the mAs should be 3.2; (22 × 0.1)+ 1 = 3.2. For thorax radiographs use 1/2 the mAs and for orthopedic images double it.
- Estimate mAs: another method is to estimate mAs based on patient body weight. For patients less than 10 kg, use 2.5-5 mAs; patients that weigh 10-25 kg, use 5-10 mAs, and for patients over 25 kg, sue 10-15 mAs.
- Estimate kVp: multiply the body part thickness in centimeters by 2 and add the SID in inches (kVp = (thickness × 2)+ SID). For example: to make an abdominal radiograph of a body part that measures 14 cm thick and using a 35 inch SID, the kVp should be 63; (14 × 2)+ 35 = 63.

- 估算mAs值：将患者体重（磅）乘以0.1后加1（mAs = (体重 × 0.1) + 1）。 例如：为体重22磅的犬拍摄腹部X光片时，mAs值应为3.2；(22 × 0.1) + 1 = 3.2。胸部X光片使用1/2的mAs值，骨科影像则需加倍。

**urography** A radiographic contrast study of the kidneys and ureters. Other names: excretory urography, intravenous urography (old term: intravenous pyelogram or IVP).
<br />

**泌尿系统造影** 肾脏与输尿管的放射性造影检查。别称：排泄性泌尿造影、静脉泌尿造影（旧称：静脉肾盂造影或IVP）。

## `V`

**vacuum phenomenon** In a joint, a collection of gas that results when traction is applied creating negative intraarticular pressure, which allows nitrogen in the synovial fluid to escape and leak into the joint.
<br />

**真空现象** 关节内牵引产生负的关节腔内压，使滑液中的氮气逸出并渗入关节腔，形成气体积聚。

**vaginography** A radiographic contrast study of the vagina, cervix, and urethra.
<br />

**阴道造影术** 对阴道、宫颈及尿道的放射性造影检查。

**vascular pulmonary pattern** Pulmonary arteries or/ and veins are larger or smaller than normal.
<br />

**血管性肺模式** 肺动脉和/或肺静脉较正常尺寸增粗或缩小。

**ventral** Toward the belly or sternum. Contrast **dorsal**.
<br />

**腹侧** 指朝向腹部或胸骨的方向。反义词**背侧**。

**ventral recumbency** Animal is positioned lying on its sternum.
<br />

**腹位（ventral recumbency）** 动物呈俯卧于胸骨的姿势。

**ventrodorsal (VD) radiograph** The x-ray beam enters the ventral surface of a body part and exits the dorsal surface.
<br />

**腹背位（VD）X光片** X射线束从身体部位的腹侧面进入，从背侧面射出。

**vertebral heart size (VHS)** An objective measurement of the size of the cardiac silhouette made on a lateral view thoracic radiograph using the lengths of thoracic vertebral bodies as units of measure. Technique is based on a study performed by Buchanan and Bucheler in 1995. Also called vertebral heart score, vertebral heart sum.
<br />

**椎体心脏比值（VHS）** 基于侧位胸片心脏轮廓的客观测量值，以胸椎体长度为计量单位。该技术源自1995年 Buchanan 和 Bucheler的一项研究。该术语亦称椎体心脏评分或椎体心脏总和。

**vertical beam** The x-ray beam is directed vertically.
<br />

**垂直束** X射线束垂直投射。

**visual acuity** The ability to resolve a spatial pattern in an image.
<br />

**视敏度** 区分图像中空间图案的能力。

**vomit** Forceful expulsion of gastric or intestinal content through the mouth due to reflex contractions of stomach and abdominal muscles and preceded by nausea; usually caused by a problem caudal to the diaphragm (differentiate from regurgitation).
<br />

**呕吐** 因胃部及腹部肌肉反射性收缩，伴随恶心感，导致胃肠内容物经口腔强力排出；通常由膈肌下方病变引起（需与反流区分）。

## `W`

**well-defined** A distinct margin. A simple test to determine whether a margin is well defined or not is to attempt to accurately draw a line around the lesion or border. If you can easily trace the edges, it is well-defined. "Well-defined" often is incorrectly described as "smooth"; but *smooth* is a shape, whereas *well-defined* is a description of the margin.
<br />

**边界清晰** 边缘分明。判断病灶边缘是否清晰的简易方法是尝试精确描绘病灶轮廓线。若能轻松勾勒边缘则属界限清晰。"界限清晰"常被误解为"光滑"，但光滑指形态特征，而界限清晰描述的是边缘形态。

**Wolff's Law** "Bone is deposited and removed according to the stresses placed on the frame."
<br />

**沃尔夫定律**："骨骼的沉积与吸收取决于骨骼框架所承受的应力。"

## `X`

**x-rays** A type of electromagnetic radiation (a form of energy with wave-like behavior that travels at the speed of light). The short wavelength and high frequency of x-rays allows them to penetrate many objects and to be useful for medical imaging. X-rays are a form of ionizing radiation and are carcinogenic; exposure to x-rays should always be "as low as reasonably achievable" (ALARA).
<br />

**X射线** 一种电磁辐射（以波的形式传播、速度等同于光速的能量形式）。X射线因其短波长和高频特性，能穿透多种物质，在医学影像领域具有重要应用价值。X射线属于电离辐射，具有致癌性；接触X射线时应始终遵循"尽可能低"原则（ALARA）。

**x-ray film** Consists of a transparent sheet (base) coated with an emulsion containing silver halide crystals, which are sensitive to x-rays, visible light, heat, and pressure.
<br />

**X射线胶片** 由透明基底涂覆感光乳剂构成，其中含对X射线、可见光、热量及压力敏感的卤化银晶体。
